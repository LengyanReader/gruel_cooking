对话优先 · 以 MVP 接住真正在做有意义工作的人Dialogue-first · Meeting meaningful work with a small, real deliverable
不走「海投申请流水线」。关心的次序是：① 谁在做什么有意义的工作——卓越而开明的导师，以及有潜力的新人；② 若值得，先做一件 MVP 开启专业对话；③ 借工作建立相互了解，再看有无机会合作 或 受教。时间线只作跟踪字段，不作驱动理由。 Not the "broadcast-application pipeline". The order of concern is: (1) who is doing meaningful work — outstanding and open-minded advisors, plus promising newcomers; (2) if worth it, build a small MVP first to open a professional dialogue; (3) use the work to build mutual understanding, then see whether collaboration or mentorship is possible. Timeline is a tracking field, never the driver.
