为何此刻仍要读文学？原文的引子讲了一桩轶事：《华尔街日报》报道哈佛大学一门人文经典课意外走红——学生用「笨办法」研读柏拉图、但丁、蒙田、莎士比亚、斯宾诺莎、奥斯汀，定期写分析论文，选课者却踊跃。当知识触手可及、信息愈发泛滥，文学为何依然值得阅读——「从古希腊的爱欲观念到现代文学中的虚构与伦理，从女性形象的历史演变到文学与社会、政治的关系」，这份由普林斯顿大学出版社（PUP）开出的年度书单，给出的回答横跨两千五百年。 Why read literature now? The original list opens with a story: The Wall Street Journal reported on a Harvard humanities course gone unexpectedly popular — students reading Plato, Dante, Montaigne, Shakespeare, Spinoza and Austen the slow way, writing analytical essays, and lining up for it. When knowledge is one prompt away, why does literature still matter — "from the Greek idea of eros to fiction and ethics in modern literature, from the history of female figures to literature's relation with society and politics"? The year-end shelf from Princeton University Press answers across twenty-five centuries.

古典 · 爱欲CLASSICS · EROS

爱的苦甜：一篇论说（译名自拟）Eros the Bittersweet: An Essay

Eros the Bittersweet: An Essay — Anne Carson
普林斯顿 1986 年初版 · Princeton Classics 2023 · 208 页。诺奖新版精装：9780691250625（PUP 页今冠「WINNER OF THE NOBEL PRIZE」）。First published 1986 · Princeton Classics 2023 · 208 pp. The Nobel hardcover: 9780691250625 (PUP's page now carries "WINNER OF THE NOBEL PRIZE").

卡森的成名作，由博士论文长成，学术批评与散文笔法互嵌。题眼是一个词：萨福《断片 130》造出「γλυκύπικρον」——甜苦两悬——形容爱欲，卡森译作 bittersweet。柏拉图把爱欲收编为灵魂之爱，萨福偏说它是一种「欲甜未甜、欲苦未苦」的悬置；卡森以此为起点，把希腊文学与哲学里的爱情读成既悲惨又令人愉悦的矛盾体。原文点题：此书完全呼应诺奖授奖词中的「与古典传统嬉戏」。Carson's breakthrough, grown out of her dissertation, criticism and essay fused. The hinge is one word: in Fragment 130 Sappho coins «γλυκύπικρον» — sweet-bitter at once — for eros, and Carson renders it bittersweet. Where Plato folds desire into love of the soul, Sappho suspends it between arriving sweetness and arriving pain; from there Carson reads love in Greek literature and philosophy as a thing both miserable and delightful. The push's own line: the book answers the Nobel citation's "playful dialogue with the classical tradition".

一本讲「悬置」的书，恰在此刻最不悬置——诺奖把它从古典学界的暗器变成公共事件；但它的真本事是教人如何与「未完成」相处：断片不是残缺，是形式。A book about suspension that is no longer suspended — the prize made a classics-world weapon public; its real gift is teaching how to live with the unfinished: the fragment is not damage, it is form.

传记 · 歌德BIOGRAPHY · GOETHE

歌德：观念中的一生（译名自拟）Goethe: A Life in Ideas

Goethe: A Life in Ideas — Matthew Bell
普林斯顿 2025-09-09 · 776 页 · 精装 9780691153957（本次实核补年：原文未给出版年）。PUP, 9 Sep 2025 · 776 pp · hardcover 9780691153957 (year supplied by our check; the push gave none).

一部新的歌德思想传记：不做编年流水，而以观念为纲。Bell 依凭歌德书信与日记——其中大部分至今仅有德文——把思想放回 1750–1830 年代欧洲文化生活的现场。两个突出的论点：其一，歌德是最早的进化生物学家之一，某些发现早于达尔文；其二，其政治思想渗透于文学创作却长期未被正视。全书还想解决一个悖论：为何一个在宗教、性与科学上激进求新的个人主义者，政治上却趋于保守与威权？A new intellectual biography organised by ideas rather than chronology. Bell works from Goethe's letters and diaries — still mostly untrans­lated — placing the thought inside European life, 1750s–1830s. Two bold claims: Goethe as one of the earliest evolutionary biologists, ahead of Darwin on some findings; and a politics threaded through the literary work yet never properly faced. The book also takes the paradox head-on: why did a radical innovator in religion, sex and science lean conservative and authoritarian in politics?

「歌德不是两栖动物，是同一具身体站在两条岸上」——把悖论当纲领而非缺陷来写，这本传记的胆识正在于此；776 页的体量本身就是一种立场：思想不能被摘要。Not an amphibian but one body standing on both banks — writing the paradox as programme rather than flaw is this biography's nerve; 776 pages is itself a thesis: thought cannot be summarised.

学者荐语BLURB 「歌德几乎涉猎了人类知识的各个领域，这本思想传记出色地呈现了这一点……尤其值得称道的是书中对歌德的政治观点与其宗教信仰、科学研究和文学作品之间关系的细致入微的分析。」——Avi Lifschitz，牛津大学 "Goethe ranged over nearly every field of human knowledge, and this intellectual biography presents that superbly… Especially fine is the nuanced analysis of how his politics relate to his religion, science and literary work." — Avi Lifschitz, University of Oxford 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

伍尔夫 · 新发现WOOLF · NEWLY FOUND

维奥莱特的一生：三个早期故事（译名自拟）The Life of Violet: Three Early Stories

The Life of Violet: Three Early Stories — Virginia Woolf, ed. Urmila Seshagiri
普林斯顿 2025-10-07 · 精装 9780691263137（本次实核补年）。PUP, 7 Oct 2025 · hardcover 9780691263137 (year supplied by our check).

1907 年，距首部长篇还有八年，二十五岁的伍尔夫写下三篇彼此联环的喜剧故事：一位以友人 Mary Violet Dickinson 为原型的女巨人的冒险。直到 2022 年，伍尔夫研究专家 Seshagiri 才找到这些故事的最终修订打字稿——它们不是草稿，而是一部完成了的仿传体作品，伍尔夫第一次完整成形的文学实验；《奥兰多》的雏形与《一间自己的房间》里关于女性教育、婚姻与文学史的思考，都已在其中显形。书附前言、后记、注释与照片。In 1907, eight years before her first novel, the twenty-five-year-old Woolf wrote three linked comic stories about a giantess modelled on her friend Mary Violet Dickinson. Only in 2022 did the scholar Urmila Seshagiri find the final revised typescript — not drafts but a finished faux-biography, Woolf's first fully formed experiment, with Orlando and the arguments of A Room of One's Own already visible. Foreword, afterword, notes and photographs included.

这是「档案在说话」的一类书：不是遗作炒冷饭，而是一部完成态的打字稿改写了伍尔夫年表——二十五岁的她已在演练日后推翻婚姻叙事的全部手法。A book in which the archive speaks: not reheated leftovers but a finished typescript that redraws Woolf's chronology — at twenty-five she was already rehearsing everything that would overturn the marriage plot.

学者荐语BLURB 「这些故事妙趣横生，令人捧腹……也是伍尔夫早期在虚构与传记融合方面的深刻尝试，这种融合后来孕育了《奥兰多》……伍尔夫小说爱好者必读之作。」——Jessica Berman，A Companion to Virginia Woolf 编者 "Wonderful, hilarious stories… also a profound early experiment in fusing fiction and biography — the fusion that would later bear Orlando… Essential for Woolf readers." — Jessica Berman, editor of A Companion to Virginia Woolf 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

童话 · 初版复原FAIRY TALES · FIRST EDITION

格林童话初版全集（译名自拟）The Original Folk and Fairy Tales of the Brothers Grimm

The Original Folk and Fairy Tales of the Brothers Grimm: The Complete First Edition — trans. Jack Zipes, illus. Andrea Dezsö
普林斯顿 2014 · 精装 9780691160597 · 156 篇（1812/1815 两卷初版全译）。PUP, 2014 · hardcover 9780691160597 · 156 tales (the complete 1812/1815 first edition in English).

1812 与 1815 年，格林兄弟出版《儿童与家庭故事集》两卷——他们不会想到《莴苣姑娘》《汉塞尔与格蕾特》《灰姑娘》会成为世界童话。此后四十年间，兄弟俩亲手修订六版，初版里的大部分故事反被遗忘。Zipes 的译本第一次把初版 156 篇完整搬进英文：保留了简练的描写与生猛的叙事，附原序原注与 Dezsö 的插图。今日读它，读到的其实是「洁净化之前」的民间故事——怀孕的莴苣、教孩子宰杀的寓言都在。The two volumes of 1812 and 1815 — the Grimms could not have guessed Rapunzel, Hansel and Gretel and Cinderella would become the world's bedtime stories. Over the following forty years they revised through six editions, and most of the first-edition tales fell out of memory. Zipes's translation is the first complete English rendering of all 156: lean, ferocious, with the brothers' own prefaces and notes and Dezsö's illustrations. What you read is the folk tale before sanitation — a pregnant Rapunzel, a parable of children playing at slaughter.

初版格林不是「黑暗版周边」，而是方法论：童话的净化史就是一部教育史——想知道一个社会怕什么，去看它给孩子的故事里删掉了什么。The first-edition Grimm is not a "dark edition" gimmick but a method: the sanitising of fairy tales is itself a history of education — to know what a society fears, read what it deletes for its children.

学者荐语BLURB 「这本从未以英文出版过的格林兄弟童话第一版，展现了这些两百多年来一直被当作睡前故事讲述的未经粉饰的版本……展现了这些家喻户晓的故事截然不同的一面，以及一些令人毛骨悚然的新内容。」——Alison Flood，《卫报》 "The first edition, never before published in English, shows the unvarnished versions of tales told as bedtime reading for two hundred years… a completely different side, and some chilling new material." — Alison Flood, The Guardian 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

原型 · 深度心理学ARCHETYPE · DEPTH PSYCHOLOGY

大母神：原型的分析（译名自拟）The Great Mother: An Analysis of the Archetype

The Great Mother: An Analysis of the Archetype — Erich Neumann, trans. Ralph Manheim (Princeton–Bollingen)
德文初版 1955 · Princeton–Bollingen 系列英译（Manheim）· 新版平装 2015（9780691166070）· 新版精装（Liebscher 前言，9780691279046）。German original 1955 · Princeton–Bollingen English translation (Manheim) · new paperback 2015 (9780691166070) · new hardcover (foreword by Liebscher, 9780691279046).

荣格学派的里程碑之作。Neumann 广泛借用仪式、神话、艺术与梦境记录，考察「大母神」原型自史前以来的诸种显形：女神、怪物、门户、立柱、树木、月亮、太阳、容器，以及从蛇到鸟的动物。他从中间读出一种普遍的人类经验——母性同时是滋养庇护与令人恐惧的两面，其根在于意识（儿童为象征）与无意识及未知世界（大母神为象征）之间的辩证。A landmark of the Jungian school. Neumann draws on ritual, myth, art, dreams and fantasies to trace the Great Mother's manifestations since prehistory: goddess, monster, gateway, pillar, tree, moon, sun, vessel, and animals from snake to bird. Beneath them he reads a general human experience — motherhood as both nurturing and terrifying, rooted in the dialectic between growing consciousness (the child) and the unconscious, the unknown (the Great Mother).

读它要带双重心境：作为荣格传统的殿军之作，其体系自成一国；作为 1955 年的文本，其「女性性原则」的表述带着那个时代的指纹——两种读法都成立，且互为校勘。Read it doubly: as the crowning system of the Jungian line it is a country of its own; as a 1955 text its "feminine principle" bears that era's fingerprints — both readings stand, and each emends the other.

学者荐语BLURB 「Neumann 的创造性直觉使他能够从这些历史记录中解读出深刻的内容和意义，为人类心理史提供了一束光……引人入胜，发人深省。」——《分析心理学期刊》 "Neumann's creative intuition enables him to read deep content and meaning out of these historical records, shedding light on the history of the human psyche… fascinating and thought-provoking." — Journal of Analytical Psychology 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

性别史 · 四千年GENDER HISTORY · 4000 YEARS

怪物恶女：一部「可怕女性」的历史（译名自拟）Monstrous Bitch: A History of Terrifying Women

Monstrous Bitch: A History of Terrifying Women — Eleanor Johnson
普林斯顿 2026-10-06 · 344 页 · 精装 9780691291666（本次实核补年）。PUP, 6 Oct 2026 · 344 pp · hardcover 9780691291666 (year supplied by our check).

一部「怪物恶女」（monstrous bitch）的四千年形象文化史。起点是古代美索不达米亚的女恶魔拉玛什图（Lamashtu）——杀婴、纵欲、言语放肆；在希腊她化身为美狄亚（Medea）、拉弥亚（Lamia）与复仇女神；中世纪与近代早期她是淫荡杀婴的女巫；浪漫主义与维多利亚时代成了女吸血鬼；精神分析兴起后又被塑造成精神病患者。最后 Johnson 转向当代电影：《变种女狼》《詹妮弗的肉体》《女巫》如何把这一形象反转成英雄主义与反抗。贯穿的判断：形象在变，「对强大女性的恐惧与妖魔化」始终如一。A four-thousand-year cultural history of the "monstrous bitch". It begins with the Mesopotamian demon Lamashtu — killer of infants, insatiable, loud-mouthed; in Greece she is Medea, Lamia and the Erinyes; in the medieval and early modern world, the wanton child-killing witch; in the Romantic and Victorian age, the vampire; under psychoanalysis, the hysteric. Johnson then turns to contemporary film — Ginger Snaps, Jennifer's Body, The Witch — where the figure flips into heroism and resistance. The through-line: the image changes, the fear of powerful women does not.

这份长时段档案最刺人的地方在结尾：当代的「反转」不是治愈而是同一恐惧的第二次利用——把妖魔收编为消费品，比烧死她更省事。The sting is in the ending: the contemporary "reversal" is not a cure but the fear's second life — recasting the monster as merchandise costs less than burning her.

学者荐语BLURB 「本书对四千多年来女性被妖魔化的这种似曾相识的模式进行了细致入微的研究和精辟的论证。」——Tracy Fernandez Rysavy，《洛杉矶书评》 "A meticulous and penetrating study of the deja-vu pattern of women's demonisation across four thousand years." — Tracy Fernandez Rysavy, Los Angeles Review of Books 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

批评理论 · 西马CRITICISM · WESTERN MARXISM

马克思主义与形式：二十世纪辩证文学理论（沿通行译名）Marxism and Form: 20th-Century Dialectical Theories of Literature

Marxism and Form: 20th-Century Dialectical Theories of Literature — Fredric Jameson
普林斯顿 1971 年初版 · 平装 9780691013114。作者 Fredric Jameson（1934–2024），杜克大学 Knut Schmidt Nielsen 杰出教授（比较文学）。PUP, first published 1971 · paperback 9780691013114. Fredric Jameson (1934–2024), Knut Schmidt Nielsen Distinguished Professor of Comparative Literature, Duke University.

西方马克思主义文艺批评的奠基性目录：逐一细读阿多诺、本雅明、马尔库塞、布洛赫、卢卡奇、萨特，梳理二十世纪辩证法的诸条脉络，并提出全书的真问题——艺术形式与其历史条件之间如何建立联系：文化作品怎样借审美形式把时代经验转写成形。此书为 Jameson 此后全部方法（含《政治无意识》）立了地基，也把黑格尔-马克思主义的辩证批评第一次系统带进英语世界。The founding survey of Western Marxist literary criticism: close chapters on Adorno, Benjamin, Marcuse, Bloch, Lukács and Sartre, the currents of twentieth-century dialectics, and the real question — how does artistic form connect to its historical situation: how do cultural works transcribe the experience of an age into form? The book laid the ground for everything Jameson later built (including The Political Unconscious) and carried Hegelian-Marxist dialectical criticism into English at scale.

重读它的 2026 年理由恰在「AI 写作」：如果形式真能转写历史经验，那么需要检验的正是生成文本有没有「经验」可供转写——这本书是一台针对当代输出的检验仪。The 2026 reason to reread it is AI writing: if form really transcribes historical experience, then what must be tested is whether generated text has any experience to transcribe — this book is a testing instrument aimed at contemporary output.

学者荐语BLURB 「Fredric Jameson 不仅是一位思想巨匠，更是当代思想界最后一位真正的天才。他是西方马克思主义的巅峰代表……可以说，他是最后一位文艺复兴式的人物。」——齐泽克（Slavoj Žižek） "Fredric Jameson was not only a giant of thought but the last true genius of our intellectual age. He is the peak of Western Marxism… the last Renaissance figure." — Slavoj Žižek 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push

Thinking Through Shakespeare — David Womersley
普林斯顿 2026-03-10 · 精装 9780691154107（本次实核补年）。作者为牛津大学 Thomas Warton 英国文学教授。PUP, 10 Mar 2026 · hardcover 9780691154107 (year supplied by our check). The author is Thomas Warton Professor of English Literature, Oxford.

约翰逊博士的老命题——莎士比亚「是为读者举起一面忠实映照世态与人性的镜子的诗人」——至二十世纪末遭遇普遍质疑。本书作出回应，但不走老路：既不主张人性普遍恒定，也不封圣作者，而是换一个提法——莎士比亚的强大在于他的戏可用于审视那些几个世纪以来反复困扰社会的根本难题。全书锁定四题：个人身份、文明与野蛮的界限、政治权力与宗教权威的关系、目的与手段的张力，以《奥赛罗》《哈姆雷特》《麦克白》《李尔王》为解剖台。Dr Johnson's old claim — Shakespeare "holds a mirror to nature" — was under general suspicion by the century's end. This book answers, but not by nostalgia: neither universal human nature nor a canonised author, rather a re-pitch — the plays matter because they can be used to examine the problems that keep troubling societies. Four of them: personal identity; the line between civilisation and barbarism; political power and religious authority; ends and means — dissected through Othello, Hamlet, Macbeth and Lear.

「镜喻」之争的真问题是镜子朝哪儿放：不是照见永恒人性，而是每个时代把脸凑近时看见自己——本书把这句老话从颂词改写成工作指令。The mirror debate was always about where the mirror faces: not reflecting eternal nature but showing each age its own face as it leans in — the book turns the old simile from tribute into an instruction.

学者荐语BLURB 「Womersley 以令人振奋的潇洒气度，跳出了学术专业窠臼；他笔下的莎士比亚是一位宏大辩论的组织者，一位对立与矛盾的驾驭大师，他阐述了众多相互竞争的观点，却又没有哪一种观点能够完全概括他的作品。」——Thomas Keymer，多伦多大学 "With exhilarating panache Womersley steps outside the academic groove; his Shakespeare is an organiser of grand debates, a master of antinomies, stating competing views none of which exhausts the work." — Thomas Keymer, University of Toronto 转引自原文推送as quoted in the original push
