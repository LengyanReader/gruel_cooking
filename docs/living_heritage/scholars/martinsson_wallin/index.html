Helene Martinsson-Wallin

Uppsala University, Campus Gotland

Archaeology; landscape; historical ecology; sustainability

Professor of Archaeology, Uppsala University, Campus Gotland (30+ years on Gotland). Contributes deep time, archaeology, landscape, historical ecology, sustainability, fisheries, tourism, and long-term human-environment relations. Key works include Martinsson-Wallin 1994 (PhD on Easter Island ahu) and Poort et al. 2021 (Sustainability 13(11): 6302) on Gotland-Rapa Nui comparative cultural tourism. Evidence: Uppsala University staff records [A].

https://www.uu.se/en/contact-and-organisation/staff?query=N12-2422

Taste

Islands as living laboratories — comparing Gotland with Rapa Nui and the Baltic littoral; a taste for deep time that meets the present in sustainability questions.

Quality

Steadfast longitudinal grounding (decades on Gotland), methodical integration of archaeology, historical ecology, tourism and fisheries; evidence-led comparative practice.

Spirit

Respect for deep time and for the people who live with it now; patience — the willingness to let one place teach you for decades.

Character

Calm, local-first, collaborative and pragmatic; a bridge-builder between Swedish heritage management, island communities and South Pacific scholarship.

Concerns

Island sustainability; tourism authenticity and belonging; coastal communities and fisheries; long-term human-environment feedback; living heritage on Gotland.

Philosophy

Historical ecology framing: present problems read through centuries of human–environment coupling; each island carries its own logic — comparison respects difference.
