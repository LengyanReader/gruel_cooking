Ceramics and Society: A Technological Approach to Archaeological Assemblages

[A]

Valentine Roux (with M.-A. Courty) · book · 2019

The summa of the French chaîne opératoire school: decoding ceramic assemblages as coalitions of technical choices whose boundaries are social boundaries.
