熬粥之法The Art of Porridge

运行原则Operating Principles
1

从可感处起Start with the observable

一切追问始于一个可见的现象或一个具体的问题。Every inquiry begins with a visible phenomenon or a concrete question.

2

化至最简Reduce to the minimal

用第一性原理找到能解释现象的最小模型。Find the smallest model that explains the phenomenon using first principles.

3

反问解释本身Question the explanation itself

这个原理为何成立？预设了什么？在哪里失效？Why does this principle hold? What does it assume? Where does it fail?

4

追溯谱系Trace the genealogy

每个想法都有来历。记录影响的链条。Every idea has an origin. Record the chain of influence.

5

跨界授粉Cross-pollinate

经济学滋养气候学，气候学滋养天文学。让连接活着。Economics nourishes climate science, which nourishes astronomy. Keep the connections alive.

6

以最朴素的食材The simplest ingredients

复杂性从简单规则的交互中涌现。相信那碗粥。Complexity emerges from the interaction of simple rules. Trust the porridge.

7

来源先于叙述Sources before narrative

引用一手文献，核实来源，标注可靠度。不可验证的链接不如没有。Cite primary sources, verify origins, mark reliability. An unverifiable link is worse than none.

舟洲粥Zhōu Zhōu Zhōu ，三字同音 zhōu。 , three characters, one sound: zhōu.

舟——行远探索的渡具；洲——万域知识的栖所；粥——最朴素的理解之味。 Zhōu (boat) — vessel for distant exploration; zhōu (continent) — dwelling of knowledge across domains; zhōu (porridge) — the taste of understanding in its simplest form.

项目名是 deliberate 的：粥是最简单的食物，用最根本的食材熬成。我们追寻的是能解释复杂现象的最简内核，并追问：这个内核本身为何成立？它的边界在哪里？ The name is deliberate: porridge is the simplest food, made from the most fundamental ingredients. We seek the minimal kernel that explains complex phenomena, and then ask — why does this kernel hold? Where is its boundary?
