「月旦」者何？汉末许劭与郭泰每月初一品评乡党人物、臧否文章，谓之月旦评。自兹以降，「月旦」遂为定期品评之雅名。今取此义：每月一梳，梳读物、梳研究、梳影艺、梳舞台、梳声音——只梳当月最值得谈的作品，且每一条都要回得去源头。 Why "Month's Verdict" (月旦)? At the end of the Han dynasty, the critics Xu Shao and Guo Tai appraised their age's persons and writings on the first of every month — an assessment known as the Yuèdàn Píng. We revive the name: one monthly combing of reading, research, screen and stage, sound and space — only the works worth talking about, and every line traceable back to its source. ——《后汉书·许劭传》：「月旦辄更，汝南号曰月旦评」— Hou Han shu, biography of Xu Shao: "changed each first-of-month; all Runan called it the Month's Verdict"

凡·艾克 · 伦敦VAN EYCK · LONDON

国家美术馆把九幅凡·艾克聚于一室：11·21 开展，跨冬至来年四月The National Gallery gathers nine Van Eycks in one room: opens 21 Nov, through winter to April

伦敦国家美术馆的扬·凡·艾克（Jan van Eyck）专题展于 11 月 21 日开展，展至 2027 年 4 月 11 日，将九幅真迹同室并置——对一位存世作品本已稀少的十五世纪尼德兰大师而言，这几乎是「一生一次」的策展。凡·艾克的油彩层叠把光关进颜料里，其细节之密，须以放大镜读，故聚九作于一处的意义，不只是数量，而是让人得以横向比对同一双手在不同委托下的取舍。展期横跨整个冬天，本号只记开幕档期，具体到场次与借展名录以官网为准。The National Gallery's Jan van Eyck show opens 21 November and runs to 11 April 2027, seating nine autograph works in one room — for a fifteenth-century Netherlandish master whose surviving oeuvre is scant, close to a once-in-a-lifetime piece of curation. Van Eyck's glazed oils trap light inside the paint, and the density of his detail asks for a magnifying glass; the point of nine at once is not the count but the chance to read, side by side, what the same hand conceded under different commissions. The run spans the whole winter; this issue logs only the opening, with hours and the loan list per the gallery's own page.

把真迹聚齐是一种「不可复制的公共财」：论文随时能读，凡·艾克的手却几年不能重聚一次。美术馆的价值，恰在这类限时、限地、限次方可成立的经验。Bringing autograph works together is an unreplicable public good: papers can be read any day, but Van Eyck's hand cannot be reassembled more than once in years. A museum's worth lives exactly in experiences that are possible only under a limit of time, place and number.
