「月旦」者何？汉末许劭与郭泰每月初一品评乡党人物、臧否文章，谓之月旦评。自兹以降，「月旦」遂为定期品评之雅名。今取此义：每月一梳，梳读物、梳研究、梳影艺、梳舞台、梳声音——只梳当月最值得谈的作品，且每一条都要回得去源头。 Why "Month's Verdict" (月旦)? At the end of the Han dynasty, the critics Xu Shao and Guo Tai appraised their age's persons and writings on the first of every month — an assessment known as the Yuèdàn Píng. We revive the name: one monthly combing of reading, research, screen and stage, sound and space — only the works worth talking about, and every line traceable back to its source. ——《后汉书·许劭传》：「月旦辄更，汝南号曰月旦评」— Hou Han shu, biography of Xu Shao: "changed each first-of-month; all Runan called it the Month's Verdict"

本月的文学气候由三股气团决定：诺奖把文学奖给了古典学者兼诗人；布克把六本书送进十一月的长桌；而中文小说第一次登上了国际布克的领奖台——它其实是今年五月摘的果，十月仍是我们谈中文写作出海时绕不开的名字。Three air-masses set this month's literary weather: the Nobel went to a classicist-poet; the Booker fed six novels to a November long table; and a Chinese-language novel stood on the International Booker podium — picked in May, yet unavoidable this October whenever Chinese writing's voyage outward is discussed.

诺贝 尔文学奖NOBEL LITERATURE

安妮·卡森（Anne Carson）——授奖之年的中文回潮Anne Carson — and the Chinese re-tide of her award year

10 月 8 日，瑞典学院将 2026 年诺贝尔文学奖授予 76 岁的加拿大诗人、古典学家、翻译家安妮·卡森。她毕生在做一件极罕见的事：以当代诗的自觉去译两千六百年前的萨福残卷，又以西学的手感写《红的自传》。中文世界早有伏笔——《红的自传·丈夫之美》《夜》、译著《若非，冬季：萨福断章》（黄茜译，商务印书馆）等已陆续引进，获奖当日各大中文媒体连夜整理卡森书单。评者谓其「让断裂处显形」——这正是本仓《语言叠织》奉为圭臬的那类写作：翻译不是透明玻璃，是叠放的镜片。On 8 October the Swedish Academy awarded the 2026 Nobel to Anne Carson, 76 — Canadian poet, classicist, translator. Her life's rare work: rendering Sappho's fragments with a contemporary poet's self-awareness, while writing Autobiography of Red with a classicist's hands. Chinese shelves were already laid — Autobiography of Red · The Beauty of the Husband, If No, Winter (trans. Huang Qian, Commercial Press), Night — and the award night saw every Chinese book desk rush out a Carson list. Her praise: "letting the fractures show" — precisely the writing the Language Stacking module reveres: translation as stacked lenses, not clear glass.

视角：今年诺贝尔全赏「实验室里的手艺」Lens: an Nobel season rewarding lab craft

十月科学气候的最大特征：四个诺贝尔都颁给了可被复制进生命与物质本身的技艺——用光控制神经、用冰立方捕获幽灵粒子、用手性分子自我复制、用微观激励重建经济学地基。科学的「可介入性」因此本月史上最高。The October scientific weather: every Nobel went to a craft that can be copied into living matter or physical substance itself — light steering neurons, ice capturing ghost particles, chirality self-replicating, incentives rebuilding economics from its micro-foundations. Science's "intervene-ability" has rarely read higher.

光遗传学：Deisseroth · Hegemann · Nagel（10 月 5 日）Optogenetics: Deisseroth · Hegemann · Nagel (5 Oct)

Karl Deisseroth（Stanford / 美）· Peter Hegemann · Georg Nagel（德）

Karl Deisseroth (Stanford, US) · Peter Hegemann · Georg Nagel (Germany)

把光敏蛋白装进神经元，用一束光给大脑「按开关」。这是沉思科学与 F3 幸福科学共用的那把新钥匙——当第一人称的禅修报告与第三人称的光控神经记录同桌，「修行的神经机制」才从隐喻变为可实验的命题。Deisseroth 接获电话时自陈处于「梦一般的状态」（dream-like state）——获奖名单本身即是科学史的当下时间戳。Light-sensitive proteins installed in neurons; one beam becomes a switch for the brain. This is the very key contemplative science and wellbeing science (axes F3/F5 of our frontier scan) have been waiting for — only when first-person meditation reports and third-person optically controlled recordings share a table can "the neural mechanics of practice" leave metaphor for experiment. Co-laureate Karl Deisseroth told Stanford media he was in a "dream-like state" when the call came — a living timestamp on the science-historical record.

诺贝 尔化学NOBEL CHEMISTRY

Kagan × 佐井敬三：不对称自催化——分子层面的「镜像选择」Henri B. Kagan × Kenso Soai: asymmetric autocatalysis — molecular mirror-choice

「不对称自催化」意味着一个手性分子能诱导自己成百万倍地复制同一种手性。这是化学为「生命为何全选左手氨基酸」递交的候选答案之一——一个自放大、可追溯、关乎生命起源的手性事件。授奖本身即是超学科信号：化学奖第一次这样正面奖励「从分子通向起源」的叙事。Asymmetric autocatalysis: one chiral molecule teaching itself to replicate its own handedness a millionfold. A candidate answer to why life chose left-handed amino acids exclusively — a self-amplifying, traceable, origin-adjacent event in chirality. The award itself is a transdisciplinary signal: chemistry honouring, this squarely, a narrative that runs from molecule to origin.

The Nobels reward craft
