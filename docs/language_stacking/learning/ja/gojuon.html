五十音 ＝ 清音 46 音（5 段 × 9 行 + 撥音 ん）。「行」以辅音命名，「段」以元音命名（a i u e o）——矩阵本身就是语音的坐标系。

读音要点

  • し shi ・ ち chi ・ つ tsu ・ ふ fu 保留古音拼写，与「五十音」的现代发音不完全对应，须单独记忆。
  • を 与 お 同音，仅作助词（宾格标记）；ん 为独立音节鼻音，可自成音节（如 しん）。
  • 浊音（が ざ だ ば）、半浊音（ぱ）、拗音（きゃ きゅ きょ…）、长音・促音・拨音皆由清音派生。
  • や行・わ行的空位（や行のい・え，わ行のいうえ）是现代废弃的历史音位（古时曾有 ゐ・ゑ）。

The gojūon is the 46 pure sounds of Japanese (5 vowel-columns × 9 consonant-rows + the nasal ん). Rows are named by their consonant, columns by their vowel (a i u e o) — the matrix itself is a coordinate system of speech.

Reading Notes

  • shi・chi・tsu・fu keep archaic spellings; they deviate from the modern sound grid and need their own hooks.
  • を is pronounced お and is only a particle (object marker); ん is a syllabic nasal that can stand alone (e.g. しん).
  • Voiced (ga za da ba), semi-voiced (pa), contracted (kya kyu kyo…), long, geminate and nasal sounds all derive from the pure 46.
  • The empty slots in the ya- and wa-rows (yi/ye, wi/wu/we) are obsolete historical positions (ゐ・ゑ once existed).

记忆建议 / Memory Tips

  • 按行抄写 + 按段朗读交叉进行：先「あ・い・う・え・お」，再整行「か・き・く・け・こ」。
  • 用每个音的经典词做锚（雨・命・歌・月・水・雪・和……），词里去听音比孤立背形更稳。
  • 平假名与片假名成对记忆（あ↔ア、い↔イ），不要分两次学。
  • Alternate copying by row and reading by column: start with a i u e o, then whole rows (ka ki ku ke ko).
  • Anchor each sound to a classic word (rain・life・song・moon・water・snow・harmony…) — hearing the sound inside a word beats memorizing a shape in isolation.
  • Learn hiragana and katakana in pairs (あ↔ア、い↔イ), never as two separate passes.
