反生活counterlife

archetypeliteraturecluster → mirror-double
定义def未曾活过的那种生活；人如此，国家亦然——「国家的反生活」是本项目的核心观察工具。The life you did not live; just as individuals have counterlives, so do states — the project's core lens.
典例epitome Emily St. John Mandel《Exit Party》(2026) —— A/B 两面皆国家的反生活；Philip Roth《The Plot Against America》(2004) 反事实极权美国Mandel, Exit Party (2026) — both A/B sides are national counterlives; Roth, The Plot Against America (2004), a counterfactual totalitarian America

双身与镜像mirror-double

archetypetheory / literaturecluster → mirror-double
定义def人物、国家或文本的镜像分裂与互认；以重复句、对应人物缝合两面——超越解谜，读作「同一焦虑的两种结局」。Mirror-selves sutured by repeated sentences; read as twin outcomes of one anxiety, not a puzzle.
典例epitome Exit Party 的 Ari/Ibari 与双卡里姆；China Miéville《The City & The City》(2009)；Naomi Klein《Doppelganger》(2023)Exit Party's Ari/Ibari and the two Karims; China Miéville, The City & The City (2009); Naomi Klein, Doppelganger (2023)

余波叙事aftermath-narrative

archetypeliteraturecluster → aftermath
定义def聚焦灾难**之后**而非灾难本身的叙事——余波重于现场，希望与照护藏在废墟里。Narrative of the aftermath, not the disaster — what follows matters more than the event.
典例epitome Mandel《Station Eleven》(2014) 与《Exit Party》；《The Leftovers》后启示录剧集Mandel, Station Eleven (2014) and Exit Party; The Leftovers, the post-apocalyptic series

图像化暴力aestheticized-violence

archetypetheory / literaturecluster → aestheticized-violence
定义def暴力被「排演」成美学构图——凶手以文学/影像为世界观，受害者被酿成偶像化身；罪案由此成为谜面本身。Violence staged as an aesthetic code — the killer's lens is literature and film, the victim composed into an icon.

互文宝匣intertext-vault

traditiontheorycluster → language-meaning
定义def以一首诗 / 一幅画 / 一部电影为全书的互文枢纽，标题即答案的一半——文本在文本之网中获得意义。One quoted artifact (a poem, a painting, a film) as the intertextual hinge; meaning lives in the network of texts.
典例epitome Vargas 取 Nerval《El Desdichado》(1853)「一束唯一的光」为书名与谜面；Mandel《Swan Dive》画作串起全篇Vargas takes Nerval's El Desdichado (1853), "one unique light", as title and riddle; Mandel's painting Swan Dive threads the whole book

记忆与复诵memory-repetition

conceptphilosophy / literaturecluster → language-meaning
定义def人物的反复重述、行走、绕圈——记忆不被记住，而被迫上演；复诵既是创伤机制，也是解谜钥匙。Characters who repeat, walk, circle; memory is performed, not recalled — repetition is both trauma and key.
典例epitome Vargas Adamsberg 系列；普鲁斯特式非自愿记忆传统Vargas's Adamsberg cycle; the Proustian tradition of involuntary memory
