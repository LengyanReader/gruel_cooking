熬粥之法 · 本域铁律Serving rules · this domain

1

来源先于叙述Sources before narrative

不可验证的链接不如没有。An unverifiable link is worse than none.

2

立场必标Reliability always

✓ / ◐ / ○ / ✗，四等分明，无标注不入档。✓ verified / ◐ claim / ○ echo / ✗ disputed — nothing unlabelled.

3

追溯谱系Trace the genealogy

每个想法都有来历，记录影响之链，包括谁误读了它。Every idea has an origin — record the chain, including who misquoted it.

4

开阔胜过完备Open beats complete

延伸地图允许未完成，其职责是让邻近可见。The extension map may stay unfinished — its job is to make adjacency visible.

5

不同意即进步Disagreement is progress

每条笔记至少一个歧义、一个回响。One disagreement and one echo minimum per note.

6

双语默认Bilingual by default

中英并列，站点可切换，互不驱逐。Chinese and English co-primary; the site toggles, neither expunges.

7

单一来源 → 生成页Single source → pages

散文入 Markdown，数据入 JSON，页由生成器产出。Prose in Markdown, data in JSON, pages generated.

8

源出同步Source ↔ Output sync

改源即重建提交，CI 锁校验。Change source, rebuild and commit together; CI enforces the diff.
