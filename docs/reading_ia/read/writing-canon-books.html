The Elements of Style Press 美国the American 非虚构 · 英语用法与风格规则 · 原 1918nonfiction · English usage & style rules · orig. 1918 kind · nonfictionnonfiction writing, style 读过read

现代英语风格手册的原型：以极简的条目式规则规定「简明英语」，是其后几乎所有写作指南（含 Pinker 本书）的对话起点。

The prototype of the modern style manual: terse, numbered rules for plain English — the starting point that later guides (including Pinker's) argue with.

  • · 省略不必要的词Omit needless words
  • · 使用主动语态Use the active voice
  • · 以肯定形式表述Put statements in positive form
  • · 以段落为作文单位：一段一主题Make the paragraph the unit of composition: one to each topic
  • · 把相关的词放在一起Keep related words together
  • · 把句子中强调的词放在末尾Place the emphatic words of a sentence at the end

《风格的感觉》The Sense of Style The Sense of Style

史蒂芬·平克Steven Pinker · Steven Pinker
Viking（美）/ Allen Lane·Penguin（英）· 2014 · ISBN 978-0-670-02521-9Viking (US) / Allen Lane·Penguin (UK) · 2014 · ISBN 978-0-670-02521-9
Viking / Penguin 美国the American 非虚构 · 认知科学视角的写作与阅读指南 · 2014nonfiction · a thinking person's guide to writing, from cognitive science · 2014 kind · nonfictionnonfiction writing, cognitive science 读过read

把「什么是好文笔」从传统清规中解放出来，用语言学认知科学重建：散文是通向世界的窗（classic style），坏文笔多源于知识的诅咒（the curse of knowledge）。

Liberates 'good prose' from folk rules and rebuilds it on linguistics and cognitive science: prose as a window onto the world (classic style), bad writing as chiefly the curse of knowledge.

  • · 以「经典风格」写作：散文是通向世界的窗Write in the classic style: prose as a window onto the world
  • · 坏文笔的根源多为「知识的诅咒」Obscurity is mostly the curse of knowledge
  • · 句子是语义之网→短语之树→线性之串的映射A sentence maps a web of meaning onto a tree, then a string
  • · 连贯靠话题链、连接词与回指成「弧」Coherence runs in arcs of topic, connective, and reference
  • · 许多传统规则源自 folklore，应随语言演化优雅取舍Many classic rules are folklore; bend them gracefully as language changes
  • · 警惕刻意艰深：清晰的深奥优于含混的深奥Beware pretension — clear depth beats murky depth

「他们说 / 我说」They Say / I Say They Say / I Say

Gerald Graff 与 Cathy BirkensteinGerald Graff & Cathy Birkenstein · Gerald Graff & Cathy Birkenstein
W. W. Norton · 第六版（附读本）· 2024-07-05 · ISBN 978-1-324-07003-0W. W. Norton · Sixth ed. (with readings) · 2024-07-05 · ISBN 978-1-324-07003-0
W. W. Norton 美国the American 非虚构 · 学术写作「对话」模板 · 第六版nonfiction · templates for academic writing as conversation · 6th ed. kind · nonfictionnonfiction writing, rhetoric, education 读过read

把学术写作还原为一个动作：进入一场对话。先如实复述「他们说」，再以模板化的招式给出「我说」——为最抽象的「论证」提供了可复制句式。

Reduces academic writing to one act: entering a conversation. Summarize 'they say' honestly, then answer 'I say' with templated moves — turning abstract 'argument' into reproducible sentence-frames.

  • · 写作即进入对话：先「他们说」，再「我说」Writing is entering conversation: 'they say', then 'I say'
  • · 用模板句引述与概括，忠实呈现他人观点Use templates to quote and summarize others faithfully
  • · 三种回应：同意并延伸 / 反驳 / 让步但是Three responses: agree-and-add / disagree / concede-but
  • · 回答「那又怎样 / 谁在乎」赋予论证意义Answer 'so what? who cares?' to give the argument stakes
  • · 元评论（metacommentary）引导读者理解你的意图Metacommentary steers the reader through your intent
  • · 第六版新增：当「他们说」来自 AI/机器人New in 6e: when the 'they say' is an AI/bot

《做一个科学家》On Being a Scientist On Being a Scientist

美国国家科学院 · 工程院 · 医学院National Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Engineering, and Institute of Medicine · National Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Engineering, and Institute of Medicine

National Academies Press 美国the American 非虚构 · 负责任研究 conduct 指南 · 第三版 · 开放获取nonfiction · a guide to the responsible conduct of research · 3rd ed. · open access kind · nonfictionnonfiction research ethics, science 在读currently reading

科研大厦立于「信任」之上。这本书把研究者日常面对的伦理与规范（数据、署名、评审、利益冲突、不当行为、与公众沟通）写成一份可教可读的负责任研究 conduct 入门。

Science rests on trust. This guide turns the everyday ethics of research — data, authorship, peer review, conflicts, misconduct, communication with the public — into a teachable primer on the responsible conduct of research.

  • · 科研共同体建立在「信任」之上The research community rests on trust
  • · 负责任研究 conduct 是可教、可学的The responsible conduct of research is teachable
  • · 数据、署名、同行评审、利益冲突各有规范Data, authorship, peer review and conflicts each carry norms
  • · 科研不当 = 伪造 / 篡改 / 剽窃（FFP）Misconduct = fabrication, falsification, plagiarism (FFP)
  • · 科学家对公众与政策负有沟通责任Scientists owe communication to the public and policymakers

  • Tier 1 待读：Day & Gastel《How to Write and Publish a Scientific Paper》、Schimel《Writing Science》、Alley《The Craft of Scientific Writing》、Swales & Feak《Academic Writing for Graduate Students》

  • Tier 2 待读：Sword《Stylish Academic Writing》、Hayot《The Elements of Academic Style》、Swales & Feak《English in Today's Research World》、Clark《Writing Tools》
  • Tier 2 to read: Stylish Academic Writing (Sword), The Elements of Academic Style (Hayot), English in Today's Research World (Swales & Feak), Writing Tools (Clark)
  • Tier 3 待读：Germano《Getting It Published》《From Dissertation to Book》、Luey《Handbook for Academic Authors》、Kelsky《The Professor Is In》
  • Tier 3 to read: Getting It Published & From Dissertation to Book (Germano), Handbook for Academic Authors (Luey), The Professor Is In (Kelsky)
  • Tier 4 待读：Belcher、Silvia、Bolker、Goodson、Jensen（写作习惯与完成博士论文五书）
  • Tier 4 to read: Belcher, Silvia, Bolker, Goodson, Jensen (the five habit / dissertation-completion books)
  • Tier 5 待读：Eco《How to Write a Thesis》
  • Tier 5 to read: How to Write a Thesis (Umberto Eco)
  • 补充线索：写作正典可与「认知—清晰」（Pinker）及「对话—论证」（They Say）两条主轴继续向伦理/发表两端延展
  • Lead: extend the canon along its two axes — cognition/clarity (Pinker) and conversation/argument (They Say) — toward the ethics/publishing ends
