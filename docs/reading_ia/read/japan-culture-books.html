The orbit. The Special is only the nearest reach of one long river. 《分身：新日本论》所追溯的「中国镜像」的起点之一。黄遵宪以外交官与诗人的双眼睛，用通志体例记日本地理、制度、学术、风俗——把「知日」第一次写成一部可查的国志。

A starting point of the "Chinese mirror" Li Yongjing traces. Huang Zunxian, diplomat and poet, records Japan's geography, institutions and customs in gazetteer form — for the first time making "knowing Japan" a researchable national history.

拾捌 · 论日本对手X VIII · rivalZH

《日本论》A Discussion of Japan 日本论

戴季陶 Dai Jitao · 1924
政论小册 · 近代中国人「知日—对日」思想的代表文本A political tract · a key modern Chinese "know-Japan / face-Japan" text
政论痛切策论✓

以革命者的切身经验论日本的国体、武士道、宗教与对外性格。它是《分身》自陈的直接前身之一：一部既是「知日」也是「用日」的策论——认识他者从来连着认识自身。

Argues Japan's polity, bushido, religion and foreign character from a revolutionist's lived experience. Li names it a direct predecessor of 分身: a tract that is at once "knowing Japan" and "using Japan" — knowing the other always entangles knowing oneself.

拾玖 · 论日本对手X IX · rivalEN

《菊与刀》The Chrysanthemum and the Sword 文化人類学の日本研究

鲁思·本尼迪克特 Ruth Benedict · 1946
二战末期受托研究日本 · 中译多种 · 「耻感文化」范式Wartime study of Japan · many ZH eds. · the "shame-culture" paradigm
1946冷静定型✓

人类学家不赴日本而凭材料为日本「定型」：菊（审美、耻）与刀（武士、忠）的两极并置。「各安其分」的等级观，成为战后一切日本论——包括其批判者——绕不开的对手。

An anthropologist "types" Japan without ever going there: the chrysanthemum (taste, shame) and the sword (warrior, loyalty) held in polarity. Its "each in his proper place" is the rival every post-war Japan-ology — critics included — must pass through.

弐拾 · 论日本对手XX · rivalJA

《杂种文化 · 日本文化小史》Hybrid Culture / A Short History of Japanese Culture 雑種文化・日本文化の概要

加藤周一 Katō Shūichi · 「杂种文化」1956
「杂种文化」论（1956）＋日本文化通论 · 中译多种The 1956 essay + a general survey · several ZH eds.
加藤周一清醒自嘲✓

「日本文化是杂种文化。」加周一指出日本在西方与东方、传统与现代之间兼收并杂，却正因这份「不纯粹的诚实」而有其创造力的清醒自剖。是《菊与刀》式定型论之后，日本人对自身的又一记回答。

"Japanese culture is a hybrid culture." Katō shows Japan gleaning East and West, tradition and modernity — and finding creativity precisely in this honest impurity. Japan's own reply after Benedict's typing.

弐拾壹 · 文豪侧翼XXI · literaryJA

《草枕》The Three-Cornered World (Kusamakura) 草枕

夏目漱石 Natsume Sōseki · 1906
中译多种（高慧勤等）· 与特辑《浮世与病榻》同一作者的虚构面Many ZH eds. · the fiction-side of the same author as 浮世与病榻
1906超然非人情✓

「以诗人的眼睛看，以画家的眼光感。」漱石的「非人情」理想：一位画家遁入山中温泉，把人世之情隔在窗外，只求审美地观照。与《浮世与病榻》互为表里——一个入世的病、一个出世的静。

"See with a poet's eyes, feel with a painter's." Sōseki's "non-human-emotion" ideal: a painter retreats to a mountain spa and contemplates the world purely aesthetically. The inverse of 浮世与病榻 — illness engaged vs. stillness withdrawn.

弐拾贰 · 文豪侧翼XXII · literaryJA

《侏儒的话》Dwarf's Words (Jujin no Mon) 侏儒の言葉

芥川龙之介 Akutagawa Ryūnosuke · 1923–1925
中译多种（文洁若等）· 箴语 / 随笔集 · 特辑《妄想者手记》同源Many ZH eds. · aphorisms · kin to the 妄想者手记 volume
1923冷峭箴言✓

侏儒虽小，却敢刺穿巨人的脚。芥川以短峭的箴语，写文艺、人生、恋爱、幸福与「悲观」，冷到见骨，又处处是自嘲的机智。读《妄想者手记》时最好的对读文本。

A dwarf may pierce a giant's heel. Akutagawa's sharp aphorisms on art, life, love, happiness and "pessimism" — bone-cold yet wryly self-mocking. The perfect mirror-reading for 妄想者手记.

弐拾叁 · 文豪侧翼XXIII · literaryJA

《墨东逸话》Tales of the Sumida East (Bokutō Ikiva) 墨東逸話

永井荷风 Nagai Kafū · 1909
下町风俗随笔 · 与特辑《异国放浪记》同一作者的江户面Low-town sketches · the Edo-face of the same author as 放浪记
1909怀旧颓美◐

东京下町（墨田川以东）的游里、花火、艺者与旧街——荷风以「最后的江户人」之笔，为正在消逝的旧东京写挽歌。与《放浪记》的巴黎颓唐相表里：一个向外流亡，一个向内凭吊。

Kafū, "the last Edo man," writes an elegy for old Tokyo's pleasure-quarter, fireworks and geisha east of the Sumida. The inverse of Wandering Abroad: one exile outward, one mourning inward.

弐拾肆 · 文豪侧翼XXIV · literaryJA

《美丽的日本与我》Japan, The Beautiful and I 美しい日本と私

川端康成 Kawabata Yasunari · 1968 诺贝尔奖演说
讲演集（讲谈社文庫）· 英译收录于诺奖演说集 · 精确版目待核Lecture collection (Kodansha) · EN in Nobel-lecture eds. · imprint to verify
1968物哀归乡◐

诺奖演说：从「秋刀鱼过海」的古典和歌，讲到枯山水、茶道与物哀——川端把「日本之美」说成一种近乎哀伤的、对空无的亲近。全篇美学自述的收束点，也是「温柔哀感」在二十世纪的最后回响。

His Nobel lecture: from classical tanka to dry gardens, tea and mono no aware — Kawabata calls Japan's beauty an almost-sad intimacy with emptiness. The closing self-portrait, and the twentieth century's last echo of "tender grief."
