为何此刻Why now

现存最重要的早期围棋文献汇编之一：把棋论（《棋经十三篇》《棋诀》《论棋诀要杂说》）与古棋局放在一起，是「原典阅读」的入口。
One of the most important early Go anthologies: it binds treatises (the Thirteen Chapters, the Qijue, the Lun Qijue Ya Zashuo) with ancient game records — an entry to reading the sources.

背景Background

南宋翰林院棋待诏李逸民编，书名取自宋徽宗「忘忧清乐在枰棋」。2017 年浙江古籍出版社《围棋文献集成》第八册收入《忘忧清乐集》《石室仙机》《仙机武库》三种；简介称其收张拟《棋经十三篇》、刘仲甫《棋诀》、张靖《论棋诀要杂说》及诸多棋局、变式，包括「孙策诏吕范弈棋局面」等古局。
Compiled by the Southern Song imperial Go attendant Li Yimin; the title comes from Emperor Huizong's line on carefree joy at the board. Zhejiang Guji's 2017 volume 8 of the Go Literature Collection includes the Collection, Shishi Xianji and Xianji Wuku; the blurb lists the Thirteen Chapters, Liu Zhongfu's Qijue, Zhang Jing's Lun Qijue Ya Zashuo, and many game records and variations, including the Sun Ce–Lü Fan board.

推断 · The claimThe claim

李逸民编《忘忧清乐集》，要做的不是「立一家之言」，而是「存一代之文」：把棋论与古谱汇编成一部可传承的围棋经典，并以宋徽宗「忘忧清乐在枰棋」的句子，把围棋安放在审美与生活世界。

Li Yimin's aim is not a personal doctrine but preservation: to compile treatises and ancient records into a transmitted Go classic, and through Huizong's line to place Go in a world of aesthetic life.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

书名取自宋徽宗句，说明编者关心的是「忘忧」的生活情调，而非宗教修行；所收《棋经十三篇》《棋诀》《论棋诀要杂说》与孙策、晋武帝、明皇等古局，构成「论 + 谱」的双重证据；李逸民的「翰林院棋待诏」身份，说明这是宫廷制度内的文献整理，而非私人哲学写作。

The title from Huizong shows concern with carefree life rather than religious practice; the Thirteen Chapters, Qijue and Lun together with ancient records form a double evidence of treatise and record; Li's office as imperial Go attendant places the work within court documentation, not private philosophy.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

存一代之文：读《忘忧清乐集》Preserving the texts of an age

南宋棋待诏的汇编：它把棋论与古谱放在一起，以「忘忧清乐」为围棋定调。A Southern Song imperial attendant's compilation: treatises and ancient records together, tuning Go to 'carefree joy'.

《忘忧清乐集》是南宋翰林院棋待诏李逸民编的围棋文献汇编。它的书名取自宋徽宗「忘忧清乐在枰棋」，这个选择很关键：编者把围棋放在「忘忧」的审美与生活世界，而不是宗教修行体系里。

它的内容也支持这一点：《棋经十三篇》《棋诀》《论棋诀要杂说》是棋论，孙策、晋武帝、明皇等古棋局是棋谱，两者合在一起，构成「论 + 谱」的双重证据。这不是一部「思想史」专著，而是一部可传承的经典汇编。

它的价值在保存，风险也在保存：汇编不提供统一论证，读者若只读现代解释，容易把「立象比德」当成原书宗旨，而忽略它首先是棋论与棋谱的集合。读它时最好先看目录，分清「论」与「谱」，再判断哪些是原文、哪些是后人的解释。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Carefree & Innocent Pastime Collection was compiled by Li Yimin, a Southern Song imperial Go attendant. Its title, from Huizong's line on carefree joy at the board, is decisive: the compiler places Go in a world of aesthetic life, not a system of religious practice.

The contents support this. The Thirteen Chapters, the Qijue and the Lun Qijue Ya Zashuo are treatises; the Sun Ce, Jin Emperor Wu and Ming Emperor records are game records; together they form a double evidence of discourse and record. This is not a work of intellectual history but a transmitted classic.

Its value is preservation, and so is its risk: a compilation offers no unified argument, and a reader who relies on modern interpretation may mistake imaging virtue for the book's purpose while ignoring that it is first a collection of treatises and records. Begin with the contents, separate discourse from record, and then decide what is primary text and what is later interpretation.
