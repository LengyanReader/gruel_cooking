背景Background

《陰翳礼讃》1933—1934 年连载，后收入《文章読本》一系随笔。谷崎从厕所、漆器、烛光、能乐面具写到餐具与建筑，主张美不在对象本身，而在对象所处的阴翳之中。他并非单纯怀旧，而是指出：西方以照明消灭阴影，日本则以阴影保存物件的深度。中译有陈德文等多种，英译以 Thomas J. Harper（1977）与 Gregory Starr（2017）较为通行。
In Praise of Shadows was serialised in 1933–34. From toilets, lacquerware, candlelight and Noh masks to tableware and architecture, Tanizaki argues that beauty lies not in the object but in the shadow it inhabits. This is not mere nostalgia: the West abolishes shadow with lighting, Japan preserves depth through it. Chinese versions include Chen Dewen's; the usual English ones are Thomas J. Harper (1977) and Gregory Starr (2017).

第 1 篇 · 从厕所开始Piece 1 · Starting from the toilet

最不雅的场所反而最适合谈美：幽暗、木香、雨声，构成一个与自然相通的私密空间。
The least elegant place is best for beauty: gloom, wood, rain — a private room continuous with nature.

✓

第 2 篇 · 漆器与烛光Piece 2 · Lacquer and candlelight

漆器的美依赖昏暗：在电灯下它只剩黑，在烛光里它才有深度。谷崎由此展开对「照明进步」的批判。
Lacquer depends on dimness: under electric light it is merely black, in candlelight it has depth. From this he attacks the 'progress' of lighting.

✓

第 3 篇 · 建筑、能乐与餐具Piece 3 · Architecture, Noh, tableware

把阴翳从器物推到空间与表演：能乐的暗、和室的深邃、汤碗的黑红，都是同一美学。
Shadow extends from objects to space and performance: Noh's darkness, the depth of the washitsu, the black-red of soup bowls — one aesthetics.

✓

第 4 篇 · 收束：美在关系Piece 4 · Close: beauty in relation

末段回到命题：美不是属性，而是物与物之间明暗的波纹。
The close returns to the thesis: beauty is not a property but the ripple of light and dark between things.

结构Structure

文章从最不雅的场所（厕所）写起，一路升到建筑与戏剧，最后落回日常器物。谷崎的策略是「从低处进入」：先让你承认一个不好意思承认的偏好，再告诉你这偏好背后是一整套文明选择。
It begins in the least elegant place — the toilet — rises through architecture and theatre, and returns to everyday objects. Tanizaki's strategy is to enter low: first make you admit a preference you are ashamed of, then show that behind it lies a whole civilisational choice.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

二十世纪日本美学最有影响力的单篇随笔；它把「阴翳」从一个趣味词提升为文明比较的范畴，直接启发了后来的侘寂与摩灭写作。 容易被读成「怀旧散文」或「反现代化」；谷崎对女性与感官的描写也常被后来的读者批评为男性凝视。 **合成 Synthesis：** 读它不要停在「日本很美」，要抓住「美在关系」这一命题，再用它去看《摩灭之赋》如何把关系换成时间。
The most influential single essay in twentieth-century Japanese aesthetics; it lifts 'shadow' from a term of taste to a category of civilisational comparison and directly enables later writing on wabi-sabi and erosion. Easily read as nostalgic prose or anti-modernism; Tanizaki's descriptions of women and the senses are also criticised as a male gaze. **Synthesis:** Do not stop at 'Japan is beautiful'; grasp the proposition that beauty is relational, then see how Erosion swaps relation for time.

手法与亮点 · CraftCraft & highlights

从低处进入：以厕所、便器、食物等不雅对象起笔，反而更有说服力。

Entering low: toilets, chamber pots and food make the argument more persuasive, not less.

感官的具体性：不说「幽暗很美」，而说漆器在烛光下如何。

Sensory concreteness: not 'gloom is beautiful' but what lacquer does in candlelight.

文明比较：西方/日本不是本质对立，而是两种处理光的技术选择。

Civilisational comparison: West and Japan are not essences but two technical choices about light.

精读建议 · Deep readingDeep reading

读时关掉顶灯，只留一盏台灯，再读漆器与烛光一段——这是谷崎要求的阅读条件。
Read the lacquer-and-candle passage with the overhead light off and a single lamp on — the condition Tanizaki asks for.
标出所有「西洋」出现的地方，看谷崎如何把西方写成单一整体；这是全书最大的简化。
Mark every appearance of 'the West' and see how Tanizaki writes it as one whole — the book's greatest simplification.
与《摩灭之赋》对读同一件器物（如老碗）：一个看它如何反光，一个看它如何被磨薄。
Read the same object (an old bowl) in Erosion: one watches how it reflects, the other how it is worn thin.
