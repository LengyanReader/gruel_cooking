背景Background

《斑驳人类世野外指南》由 Tsing、Jennifer Deger、Alder Keleman Saxena 与 Feifei Zhou 合著，2024 年出版。它把《野性图谱》的数字材料转写为一本可携带的「野外指南」：不是教你识别鸟兽，而是教你识别「斑块」——人类活动、基础设施与非人类生命密集交织的具体地点。书的核心主张是：人类世不是全球均匀的整体，而是由无数不同步、不连续的斑块拼成。
Field Guide to the Patchy Anthropocene, co-authored by Tsing, Jennifer Deger, Alder Keleman Saxena and Feifei Zhou and published in 2024, rewrites the digital material of Feral Atlas into a portable field guide. It does not teach you to identify birds or plants but to identify patches: concrete sites where human activity, infrastructure and nonhuman life are densely intertwined. Its core claim is that the Anthropocene is not a globally even whole but a composition of countless non-synchronous, discontinuous patches.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

全书以「指南」而非「专著」的形式组织：概念条目、案例斑块、图像与阅读路线交错。它先解释「斑驳人类世」是什么，再给出识别斑块的工具（注意、追踪、比较），最后把工具放回具体地点。读者可以按顺序读，也可以像使用野外指南一样，按地点或主题跳读。
The book is organised as a guide rather than a monograph: conceptual entries, case patches, images and reading routes interlace. It first explains what the patchy Anthropocene is, then offers tools for identifying patches (noticing, tracing, comparing), and finally returns the tools to concrete sites. Readers can go in order or, like a field guide, jump by site or theme.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

2024 年出版后被视为 Tsing 多物种方法的总汇，常与《菌菇》《Feral Atlas》并读；「斑驳人类世」成为描述不均匀人类世经验的常用词。 评论也提出：指南形式可能让论证显得碎片化；「斑块」概念在跨尺度使用时需要更明确的边界。 **合成 Synthesis：** 本书的贡献是把「人类世」从全球话语变成一套可学习的注意技艺：先看见斑块，再谈整体。

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

把人类世装进口袋：读《斑驳人类世野外指南》A pocket Anthropocene

人类世不是整体，而是由不同步的斑块拼成；理解它需要一套可携带的注意技艺。The Anthropocene is not a whole but a composition of non-synchronous patches; understanding it requires a portable craft of noticing.

《斑驳人类世野外指南》是一本奇怪的书：它把「人类世」这样的大词，写成了一本可以随身携带的指南。它不教你识别鸟兽，而是教你识别「斑块」——那些人类活动、基础设施与非人类生命密集交织的具体地点。它的核心主张很简单，也很难做到：不要从「全球」开始，从你能看见的斑块开始。

这种形式不是装饰。如果人类世是不同步的、不连续的，那么一本线性的总论就会错过它的对象。指南的条目化、可跳读、可携带，正是为了让你在不同的斑块之间移动，并逐渐看见它们之间的差异与联系。

局限也来自形式：条目化可能削弱论证的连贯性，「斑块」的边界在跨尺度使用时并不清楚，案例选择也可能偏向作者熟悉的地区。它更像一套训练工具，而不是一部完整的人类世理论。

把它与《菌菇》对读，会看见 Tsing 的方法从「废墟」走向「斑块」：前者强调时间与生计，后者强调空间与注意。两者都不是答案，而是让你重新学会看世界的方式。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Field Guide to the Patchy Anthropocene is an odd book: it turns a large term, the Anthropocene, into a guide you can carry. It does not teach you to identify birds or plants but to identify patches — concrete sites where human activity, infrastructure and nonhuman life are densely intertwined. Its core claim is simple and hard to practise: do not start from the global; start from a patch you can see.

The form is not decoration. If the Anthropocene is non-synchronous and discontinuous, a linear overview will miss its object. The guide's entries, skippability and portability are meant to let you move among patches and gradually see their differences and connections.

The limits come from the form: entry-based writing can weaken coherence, the boundaries of a patch are unclear across scales, and case selection may favour regions familiar to the authors. It is more a training kit than a complete theory of the Anthropocene.

Read alongside Mushroom, it shows Tsing's method moving from ruins to patches: the former stresses time and livelihood, the latter space and attention. Neither is an answer; both retrain how you see the world.
