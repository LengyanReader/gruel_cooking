背景Background

侘＝主动安于不足、枯淡的心境；寂＝时间沉积的静默。大西克礼再析其枯、寒、简、远等三重审美构造，把常被当作装饰风格的侘寂还原为一种观照世界的方式。
Wabi is a heart resting willingly in lack; sabi the quiet sediment of time. Onishi parses its triple structure and restores wabi-sabi from décor to a way of contemplating the world.
