背景Background

《与大地共感思》是一部西班牙语文集，汇集 Escobar 在 2000 年代至 2010 年代初关于发展、领地与差异的文章与讲演。书名取自哥伦比亚加勒比沿岸的民间说法 sentipensar——「用心思考」或「用身体感受着思考」，Escobar 从奥兰多·法尔斯·博尔达（Orlando Fals Borda）那里接过这个词，把它变成一种方法论：研究者不能只做旁观的分析者，必须让自己的身体与情感进入与土地、社区的关系。全书讨论四个问题：发展的替代、领地的政治、差异的本体论、以及「认识论南方」的可能。
Sentipensar con la tierra is a Spanish-language collection of essays and talks from the 2000s and early 2010s on development, territory and difference. The title takes a popular expression from Colombia's Caribbean coast — sentipensar, to think with the heart or to feel-think — which Escobar receives via Orlando Fals Borda and turns into a method: the researcher cannot remain a detached analyst but must let body and affect enter into relation with land and community. The book addresses four questions: alternatives to development, the politics of territory, the ontology of difference, and the possibility of an 'epistemology of the South'.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

文集不是线性专著，而是围绕四个关键词反复推进的短章：先批评「发展」的单一尺度，再讨论领地如何成为生命世界的政治，然后把「差异」从文化概念提升为本体论问题，最后提出「共感思」作为一种认识论姿态。篇章之间有重复，也有修订，读者可以把它当作 Escobar 思想的「工作台」——许多后来在《多元宇宙的设计》中成形的概念，在这里还带着草稿的痕迹。
The collection is not a linear monograph but short chapters circling four keywords: a critique of development's single scale, territory as the politics of a lifeworld, difference raised from culture to ontology, and sentipensar as an epistemological stance. There is repetition and revision across chapters; readers can use it as Escobar's workbench, where many concepts later formalised in Designs for the Pluriverse still carry the marks of draft.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

在西语学界与拉丁美洲研究圈内影响很大，「共感思」成为去殖民方法论的常用词；它也让英语读者看到 Escobar 思想更口语、更介入的一面。 作为文集，结构松散、篇章重复；「共感思」的诗性定义也常被批评为难以操作，容易变成姿态而非方法。 **合成 Synthesis：** 本书的价值不在体系，而在姿态：它提醒研究者，关于土地与差异的知识不能只从外部描述，必须在关系中被共同生产。
It has had wide influence in Spanish-language scholarship and Latin American studies; sentipensar became a common term in decolonial methodology, and the book shows English readers a more spoken, more engaged Escobar. As a collection it is loosely structured and repetitive, and the poetic definition of sentipensar is often criticised as hard to operationalise, risking stance over method. **Synthesis:** Its value lies not in system but in stance: it reminds researchers that knowledge about land and difference cannot be described only from outside but must be co-produced in relation.

推断 · The claimThe claim

Escobar 要证明：关于发展、领地与差异的知识不能只从外部描述，必须在身体、情感与土地的关系中被共同生产；「共感思」因此不是修辞，而是认识论立场。

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

用心思考土地：读《与大地共感思》Thinking the land with the heart

「共感思」不是诗意的装饰，而是 Escobar 全部工作的认识论枢纽。Sentipensar is not poetic decoration but the epistemological hinge of Escobar's whole project.

如果要找一本能解释 Escobar 为什么这样写作的书，《与大地共感思》比《遭遇发展》更合适。它不是一部体系之作，而是一部工作台之书：讲演、短文、序言与回应被放在一起，反复打磨几个词——发展、领地、差异、共感思。

「共感思」（sentipensar）是全书的核心。这个词来自哥伦比亚加勒比沿岸的民间说法，意思是「用心思考」或「带着感受去思考」。Escobar 经 Fals Borda 接过它，用它反对两种知识姿态：一种是抽离的、只做描述的社会科学；另一种是只讲立场的、拒绝分析的宣言。他要的是一种既不放弃分析、也不假装中立的写法。

这本书的力量在于姿态，局限也在于姿态。文集的结构松散，篇章之间重复不少；「共感思」的定义诗意而难以操作，读者很难从中学到一套可复制的步骤。它更像一次邀请：请你在读土地、读领地、读差异时，把自己的身体与情感也算进知识里。

把它当作《多元宇宙的设计》的前传读，会看见许多后来被系统化的概念还带着草稿的痕迹。这种草稿感不是缺点，而是它最诚实的地方。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

If you want a book that explains why Escobar writes the way he does, Sentipensar con la tierra is a better guide than Encountering Development. It is not a systematic work but a workbench book: talks, short essays, prefaces and responses gathered to polish a few words — development, territory, difference, sentipensar.

Sentipensar is the core. The term comes from Colombia's Caribbean coast and means to think with the heart, or to think while feeling. Escobar receives it through Fals Borda and uses it against two postures: detached social science that only describes, and manifesto-style writing that refuses analysis. What he wants is a writing that neither abandons analysis nor pretends to neutrality.

The book's strength is its stance, and so is its limit. The collection is loosely structured and repetitive; the definition of sentipensar is poetic and hard to operationalise, and readers will not extract a replicable procedure. It is closer to an invitation: when reading land, territory and difference, count your body and affect as part of the knowledge.

Read as a prequel to Designs for the Pluriverse, it shows many later systematised concepts still carrying draft marks. That draft quality is not a defect but the most honest thing about it.
