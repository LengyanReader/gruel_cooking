背景Background

《钻石女王的领地》改写自 Tsing 在印尼南加里曼丹的博士田野。1970—80 年代，梅拉瑙人（Meratus）生活在国家林业、伊斯兰化、伐木业与新秩序开发的交叠处；Tsing 追踪他们如何在被国家视为「偏远」的地方继续组织婚姻、迁徙、狩猎与仪式。书名取自当地关于「钻石女王」的传说：一位从远方来的女王留下秩序又离开，留下的是需要不断重述的关系。Tsing 由此提出：边缘不是中心的对立面，而是国家、资本与地方生活相互制作的接触带。
In the Realm of the Diamond Queen grew out of Tsing's doctoral fieldwork in South Kalimantan, Indonesia. Through the 1970s and 1980s the Meratus lived where state forestry, Islamisation, logging and New Order development overlapped; Tsing follows how they kept organising marriage, mobility, hunting and ritual in a place the state treated as remote. The title comes from a local legend of a queen who arrives from elsewhere, leaves an order behind, and departs — leaving relations that must be retold. Marginality, Tsing argues, is not the opposite of the centre but a contact zone where state, capital and local life make one another.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

全书以民族志章节而非编年展开：先写国家如何用地图、身份证与林业法规把梅拉瑙人简化为「游耕者/原始人」，再写梅拉瑙人如何用迁徙、亲属关系与仪式把这些范畴拆开；性别与萨满实践是两条副线，显示边缘秩序并不只是「抵抗国家」，也在内部生产权威与差异。结论把「边缘」重新定义为一种关系位置，而非地理剩余。
The book unfolds through ethnographic chapters rather than chronology: first how maps, identity cards and forestry law simplify the Meratus into 'shifting cultivators' or 'primitives', then how mobility, kinship and ritual prise those categories apart. Gender and shamanic practice run as subplots, showing that marginal order is not only resistance to the state but also an internal production of authority and difference. The conclusion redefines marginality as a relational position, not a geographical leftover.

核心论题 · ThesisThesis

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

出版后成为东南亚研究与人类学「边缘性」讨论的常用参照；「out-of-the-way place」一语被广泛借用来描述国家触角之外的场所。 早期评论也指出：民族志细节丰富，但理论框架尚未像《摩擦》那样系统化；「边缘」有时被读成抵抗的浪漫场所。 **合成 Synthesis：** 把本书读成「边缘作为方法」的起点，而不是「摩擦」的完成版：它提供了后来所有概念的田野感性，但还没有把全球连接本身当作对象。
It became a common reference in Southeast Asian studies and anthropological debates on marginality; the phrase 'out-of-the-way place' was widely borrowed for sites beyond the state's reach. Early reviews also noted that the ethnography is rich but the theoretical frame is not yet as systematic as in Friction; the margin can be read as a romantic site of resistance. **Synthesis:** Read it as the starting point of 'marginality as method', not as the finished version of Friction: it supplies the field sensibility for all the later concepts but does not yet make global connection itself the object.

当时的接受 · Contemporary receptionContemporary reception

1993 年由 Princeton University Press 出版，随后进入东南亚研究与人类学课程。评论普遍肯定其民族志密度与对「边缘」概念的重写，也注意到它仍是一部区域民族志。

Published by Princeton University Press in 1993, it entered Southeast Asian studies and anthropology syllabi. Reviews generally praised its ethnographic density and its rewriting of marginality, while noting that it remained a regional ethnography.

后世的再评价 · Later reassessmentLater reassessment

后世把它视为 Tsing 全部工作的起点：从「边缘」到「摩擦」，再到「多物种」，问题一直是「不同世界如何在不平等权力中相互接触」。

Later scholarship treats it as the origin of all Tsing's work: from margin to friction to multispecies, the question remains how different worlds meet under unequal power.

影响 · InfluenceInfluence

「out-of-the-way place」成为讨论国家边缘、基础设施与地方生活的常用词；本书也常与斯科特的国家简化论、Escobar 的领地政治并读。

'Out-of-the-way place' became a common term for state margins, infrastructure and local life; the book is often read alongside Scott on state simplification and Escobar on territory.

争议与反证 · CritiqueCritique

最主要的保留是：本书仍以「边缘—中心」为隐含框架，且对跨国资本与全球连接的处理要到《摩擦》才充分展开；若把它当作完整的全球化理论，会高估其范围。

The main reservation is that the book still implies a margin–centre frame, and its treatment of transnational capital and global connection only becomes full in Friction; reading it as a complete theory of globalisation overestimates its scope.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

边缘作为方法：读《钻石女王的领地》Marginality as method

Tsing 的起点：边缘不是缺席，而是另一种世界生成的现场。Tsing's starting point: marginality is not absence but another site of world-making.

《钻石女王的领地》常被当作 Tsing 的「早期作品」读过，但它的野心并不小：它要把「边缘」从一个地理形容词改造成一个分析位置。梅拉瑙人生活在国家林业、伊斯兰化与伐木业的交叠处，Tsing 关心的不是他们「缺少」什么，而是他们如何用迁徙、亲属与仪式，把国家强加的范畴重新拆开。

这本书最有力的地方，是让「国家简化」与「地方重述」在同一页上发生。地图、身份证与林业法规把复杂的地方压成可治理的图式；而梅拉瑙人的迁徙与仪式，又不断把这些图式拖回具体的关系。边缘因此不是中心的失败复制，而是中心必须不断处理的外部。

它的局限也同样明显：本书仍以「边缘—中心」为隐含框架，跨国资本与全球 NGO 要到《摩擦》才成为正面对象。若把本书当作完整的全球化理论，会高估它的范围；但若把它当作「边缘作为方法」的起点，它几乎提供了后来所有问题的种子。

读这本书，最好的方式不是寻找「Tsing 后来讲了什么」，而是看她如何把一个看似边缘的田野，写成理解国家、资本与生活形式之间关系的入口。边缘不是世界的剩余；边缘是世界被制作出来的地方之一。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

In the Realm of the Diamond Queen is often read as Tsing's early work, but its ambition is larger: it turns marginality from a geographical adjective into an analytical position. The Meratus live where state forestry, Islamisation and logging overlap; Tsing asks not what they lack but how mobility, kinship and ritual prise open the categories the state imposes.

The book's strength is to make state simplification and local retelling happen on the same page. Maps, identity cards and forestry law compress a complex place into governable schemas; Meratus mobility and ritual keep dragging those schemas back into concrete relations. The margin is therefore not a failed copy of the centre but an outside the centre must keep processing.

Its limit is equally clear: the book still implies a margin–centre frame, and transnational capital and global NGOs only become explicit objects in Friction. Read as a complete theory of globalisation, it overreaches; read as the starting point of marginality as method, it seeds almost every later question.

The best way to read it is not to look for what Tsing will say later but to watch how she turns an apparently marginal field into an entry point for understanding state, capital and forms of life. The margin is not the world's leftover; it is one of the places where the world is made.
