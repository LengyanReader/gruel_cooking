长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

尺度改变问题：读《远方的地平线》Scale changes the question

对远读的再反省：尺度不是中性的，量化也不等于客观。A reflection on distant reading: scale is not neutral, and quantification is not objectivity.

《Distant Horizons》不是又一本「远读宣言」，而是对远读的再反省。它的核心问题是：当我们把分析单位从一部作品换成一个世纪、一个文类、一个语料库，我们究竟看见了什么、又看不见什么？

它的力量在于把「尺度」写成证据问题，而不是技术问题；它的风险也在这里：对尺度的反省仍可能是实证主义的自我修补，模型不透明与语料偏差也仍在。

读它时最好把「量化」当作一种修辞而非保证：它能让某些模式可见，却不能自动给出解释。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Distant Horizons is not another distant-reading manifesto but a reflection on it. Its core question: when the unit moves from a work to a century, a genre or a corpus, what exactly becomes visible and what disappears?

Its power is to treat scale as a problem of evidence rather than technique; its risk is that such reflection may remain positivist self-repair, with model opacity and corpus bias intact.

Read quantification as a rhetoric rather than a guarantee: it makes some patterns visible but does not automatically explain them.
