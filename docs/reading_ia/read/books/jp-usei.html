为何此刻Why now

漱石随笔的中文选本，与《草枕》的出世之美互为表里。
A Chinese selection of Soseki's essays, the inverse of Kusamakura's withdrawn beauty.

背景Background

写胃溃疡的吐血、写伦敦的忧郁、写「个人主义」的界限，也写夏目式的俏皮。漱石把近代知识人的自我审视推到病榻上：浮世是入世的观察，病榻是向内的解剖。
Writing the bleeding ulcer, the London melancholy, the limits of individualism, and Soseki's wit — a modern intellectual honing lucidity on the sickbed: the floating world observed outward, the self dissected inward.
