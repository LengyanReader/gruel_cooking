为何此刻Why now

《浮世与病榻》互为表里的虚构面：一个入世的病，一个出世的静。
The fiction inverse of the Sickbed volume: engaged illness vs withdrawn stillness.

背景Background

开篇「智に働けば角が立つ」一句立起全书：非情（去人情）之美、俳谐之心，让叙述者以画意观看山中女子，不动情而观照。它是漱石「非人情」美学主张的小说化。
Its famous opening sets the whole: beauty beyond emotion, a haiku-mind, letting the narrator view a mountain woman pictorially without passion. It novelises Soseki's creed of disinterestedness.
