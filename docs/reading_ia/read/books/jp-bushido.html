背景Background

新渡户以义、勇、仁、礼、信、忠、名誉诸德，把武士道写成一部「道德组织」的自辩；受西方读者欢迎，也招来把日本尚武精神本质化的批评，成为后世《菊与刀》一路论著的靶子。
Nitobe sets out justice, courage, benevolence, courtesy, sincerity, loyalty and honour as a moral organisation of the soul — welcomed by Western readers, yet charged with essentialising Japanese militarism, and a target for later works from Benedict onward.
