为何此刻Why now

近代日本美学论文里方法论最自觉的一部，九鬼以海德格尔式分析解剖江户的粹。
The most methodologically self-conscious of modern Japanese aesthetic essays — Kuki dissects Edo's iki with a Heideggerian analysis.

背景Background

原刊于《思想》1930 年，后入岩波。九鬼把粹析为媚态、意地（不服输）、达观（諦め）诸契机的统一，是对一个只可意会的江户趣味的哲学立法。英译通道为 Nara 的《The Structure of Detachment》。
First in the journal Shiso (1930), later Iwanami. Kuki parses iki as the unity of coquetry, defiance and resignation — a philosophical legislation of an untranslatable Edo taste. Its English channel is Nara's The Structure of Detachment.
