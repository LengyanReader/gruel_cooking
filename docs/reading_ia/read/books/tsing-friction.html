背景Background

《摩擦》基于 Tsing 在印尼加里曼丹与雅加达的田野，追踪三组「普世声张」——热带雨林保护、矿业开发与原住民权利——如何在同一片森林里相遇。她跟随自然保护协会（The Nature Conservancy）的项目、伐木工人、流动的梅拉瑙与马来社区，观察全球连接如何被具体的人、文件、资金与误解拼接起来。核心论点是：全球化不是平滑的流动，而是充满阻力的「摩擦」；正是摩擦让普遍性获得地方形态，也让地方生活被卷入更大的权力网络。
Friction draws on fieldwork in Kalimantan and Jakarta to follow three universal claims — rainforest conservation, mining development and indigenous rights — as they meet in the same forest. Tsing tracks a Nature Conservancy project, loggers, mobile Meratus and Malay communities, and asks how global connection is assembled from people, documents, money and misunderstanding. The thesis is that globalisation is not smooth flow but friction; friction is what gives universals local form and pulls local life into wider power networks.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

全书分三部分：先写「繁荣」——森林如何被不同力量视为资源、保护区或家园；再写「知识」——地图、分类与 NGO 文件如何把地方转译成可治理的对象；最后写「自由」——流离、迁徙与「不服从」如何在全球连接的缝隙中发生。案例彼此不完全连贯，Tsing 有意保留民族志的碎片性，让「摩擦」作为方法而非结论出现。
The book has three parts: prosperity — how the forest is seen as resource, reserve or home; knowledge — how maps, categories and NGO documents translate place into governable objects; and freedom — how displacement, mobility and refusal happen in the seams of global connection. The cases do not fully cohere, and Tsing deliberately keeps the ethnography fragmentary so that friction appears as method rather than conclusion.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

出版后成为全球化人类学的核心文本；「摩擦」被广泛用于描述全球与地方之间既冲突又生成的接触。 评论也指出：案例的碎片性让论证不易收束；「摩擦」有时被用得过于宽泛，几乎等于「一切接触」。 **合成 Synthesis：** 本书的贡献不是给出全球化的总理论，而是提供一个可追踪的中层概念：看普遍性如何在具体接触点被翻译、扭曲与重新发明。
It became a core text in the anthropology of globalisation; 'friction' is widely used for contact that is both conflictual and generative. Reviews also noted that the fragmentary cases make the argument hard to close, and that 'friction' can be used so broadly as to mean almost any contact. **Synthesis:** The contribution is not a general theory of globalisation but a trackable middle-range concept: watch how universals are translated, twisted and reinvented at concrete points of contact.

推断 · The claimThe claim

Tsing 要证明：全球化不是普遍性从中心流向边缘，而是普遍性在边缘被摩擦、翻译与重新发明；全球连接因此是一个未完成的关系工程。

Tsing wants to show that globalisation is not universals flowing from centre to margin but universals being rubbed, translated and reinvented at the margin; global connection is therefore an unfinished relational project.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

书名把「摩擦」放在「全球连接」之前，等于把阻力而非流动当作解释原则；三部分分别处理繁荣、知识与自由，覆盖全球项目落地时最常被调动的三种资源；案例选择 NGO、矿业与原住民权利，是因为它们都以「普遍」之名行动。

The title puts friction before global connection, making resistance rather than flow the explanatory principle; the three parts handle prosperity, knowledge and freedom, the resources most often mobilised when global projects land; the cases — NGO, mining, indigenous rights — all act in the name of universals.

当时的接受 · Contemporary receptionContemporary reception

2000 年代中期出版后迅速进入全球化人类学课程；评论把它视为「全球民族志」的方法示范，而不仅是一部印尼研究。

Published in the mid-2000s, it quickly entered courses on the anthropology of globalisation; reviews treated it as a methodological demonstration of 'global ethnography', not only an Indonesian study.

后世的再评价 · Later reassessmentLater reassessment

「摩擦」后来成为跨学科高频词，被用于媒体研究、基础设施研究与环境人文；Tsing 自己则在《菌菇》中把摩擦转向多物种协作。

Friction later became a cross-disciplinary term in media studies, infrastructure studies and environmental humanities; Tsing herself turned it toward multispecies collaboration in Mushroom.

影响 · InfluenceInfluence

本书让「全球连接」从抽象结构变成可追踪的接触点，影响了后续关于 NGO、保护、供应链与基础设施的研究。

The book turned global connection from abstract structure into trackable points of contact, shaping later work on NGOs, conservation, supply chains and infrastructure.

争议与反证 · CritiqueCritique

主要保留是：案例碎片化使「摩擦」难以被证伪；概念的外延过宽，可能把一切接触都称为摩擦。此外，本书对资本结构的处理仍偏民族志，而非政治经济学。

The main reservation is that the fragmentary cases make friction hard to falsify; the concept is broad enough to call almost any contact friction. The treatment of capital also remains ethnographic rather than political-economic.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

摩擦作为方法：读《摩擦》Friction as method

全球化不是流动，而是普遍性在具体接触点被摩擦、翻译与重新发明。Globalisation is not flow but universals being rubbed, translated and reinvented at concrete points of contact.

《摩擦》最容易被误读成一本「反全球化」的书。它其实更冷静：Tsing 不否认全球连接的存在，她只是拒绝把它写成平滑的流动。热带雨林保护、矿业开发与原住民权利，三组都以「全人类」或「自然」之名说话；当它们进入加里曼丹的森林，就必须经过文件、会议、翻译与误解。摩擦不是连接的失败，而是连接得以发生的方式。

这本书的方法也值得注意：Tsing 不从一个理论体系出发，而是从多个接触点出发，让概念在案例之间慢慢显形。读者会觉得结构松散，但这种松散是有意的——它模拟了全球连接本身的不完整。

局限同样明显：当「摩擦」可以指任何接触时，它就不再能区分不同性质的冲突；本书对资本积累的整体结构也着墨不多。它更适合被当作一个中层方法，而不是全球化的总理论。

读《摩擦》最好的收获，或许是学会在看全球项目时问：它在这里被翻译成了什么？谁在翻译？翻译过程中失去了什么？这些问题比「全球化是好是坏」更有用。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Friction is easily misread as an anti-globalisation book. It is cooler than that: Tsing does not deny global connection; she refuses to write it as smooth flow. Rainforest conservation, mining and indigenous rights all speak in the name of humanity or nature; when they enter a Kalimantan forest they must pass through documents, meetings, translation and misunderstanding. Friction is not the failure of connection but the way connection happens.

The method matters too: Tsing does not start from a theoretical system but from multiple points of contact, letting concepts emerge slowly across cases. The structure feels loose, but the looseness is deliberate — it mimics the incompleteness of global connection itself.

The limits are equally clear: when friction can mean any contact, it can no longer distinguish kinds of conflict; the book also says little about the overall structure of capital accumulation. It is better used as a middle-range method than as a general theory of globalisation.

The best takeaway may be a habit of asking, whenever a global project lands: what has it been translated into here, who is translating, and what is lost in translation? These questions are more useful than whether globalisation is good or bad.
