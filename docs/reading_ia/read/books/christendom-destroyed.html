背景 · Background背景 · Background

Greengrass（谢菲尔德大学近代早期史荣休教授，前格拉斯哥、Warwick）撰写第五卷。时间跨度 1517–1648：从路德发难到三十年战争结束的威斯特伐利亚和约。核心论题：「基督教世界」(Christendom) 不是一个制度而是一种共同想象——宗教改革打破了这个想象，而打破后的代价是一百三十年的内战与外交重组。涵盖瑞士改革、再洗礼派、法国宗教战争、尼德兰革命、西班牙黄金时代、古斯塔夫二世、三十年战争。Greengrass 另一部成名作是《The European Reformation, c.1450–1618》（1996 初版，2015 第 3 版），是英语宗教改革史的标准教科书。
By Greengrass (Emeritus Prof of Early Modern History, University of Sheffield; previously Glasgow and Warwick). Volume 5, 1517–1648: from Luther to Westphalia. Core thesis: 'Christendom' was not an institution but a shared imagination — the Reformation broke it, and the price of breaking it was 130 years of civil war and diplomatic reorganization. Covers Swiss Reformation, Anabaptists, French Wars of Religion, Dutch Revolt, Spanish Golden Age, Gustavus Adolphus, Thirty Years' War. His companion book The European Reformation (1996, 3rd ed. 2015) is the English-language textbook.

「对宗教改革时代最完整、最具文学性的英语叙述」——Times Literary Supplement。 批评不止于体量：其一，1517至1648的长时段全景在德意志、法兰西、伊比利亚、意大利与东欧之间频繁切换，牺牲了任何单一区域的纵深，被指「广而不深」；其二，书名所暗示的宏大论题与书中大量并置的宗教实践、艺术、科学、国家形成等专题之间，编织有时松散，「溶解」的总纲更多浮现于结论而非贯穿论证；其三，对非精英信仰与日常宗教经验的着墨相较政治—外交主线仍偏少。 **合成 Synthesis：** 基督教世界不是被某一场革命推翻的——它是在一个世纪的争吵、杀戮和制度化中缓慢溶解的。
'The most complete and literary English account of the Reformation era' — TLS. Criticism goes beyond sheer bulk: the long 1517-1648 panorama shuttles among Germany, France, Iberia, Italy and Eastern Europe at the cost of depth in any single region, read as 'wide rather than deep'; the grand thesis signalled by the title sits somewhat loosely beside the many juxtaposed specialties - religious practice, art, science, state-formation - so the 'dissolution' arc emerges more in the conclusion than through a sustained argument; and coverage of non-elite belief and everyday religion stays thin against the political-diplomatic spine. **Synthesis:** Christendom wasn't overthrown by one revolution — it dissolved slowly through a century of argument, killing, and institutionalization.

通读札记 · Read-through notesRead-through notes

通读结论Overall verdict

Greengrass 用 8 节写完 1517-1648 一场『共同想象的溶解』。通读之后可以确认：本书的真正贡献不在宗教改革叙事本身（新教-天主教-英国国教的三方已在英语学界充分讨论），而在把『一个西方教会』作为政治-文化想象的可拆分性来写。第 5 节（Silver Age → Iron Century）是全书方法论核心——他论证 1550 年前后伊拉斯谟式的和解可能如何被教派硬化吞没。第 8 节『Europe's Paroxysm』以威斯特伐利亚为终点，但真正论点是：1648 不是新开端，而是一个长达一百三十年的溶解过程的最后确认。全书与同时代其他宗教改革通史的最大差异是他对南-北欧（西班牙-意大利-波兰-瑞典）的对称处理。
Greengrass's 8 sections describe one thing: the dissolution of a shared imagination. On a full read the book's real contribution is not the Reformation narrative itself but treating 'one Western church' as a divisible political-cultural imaginary. Section 5 (Silver → Iron Century) is the methodological core — how c.1550 Erasmian reconciliation was swallowed by confessional hardening. Section 8 ends on Westphalia but argues 1648 is not a new start, only the last confirmation of a 130-year dissolution. His distinctive move against other Reformation surveys is symmetrical treatment of southern-northern Europe (Spain-Italy-Poland-Sweden).

『基督教世界』作为可拆分想象的开篇定题Christendom as divisible imaginary

Introduction 立刻把 Christendom 从『制度』重新定义为『共同想象』——这是全书的方法论声明。之后每一节都在证明：这个想象如何在神学、政治、艺术、印刷、朝圣五条边界上被同时拆散。
Introduction redefines Christendom from institution to shared imaginary — every subsequent section proves how it was dissolved along five edges: theology, politics, art, print, pilgrimage.

『Silver Age → Iron Century』是全书的转折点Silver-to-Iron is the book's turn

第 5 节用拉丁文学隐喻处理精神氛围的转折：1500-1550 的伊拉斯谟式『白银时代』宽容-和解，如何被 1550-1600 的『铁世纪』教派硬化吞没。这个论断使全书的重心从 1517 移到 1550。
Section 5 uses a Latin-literary metaphor for the shift of spirit: Erasmian silver-age tolerance (1500-50) swallowed by iron-century confessional rigidity (1550-1600). Moves the book's centre from 1517 to 1550.

个人生命史锚定结构变化Biographies anchoring structure

通读后可以看清 Greengrass 的方法——每一节开篇 2-3 个具体个体的生命史（一个修士、一个印刷匠、一个乡村女先知、一个西班牙军官），再把他们放回制度。这是新文化史方法在通史写作里较少见的严格应用。
Each section opens on 2-3 concrete lives (a monk, a printer, a village prophetess, a Spanish officer) before returning to institutions — a rigorous application of New Cultural History inside a general survey.

对『反宗教改革』的去污名De-stigmatising Counter-Reformation

第 6 节处理天主教改革时，Greengrass 拒绝用 Counter-Reformation 而使用 Catholic Reformation —— 并把依纳爵、阿维拉的德兰、斐理伯·内里作为与新教改革者对等的正面塑造者。这一立场是英语学界 1990 年代以来 (O'Malley, Prosperi) 的共识，本书是其在通史层面的最佳载体。
Section 6 refuses 'Counter-Reformation', uses 'Catholic Reformation', and treats Loyola, Teresa, Neri as positive shapers equivalent to Reformers. Aligning with O'Malley/Prosperi but at survey scale.

全书精读评析 · Full-read critical analysisFull-read critical analysis

通读后的整体判断：Greengrass 本书的最大贡献是把宗教改革从『一场革命』重新处理成『一个一百三十年的溶解过程』—— 这使他在同类通史 (MacMullen, Dickens, Cameron, Tanner, MacCulloch) 中位置独特。方法上他同时是：文化史家 (印刷图像、生命史锚点)、政治史家 (奥格斯堡/南特/威斯特伐利亚的条约史)、宗教史家 (教义-圣礼-教会学)、跨国史家 (西班牙-德意志-法国-英格兰-意大利-波兰-瑞典的并行)。这种四重性在同一本书里同时执行是罕见的。局限：(1) 东正教几乎完全缺席 —— 1517-1648 的莫斯科与拜占庭继承问题不在书内；(2) 对女性与家庭的处理集中在少数几节 —— 相比 Merry Wiesner-Hanks 的性别研究未吸收；(3) 对宗教改革与新大陆 (Latin American colonial church) 的联系只有一节 —— 与 Benevent 学派的全球宗教改革讨论相比克制。
**Overall verdict after a full read**: Greengrass's real contribution is treating the Reformation as a 130-year dissolution rather than a revolution — this positions him uniquely among contemporaries (Dickens, Cameron, Tanner, MacCulloch). He executes four genres at once: cultural (print, life-histories), political (Augsburg / Nantes / Westphalia), religious (doctrine, sacraments, ecclesiology), transnational (Spain-Germany-France-England-Italy-Poland-Sweden in parallel). Rarity of all four in one book. Limitations: (1) Eastern Orthodoxy almost entirely absent — Muscovy and the Byzantine inheritance don't appear; (2) women and family in a few sections only, without fully absorbing Merry Wiesner-Hanks; (3) Reformation and New World (colonial Latin American church) covered in a single sub-section, restrained vs the global-Reformation school.
