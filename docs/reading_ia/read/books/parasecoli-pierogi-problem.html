背景Background

Parasecoli、Bachórz 与 Halawa 研究波兰餐饮新世代，分析他们如何在全球化中「本地化」话语、实践与价值。 《The Pierogi Problem》2025 年由 University of California Press 出版，由 Parasecoli、Agata Bachórz 与 Mateusz Halawa 合著。它研究波兰餐饮新世代，分析他们如何在全球化中「本地化」话语、实践与价值。 它也可以被读成一次「后社会主义消费」研究：1989 之后，波兰的「传统」不只是怀旧，而是新一代在全球市场中重新定位自己的资源。
Parasecoli, Bachórz and Halawa study Poland's new food generation, analysing how they localise discourse, practice and value within globalization. The Pierogi Problem (University of California Press, 2025) is by Parasecoli, Agata Bachórz and Mateusz Halawa. It studies Poland's new food generation and how they localise discourse, practice and value within globalization. It can also be read as a study of post-socialist consumption: after 1989, Polish 'tradition' was not only nostalgia but a resource through which a new generation repositioned itself in global markets.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

饺子作为问题：读《The Pierogi Problem》The pierogi as a problem

把「全球布鲁克林」的方法落到波兰：后社会主义一代如何重新发明「传统」食物。Lands the Global Brooklyn method in Poland: how a post-socialist generation reinvents traditional food.

《The Pierogi Problem》用一个具体食物命名全书：饺子。它要问的是，波兰的「传统食物」如何被新一代生产者、厨师与媒体重新发明，以回应全球化的「世界主义胃口」。

它的力量在于把「传统」写成被选择、包装、再销售的过程；它的风险也在这里：单一国家案例可能高估断裂、低估延续，三位作者的分工也需要核对。

读它时最好把「传统」拆成四层：原料、技术、叙事、市场。每一层都在被重新发明，但并非同时、同速。若四层不同步，所谓「传统」就不是一个整体，而是一组正在谈判的碎片。

最强反读：所谓「再造」可能只是市场细分，而非真正的传统重构；单一国家案例也可能高估断裂。若如此，本书是「市场中的传统」，而不是「传统的重生」。

若把「传统」当作一个整体，会看不见四层并不同步：原料可能被保留，技术被替换，叙事被重写，市场被重新定位。真正的分析，是看四层如何错位。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Pierogi Problem names the book after one food. It asks how Polish traditional food is reinvented by a new generation of producers, chefs and media in response to cosmopolitan appetites.

Its power is to treat tradition as selected, packaged and resold; its risk is that a single-nation case may overstate rupture and understate continuity, and the three authors' division of labour needs checking.

Read tradition in four layers: ingredient, technique, narrative, market. Each is reinvented, but not at the same time or speed. If the layers do not sync, 'tradition' is not a whole but a set of fragments under negotiation.

Strongest counter-reading: reinvention may be market segmentation rather than genuine reconstruction; a single-nation case may overstate rupture. On that reading it is tradition in the market, not the rebirth of tradition.

Read tradition as a whole and the four layers' desynchronisation disappears: ingredients may stay, techniques be replaced, narratives rewritten, markets repositioned. Analysis watches the misalignment.
