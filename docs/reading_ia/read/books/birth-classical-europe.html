背景 · Background背景 · Background

Price（牛津大学 University College 古典学研究员，以《仪式与权力》1984 重写罗马皇帝崇拜研究）与 Thonemann（阿德莱德大学 Greek 教授，前牛津）合撰，是企鹅欧洲史九卷本的第一卷。Price 于 2011 年因肝癌去世，《古典欧洲的诞生》成为他最后一部完整专著。全书以「记忆」为方法论线索：从迈锡尼线形文字 B 到希腊字母的引入，从荷马史诗对特洛伊的追想到希罗多德与修昔底德的历史书写，从亚历山大到罗马帝国对希腊文化的兼收并蓄——核心问题是「古典欧洲」不是天然存在，而是被一代代人的记忆、遗忘与重写所「生成」。时间跨度约 2100 年（前 1700–后 410）。
By Price (Fellow & Tutor in Ancient History, University College Oxford; author of Rituals and Power (1984), which rewrote the study of Roman emperor worship) and Thonemann (Professor of Greek, Adelaide; previously Oxford). Price died of liver cancer on 14 June 2011, making Birth of Classical Europe his last completed monograph. Uses 'memory' as a methodological thread: from Linear B to the Greek alphabet, Homeric recollection to Herodotean/Thucydidean historiography, Alexander to Roman appropriation of Greek culture — the argument is that 'Classical Europe' was generated, not discovered. Spans ~2100 years (1700BC–AD410).

论述框架 · Analytical frameworkAnalytical framework

结构 · Structure结构 · Structure

编年体与专题交叉：大时间线以四章划分（宫殿时代、城邦时代、帝国时代），每章内按专题（政治、文化、宗教、经济）展开。特色在于每章以一件具体文物或文本为锚点（线形文字B泥板、荷马抄本、帕特农神庙、亚历山大金币、奥古斯丁手稿），从物质证据出发引出论证。
Chronological with thematic cross-cuts: four major periods (Palatial, Polis, Imperial), each subdivided thematically (politics, culture, religion, economy). Signature move: each section anchored on a specific artifact/text (Linear B tablet, Homeric manuscript, Parthenon, Alexandrian coinage, Augustine codex) — arguments proceed from material evidence.

评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis

被学界誉为「以最少文字提供最多问题」的古代史导论；对「古典」概念的建构论分析尤为精彩。 部分批评者认为经济史和近东比较着墨不足。 **合成 Synthesis：** 「古典欧洲」是被记忆建构的——Price/Thonemann 以九卷开篇完成了一个方法论宣言。
Praised as an ancient-history primer offering 'the most questions in the fewest words'; the constructivist analysis of 'classical' is outstanding. Some critics note insufficient economic history and Near Eastern comparison. **Synthesis:** 'Classical Europe' was memory-constructed — Price/Thonemann's opening volume delivers a methodological manifesto.
