背景 · Background背景 · Background

Price（牛津大学 University College 古典学研究员，以《仪式与权力》1984 重写罗马皇帝崇拜研究）与 Thonemann（阿德莱德大学 Greek 教授，前牛津）合撰，是企鹅欧洲史九卷本的第一卷。Price 于 2011 年因肝癌去世，《古典欧洲的诞生》成为他最后一部完整专著。全书以「记忆」为方法论线索：从迈锡尼线形文字 B 到希腊字母的引入，从荷马史诗对特洛伊的追想到希罗多德与修昔底德的历史书写，从亚历山大到罗马帝国对希腊文化的兼收并蓄——核心问题是「古典欧洲」不是天然存在，而是被一代代人的记忆、遗忘与重写所「生成」。时间跨度约 2100 年（前 1700–后 410）。
By Price (Fellow & Tutor in Ancient History, University College Oxford; author of Rituals and Power (1984), which rewrote the study of Roman emperor worship) and Thonemann (Professor of Greek, Adelaide; previously Oxford). Price died of liver cancer on 14 June 2011, making Birth of Classical Europe his last completed monograph. Uses 'memory' as a methodological thread: from Linear B to the Greek alphabet, Homeric recollection to Herodotean/Thucydidean historiography, Alexander to Roman appropriation of Greek culture — the argument is that 'Classical Europe' was generated, not discovered. Spans ~2100 years (1700BC–AD410).

评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis

被学界誉为「以最少文字提供最多问题」的古代史导论；对「古典」概念的建构论分析尤为精彩。 批评集中于三点：其一，虽以「欧洲」为题，近东、埃及与凯尔特/日耳曼边缘仍着墨有限，被指内核实为希腊—罗马正典；其二，为统摄「从特洛伊到奥古斯丁」四千年，经济、制度与人口史被大幅压缩，物质分析让位于观念与认同史；其三，「古典是被建构的记忆」这一后设框架虽锋利，却使部分传统古典学者担忧它把古人自身的自我理解也一并解构。 **合成 Synthesis：** 「古典欧洲」是被记忆建构的——Price/Thonemann 以九卷开篇完成了一个方法论宣言。
Praised as an ancient-history primer offering 'the most questions in the fewest words'; the constructivist analysis of 'classical' is outstanding. Criticism clusters on three points: for all its 'Europe' framing, treatment of the Near East, Egypt and the Celtic/Germanic periphery stays thin, keeping Greece-Rome as the canonical core; compressing four thousand years into one volume squeezes economic, institutional and demographic history in favour of ideas and identity; and the meta-frame that 'the classical is a constructed memory', though incisive, worries traditional classicists that it deconstructs the ancients' own self-understanding as well. **Synthesis:** 'Classical Europe' was memory-constructed — Price/Thonemann's opening volume delivers a methodological manifesto.

通读札记 · Read-through notesRead-through notes

通读结论Overall verdict

通读十章之后，Price 与 Thonemann 真正的论旨并非「希腊罗马是什么」，而是「欧洲各世纪如何选择性继承希腊罗马」——从荷马对特洛伊的追想、亚历山大的希腊化模仿、罗马对希腊的挪用、皇帝崇拜的省际扩散，直到奥古斯丁用『上帝之城』重新消化罗马。全书以『记忆-重述』贯穿九百年，收束于 Lullingstone 壁画，即把不列颠行省作为『古典欧洲』最终载体——是全书方法上最漂亮的一次翻转。
Read start-to-finish, Price & Thonemann's real thesis is not 'what Greece/Rome was' but 'how successive European centuries selectively inherited them' — Homer's memory of Troy, Alexander's Hellenism, Rome's appropriation of Greece, provincial diffusion of the imperial cult, Augustine's City of God digesting Rome. Nine centuries of memory-and-retelling, ending on a Kentish villa fresco — Britain as the final carrier of 'Classical Europe' is the book's finest methodological reversal.

Ch.1 与 Ch.9 的方法对称Symmetry Ch.1 vs Ch.9

第一章以荷马笔下的特洛伊开篇，第九章以 Lullingstone 的基督教壁画收尾——两端都是『后来人如何记忆更早的过去』；作者用同一方法处理青铜时代与晚期罗马，全书由此成为一部连贯的『记忆史』而非编年。
Both open and close on 'how later people remembered earlier pasts' — Homer's Troy and a Kentish Christian fresco — the same method applied at Bronze Age and Late Antiquity turns the book into a memory-history, not a chronicle.

「欧洲 vs 亚洲」范畴的三次发明Three inventions of Europe-vs-Asia

第 5 章希波战争、第 6 章亚历山大、第 7 章布匿战争 —— 作者实际上追踪的是这个二元范畴被希腊人、马其顿人、罗马人三次重新发明的过程；这是全书最原创的隐性论证线，但在 Introduction 里只一笔带过。
Chs. 5-7 quietly re-track the Greco-Persian, Alexandrine, Punic phases as three reinventions of the Europe/Asia binary — the book's most original implicit thread, only hinted at in the introduction.

皇帝崇拜作为『省际认同技术』Imperial cult as provincial identity technology

第 8 与第 9 章中，Price 用自己成名研究的核心命题——皇帝崇拜不是中央强加而是行省精英主动挪用——把罗马帝国从『征服叙事』改写为『共谋叙事』；这条线在通读时才充分显现。
In Chs. 8-9 Price's signature thesis — the imperial cult as provincial elite self-fashioning rather than top-down imposition — rewrites the empire from conquest to complicity; visible only on a read-through.

奥古斯丁作为全书的真正收束Augustine as the real terminus

表面上第 10 章处理 284-425 的晚期罗马；实际论证是：奥古斯丁用『上帝之城』把罗马装进基督教的历史——『古典欧洲』作为叙事被基督教接管。这是全书的哲学落点，值得单独重读 Ch.10。
On the surface Ch.10 is a Late Empire survey; in fact it's Augustine's City of God absorbing Rome into Christian history — the book's philosophical landing. Ch.10 rewards a second read.

全书精读评析 · Full-read critical analysisFull-read critical analysis

通读后的整体判断：Price 与 Thonemann 用 900 年篇幅完成的是一部『欧洲对希腊罗马的层累记忆』。全书最独特的贡献不是任何单章的实证发现，而是把『古典』作为一个可分析的概念处理：每一章都问『这一世纪里的哪些人、在什么情境下、以什么目的引用更早的过去』。这个方法让本书与传统希腊罗马通史（如 Boardman、Holladay、Brunt、Goldsworthy）显著区别开。第二值得注意：作者对『欧洲 vs 亚洲』二元范畴的谱系追踪 —— 从希罗多德、埃斯库罗斯、伊索克拉底、亚历山大、罗马对迦太基的定性、直到 4 世纪护教学 —— 是全书最原创的隐性主线。第三：本书 34 位图版作者几乎都在书内文字里被处理，不是附录 —— 这在同类通史里是罕见的图文互证密度。局限：作者处理 146 BC-AD 14 的罗马扩张 (Ch.7-8) 相对粗疏，与 Ch.9 的宗教-文化密度不匹配；对晚期共和国的奴隶制与家庭经济的处理也过于压缩。
**Overall verdict after a full read**: Price & Thonemann's 900 years is really a study of Europe's layered memory of Greece/Rome. The book's contribution isn't any single chapter but the treatment of 'Classical' as an analysable concept — every chapter asks who, in what context, and for what purpose is citing the earlier past. This distinguishes it from standard Greece/Rome surveys (Boardman, Holladay, Brunt, Goldsworthy). Second: the genealogy of Europe-vs-Asia from Herodotus to Augustine is the book's most original implicit thread. Third: nearly all 34 plates are worked into the text — unusual density of text-image cross-reference. Limitation: Chs.7-8 covering 146 BC - AD 14 Roman expansion are comparatively thin versus Ch.9's religious-cultural density, and the treatment of slavery and household economy in the late Republic is compressed.

In October 2005 a huge steel, bronze and glass sculpture was unveiled outside the seat of the European Parliament in Strasbourg. The sculpture, donated to the European Parliament by the town of Agios Nikolaos on Crete, depicts the mythological princess Europa, cast in bronze, riding on the back of a steel and glass bull. Once upon a time (or so the story goes), the god Zeus fell in love with a beautiful girl named Europa. In order to gain her affections, Zeus turned himself into a magnificent bull, and carried her on his back across the sea to Crete. According to some accounts, one of their three sons was a certain Minos, who became king of Crete. So the Strasbourg sculpture acts as a elegant symbol for the place of Crete in the history of Europe: since Europa later gave her name to the continent of Europe, the Minoan civilization on Crete marks the true beginning of European history.EN · english

In the beginning, there was Troy and the Trojan War, made famous in European history by the two epic poems ascribed to Homer, the Iliad and the Odyssey . According to these poems, the Trojan War began when Helen, wife of Menelaus, king of Sparta, was abducted by Paris, a prince of Troy. The Iliad tells the story of the anger of the Greek warrior Achilles at Troy; the Odyssey recounts the adventures of Odysseus on his way home from Troy to the island of Ithaca. We see these two epics as largely fictional stories, but these and later memories of the Trojan War were of fundamental importance within antiquity and beyond. Greeks, Romans and others found their origins in the events surrounding the Trojan War, the founding acts of European history. Before looking at the later importance of the Trojan War, we need to understand the period in which these later tales are set, the period of the palaces in Crete and mainland Greece.EN · english
