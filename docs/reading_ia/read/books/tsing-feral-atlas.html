背景Background

Feral Atlas 是一个数字项目（feralatlas.org），由 Tsing、Jennifer Deger、Alder Keleman Saxena 与 Feifei Zhou 合编。它收集「野性」的田野报告：工业基础设施、殖民农业、水坝、港口与军事基地如何产生超出设计意图的生态后果。项目以互动地图、短文、图像与声音组织材料，让读者在具体地点追踪「超越人类的人类世」。它不是一本纸质书，而是一个可探索的公共知识平台。
Feral Atlas is a digital project (feralatlas.org) edited by Tsing, Jennifer Deger, Alder Keleman Saxena and Feifei Zhou. It gathers field reports of the 'feral': how industrial infrastructure, colonial agriculture, dams, ports and military bases produce ecological consequences beyond their design. Interactive maps, short essays, images and sound let readers track the more-than-human Anthropocene in specific places. It is not a print book but an explorable public knowledge platform.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

项目以「田野报告」为基本单位，每个报告从一个具体基础设施或地点出发，追踪其野性后果，再通过地图与标签与其他报告连接。读者不按线性章节阅读，而是在地点、物种、基础设施类型之间跳转。Tsing 的导论把「野性」定义为「人类活动引发、却超出人类控制的生命后果」，并说明为什么数字平台比纸质书更适合呈现这种多线索、多尺度的材料。
The project's basic unit is the field report: each starts from a specific infrastructure or site, traces its feral consequences, and connects to other reports through maps and tags. Readers do not follow linear chapters but move among places, species and infrastructure types. Tsing's introduction defines the feral as 'life consequences set off by human activity but beyond human control' and explains why a digital platform suits this multi-threaded, multi-scale material better than print.

核心论题 · ThesisThesis

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

上线后被视为数字环境人文的代表作，常与《菌菇》《野外指南》并读；它让「野性」成为描述基础设施生态后果的常用词。 作为数字项目，它的可访问性、长期维护与引用稳定性都受到限制；「野性」也可能被读成一种审美化的越界。 **合成 Synthesis：** Feral Atlas 的贡献是把「人类世」从全球模型变成可点击的地点：每一个野性后果都可以被追踪到具体的基础设施与生命。
Since launch it has been treated as a landmark of digital environmental humanities, often read with Mushroom and the Field Guide; it made 'feral' a common term for the ecological afterlives of infrastructure. As a digital project it faces limits of access, long-term maintenance and citational stability; 'feral' can also be read as an aestheticised transgression. **Synthesis:** The contribution is to turn the Anthropocene from a global model into clickable places: every feral consequence can be traced to specific infrastructure and life.

推断 · The claimThe claim

编者要证明：人类世不是全球均匀的模型，而是由基础设施在具体地点产生的野性后果；数字图谱是呈现这种多线索现实的最合适形式。

The editors want to show that the Anthropocene is not a globally even model but feral consequences produced by infrastructure in specific places; a digital atlas is the most suitable form for this multi-threaded reality.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

可点击的人类世：读 Feral AtlasA clickable Anthropocene

把「人类世」从全球模型变成可点击的地点：每一个野性后果都能被追踪到具体的基础设施与生命。Turning the Anthropocene from a global model into clickable places: every feral consequence can be traced to specific infrastructure and life.

Feral Atlas 最激进的地方不是它的内容，而是它的形式。它拒绝把人类世写成一本从第一章读到最后一章的书，而是让读者在地图、物种与基础设施之间跳转。每一个「田野报告」都从一个具体地点出发：一座水坝、一片种植园、一个港口、一处军事基地。报告追踪的不是「自然被破坏」，而是「人类活动产生了什么超出控制的生命后果」。

这种形式与论点是一致的：如果人类世是斑驳的、多线索的，那么知识形态也应该是斑驳的、多入口的。Tsing 与编者把「野性」定义为人类活动引发却超出人类控制的生命后果，这个定义既避免了「自然 vs 人类」的二分，也避免了把一切后果都归给资本的简化。

局限也来自形式：数字项目的可访问性与长期维护不稳定，引用与教学使用都比纸质书困难；「野性」的扩散叙事也可能淡化具体公司与国家的责任。它更像一个可探索的公共档案，而不是一部完整的理论著作。

把它与《野外指南》对读，会看见 Tsing 的方法在两种媒介之间的迁移：数字平台让材料可探索，纸质指南让方法可携带。两者都不是终点，而是同一场「注意」训练的不同工具。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Feral Atlas is most radical not in its content but in its form. It refuses to write the Anthropocene as a book read from first chapter to last, letting readers jump among maps, species and infrastructure. Each field report starts from a specific site: a dam, a plantation, a port, a military base. What it traces is not 'nature being destroyed' but 'what life consequences human activity sets off beyond control'.

The form matches the argument: if the Anthropocene is patchy and multi-threaded, knowledge should also be patchy and multi-entry. Tsing and the editors define the feral as life consequences set off by human activity but beyond human control, avoiding both the nature/human binary and the reduction of all consequences to capital.

The limits come from the form: digital access and long-term maintenance are unstable, citation and teaching are harder than with print, and the diffuse narrative of the feral can underplay the responsibility of specific firms and states. It is more an explorable public archive than a complete theoretical work.

Read alongside the Field Guide, it shows Tsing's method migrating between media: the digital platform makes material explorable, the printed guide makes the method portable. Neither is an endpoint; both are tools in the same training of attention.
