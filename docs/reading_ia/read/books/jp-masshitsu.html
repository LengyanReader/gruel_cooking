为何此刻Why now

特辑美学册之一，谷崎《阴翳礼赞》之后对「消逝之美」最彻底的一次当代重写：不是欣赏旧物，而是承认万物都在被时间抹去。
One of the Special's aesthetic volumes and the most thorough contemporary rewrite of the beauty of decay after In Praise of Shadows: not a taste for old things but an acknowledgement that everything is being erased.

背景Background

原作《摩滅の賦》由筑摩書房于 2003 年出版，收十三篇随笔。四方田犬彦（1953— ）是比较文学与电影学者，长期在明治学院大学任教。他从被抚摸到只剩轮廓的浮雕、被参拜者摸得发亮的佛像、被海浪侵蚀的岩石，一直写到口中的糖果——以「摩灭」为方法重看建筑、艺术与日常物。中译由蕾克翻译，2020 年 1 月出版；繁体译本为黃毓婷译（黑眼睛文化，2017）。
The original, Masshitsu no Fu, appeared from Chikuma Shobō in 2003 in thirteen essays. Yomota Inuhiko (b. 1953), a comparatist and film scholar long at Meiji Gakuin University, moves from reliefs worn to mere outline, Buddhas polished black by pilgrims' hands and rocks eaten by waves to a sweet in the mouth — using erosion as a method for rereading architecture, art and everyday things. The simplified Chinese is by Lei Ke (January 2020); the traditional Chinese by Huang Yuting (Black Eyes, 2017).

第 1 篇 · 石与佛：被触摸磨去的形Piece 1 · Stone and Buddha: form worn by touch

开篇诸篇集中于宗教造像与石窟：信仰的手比风雨更快地抹平轮廓。摩灭在此不是损坏，而是被使用过的证据。
The opening pieces centre on religious images and grottoes: hands of faith erase contours faster than weather. Erosion here is not damage but evidence of use.

结构Structure

十三篇不按论证推进，而按「物」编排：每一篇从一个具体的磨损对象起笔，转到艺术史与个人记忆，最后落回一个几乎不可见的细节。这种写法让「摩灭」既是主题也是结构——读者读到的不是定义，而是一再重复的观看动作。
The thirteen pieces advance not by argument but by object: each begins with something worn, turns to art history and personal memory, and returns to an almost invisible detail. Erosion is thus both theme and structure — the reader gets not a definition but a repeated act of looking.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

被读作日本「物哀—侘寂」传统在当代最有说服力的续篇：不靠怀旧，而靠对材料与时间的精确观察。中译本使它进入中文美学阅读的书单。 也有批评认为十三篇的笔法过于均匀，几乎每篇都循「物件—历史—感悟」的同一弧线，读多则套路显形；理论层面则始终停在隐喻，未与物质文化研究正面交锋。 **合成 Synthesis：** 放在《阴翳礼赞》之后读：谷崎为幽暗辩护，四方田则承认幽暗也在消失。两本合起来，才是日本「消逝美学」的完整弧线。
Read as the most persuasive contemporary sequel to the Japanese line of aware and wabi-sabi: not nostalgia but precise observation of material and time. Some critics find the thirteen pieces too even, each following the same arc of object–history–reflection, so the method shows as formula; theoretically it stays at metaphor and never engages material-culture studies head-on. **Synthesis:** Read after In Praise of Shadows: Tanizaki defends gloom, Yomota concedes that gloom too is vanishing. Together they form the full arc of Japan's aesthetics of disappearance.

手法与亮点 · CraftCraft & highlights

以物起兴、以学收束：每篇先给一个可触的物件，再引入艺术史与旅行经验，最后以一句近乎箴言的短句收尾。

Object first, scholarship after: each piece opens with something touchable, brings in art history and travel, and closes on an almost aphoristic line.

第一人称的克制：作者很少抒情，宁可多写一厘米的磨损，也不写一句感叹。

Restrained first person: rather than lament, the author describes another centimetre of wear.

比较视野：吴哥、罗马、日本并列，使「摩灭」不被写成日本特殊性。

Comparative frame: Angkor, Rome and Japan side by side, so erosion is not confined to Japanese exceptionalism.

论旨概述 · 非逐字原文Paraphrase · not a verbatim quotation
「摩灭」指物在被时间磨去棱角、姓名与轮廓之后，仍以残余的形式留在世上。四方田犬彦不把磨损看作损失，而看作物回到无名状态的必经之路；十三篇各写一件正在消失的东西，合起来是一部关于「减法」的艺术论。
Masshitsu names what remains after time has worn away an object's edges, name and outline. Yomota Inuhiko treats erosion not as loss but as the passage back to anonymity. Thirteen pieces, each on something disappearing, add up to an art theory of subtraction.

精读建议 · Deep readingDeep reading

准备一张纸，记录每篇的「物件—媒介—收束句」，读完会看到十三篇的共同骨架，也能看出哪一篇偏离了它。
Keep a sheet listing each piece's object, medium and closing line; the shared skeleton will appear, and so will the piece that departs from it.
与《阴翳礼赞》对读同一主题（器物之美）：谷崎看的是光如何被遮住，四方田看的是形如何被磨掉。
Read against In Praise of Shadows on the same theme: Tanizaki watches how light is shaded, Yomota how form is worn.
读完去摸一件常用的旧物（钥匙、杯沿），检验「摩灭」是否真能被触觉验证，而非只是文字隐喻。
Afterwards touch a worn everyday object (a key, a cup's rim) and test whether erosion is tactilely verifiable or only a verbal metaphor.
