One of the Special's aesthetic volumes, a contemporary revisit of the beauty of decay after In Praise of Shadows.

背景Background

摩灭＝磨损、消逝之光。四方田犬彦从石窟、贝壳、老照片到建筑，写万物在时间中被慢慢抹去的美；以比较文学者的视野，把日本的阴翳／侘寂接入世界物质文化史。
Masshitsu is the light of wearing away. From grottoes and shells to old photographs, Yomitari writes the beauty things gain as time erases them, linking Japan's shadow and wabi-sabi to a world material culture.
