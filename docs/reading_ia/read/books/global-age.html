背景 · Background背景 · Background

Kershaw 第二次为该系列执笔（前卷 To Hell and Back 1914–1949），写终卷 1950–2017。本书原为 2007 年 Roller-Coaster: Europe 1950–2010（Penguin UK），2019 年全面修订扩展至 2017 并改名为 The Global Age（US 版 Viking/Penguin Press 2019-04-30；UK 版 2020 年 9 月以 Roller-Coaster 更新版发行）。核心论题：战后欧洲经历了「黄金三十年」（1950–1973 高增长与福利国家建立）、「滞胀与转折」（1973–1989 石油危机、Thatcher/Reagan、东欧解体）、和「后冷战全球化」（1989–2017 欧盟扩展、欧元、2008 危机、难民潮、脱欧、民粹崛起）。结论开放：欧洲站在十字路口。
Kershaw's second contribution (after To Hell and Back), writing the series finale 1950–2017. Originally Roller-Coaster: Europe 1950–2010 (2007 Penguin UK), comprehensively revised and extended to 2017 in 2019 as The Global Age (Viking/Penguin Press US 2019-04-30; UK edition follows in Sep 2020 as updated Roller-Coaster). Core thesis: post-war Europe experienced 'Golden Age' (1950–1973 growth & welfare state), 'Stagnation & Turn' (1973–1989 oil crisis, Thatcher/Reagan, Eastern European collapse), and 'Post-Cold-War Globalization' (1989–2017 EU expansion, euro, 2008 crisis, migration, Brexit, populism). Open conclusion: Europe at a crossroads.

结构 · Structure结构 · Structure

三段式编年（黄金-转折-全球化）；Kershaw 在终卷中更强调欧洲在全球化背景下的相对衰落——中国崛起、美国霸权、移民挑战作为外部框架。
Three-phase chronology (golden, turning, globalization); the finale emphasizes Europe's relative decline in globalization context — China's rise, US hegemony, migration as external frame.

'Writes post-war seven decades into a whole with compelling breadth' — Financial Times. Revised from Roller-Coaster, some older content may be stale; Eastern European post-1989 transitions uneven. **Synthesis:** After three millennia the question is no longer 'what is Europe' but 'where does Europe go' — Kershaw closes the entire series with openness.
