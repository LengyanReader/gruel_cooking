「五大革命」——科学、政治、农业、工业、（第二次）宗教——Blanning 以这个框架把 1648–1815 两百年的欧洲写成一部多声部变革交响，也奠定了他此后《文化的权力》一书的史学路线。
The 'Five Revolutions' — scientific, political, agricultural, industrial, and (second) religious — Blanning frames two centuries of Europe as a polyphonic symphony of transformation, laying the ground for his later Culture of Power.

背景 · Background背景 · Background

Blanning（剑桥大学近代史教授 1993–2005，后转任都柏林圣三一学院历史学荣休教授 · FBA · 以《The Culture of Power and the Power of Culture》《The French Revolutionary Wars》《The Triumph of Music》闻名）第六卷覆盖 1648–1815——从威斯特伐利亚到维也纳会议。以「五大革命」为概念框架：科学、政治、农业、工业、（第二次）宗教；同时涵盖启蒙运动、绝对主义与立宪主义的竞争、路易十四到路易十六、英国光荣革命与工业化、哈布斯堡与奥斯曼、俄国彼得大帝到叶卡捷琳娜、法国大革命与拿破仑。「荣耀」(glory) 是全书的关键词——旧制度晚期的欧洲以荣誉、礼仪、品味、音乐与国家竞争互相编织。2013 年修订版更新了史学文献。
By Blanning (Professor of Modern History, Cambridge 1993–2005; subsequently Fellow of Trinity College Dublin; FBA; author of The Culture of Power and the Power of Culture, The French Revolutionary Wars, and The Triumph of Music). Volume 6, 1648–1815 — Westphalia to Vienna. 'Five Revolutions' as conceptual frame; also Enlightenment, absolutism vs constitutionalism, Louis XIV to XVI, Glorious Revolution & industrialization, Habsburgs & Ottomans, Peter to Catherine, French Revolution & Napoleon. 'Glory' is the keyword — the late ancien régime weaves honour, ceremony, taste, music, and state-competition into one fabric. 2013 revised edition updates historiography.

评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis

「以惊人的博学把两百年的多国历史融为可读的整体」——Literary Review。 框架的代价是主要争议点：以「五场革命」统摄1648至1815，使工业革命一章几乎只讲英格兰，欧陆与殖民地的经济变迁被边缘化；革命范式的取舍亦引发质疑——作者刻意淡化1789法国大革命的政治断裂，将其纳入渐进变革，部分革命史学者认为这低估了大革命的结构性冲击；此外「文化革命」一章以德法英美学为轴，对东欧几近略过。 **合成 Synthesis：** 现代欧洲不是一次革命而是五场变革的叠加——Blanning 以此挑战了「long 18th century = Age of Revolution」的简化叙事。
'Astounding erudition fusing two centuries of multi-national history into a readable whole' — Literary Review. The frame itself is the main point of contention: subsuming 1648-1815 under 'five revolutions' leaves the Industrial Revolution chapter essentially about England, marginalising continental and colonial economic change; and Blanning's deliberate downplaying of 1789 as a political rupture - folding the French Revolution into gradual transformation - draws fire from revolutionary historians who think it understates the structural shock of 1789; the 'cultural revolution' chapter, too, runs on a Franco-British-German aesthetic axis with the East largely omitted. **Synthesis:** Modern Europe wasn't made by one revolution but by five overlapping transformations — Blanning challenges the simplified 'long 18th century = Age of Revolution' narrative.

通读札记 · Read-through notesRead-through notes

通读结论Overall verdict

Blanning 全书四部（生与死 / 权力 / 宗教与文化 / 战争与和平）实际是把『近代欧洲的造就』拆成身体-政治-文化-国际四条同时演化的曲线。通读之后：本书的『荣耀』不是修辞，而是一种可追踪的社会资本——它同时解释路易十四的凡尔赛礼仪、莫扎特的公共音乐会、腓特烈大帝的哲学王形象、拿破仑的荣誉军团。Blanning 的『五场革命』框架在书内实际只用三次：科学革命 (Ch.1)、政治革命 (Ch.2)、文化革命 (Ch.3)——农业与工业革命在他的 2000 年后论述中被压缩。全书对 1780-1815 的处理是全书最完整的部分，尤其第四部『战争与和平』把法国革命战争-拿破仑战争-维也纳体系作为同一场『荣耀危机』的三段展开。
Blanning's four parts (Life & Death, Power, Religion & Culture, War & Peace) really decompose 'the making of modern Europe' into four co-evolving curves: bodies, politics, culture, international order. On a full read 'glory' is not rhetoric but a traceable social capital — it explains Louis XIV's Versailles etiquette, Mozart's public concerts, Frederick the Great's philosopher-king image, and Napoleon's Légion d'honneur at once. The 'five revolutions' frame actually appears three times in the book (scientific, political, cultural); agricultural and industrial revolutions are compressed. The most complete stretch is Part IV (War and Peace 1780-1815): Revolutionary Wars, Napoleonic Wars, and Vienna treated as three phases of a single crisis of glory.

『荣耀』作为可分析的资本形式Glory as analyzable capital

Blanning 的招牌概念，通读之后可以确认他不是把它当作心态史的氛围词，而是当作 Bourdieu 意义上的社会资本 —— 可积累、可兑换、可挥霍。这让他能把宫廷礼仪、音乐产业、外交荣誉、战场声誉放在同一分析框架里。
Glory is not a mentalités mood-word but Bourdieusian social capital — accumulable, convertible, squanderable. Court etiquette, music industry, diplomatic honours, battlefield reputation end up in the same frame.

第三部『宗教与文化』是全书最原创部分Part III is the book's original core

第四部的战争-和平是标准政治史；第二部的绝对主义是标准制度史；只有第三部（De-Christianization + 音乐与品味 + 公共领域）真正把 Blanning 与同时代史家区别开。他的音乐史背景在这里充分发挥——莫扎特与海顿章节的密度甚至超过伏尔泰与狄德罗章节。
Part IV is political history, Part II institutional history; only Part III (de-Christianisation, music and taste, public sphere) actually distinguishes Blanning from contemporaries. His music-history background shows — the Mozart/Haydn chapters are denser than the Voltaire/Diderot ones.

与 Habermas 的隐性对话Implicit dialogue with Habermas

『公共领域的兴起』这一 motif 在 Ch.6-8 反复出现 —— Blanning 采纳哈贝马斯《公共领域的结构转型》的核心命题，但把它的时代从 18 世纪晚期上推到 1680 年前后。这是他对哈贝马斯命题的历史学改写。
'Rise of the public sphere' recurs Chs.6-8 — Blanning adopts Habermas' Structural Transformation but shifts its date from late-18th c. back to c.1680. A historian's revision of Habermas.

『De-Christianization』命题的争议性The contested de-Christianisation thesis

第三部第二章沿用法国史学 (Vovelle, Boutry) 的『去基督教化』命题 —— 认为 1750-1850 法国-比利时-莱茵兰的教友实践率显著下降。这一命题在 2002 年本书出版时是主流；但 2010 年代后被 Michael Löwy, John Bossy, Hugh McLeod 等重新质疑，通读时应把这个争议挂起来看。
Part III Ch.2 adopts Vovelle/Boutry's de-Christianisation thesis (1750-1850 decline in French/Belgian/Rhenish practice). Mainstream in 2002; contested since by Löwy, Bossy, McLeod — read this chapter knowing the later debate.

全书精读评析 · Full-read critical analysisFull-read critical analysis

通读后的整体判断：Blanning 本书是他自己在 1986-2002 十七年学术积累的收束 —— 每一部分都能在他之前的专著里找到源头 (Joseph II 1986 对应 Part II；French Revolutionary Wars 1989 对应 Part IV；Culture of Power and Power of Culture 2002 直接是 Part III 的扩展)。全书最原创的贡献是把『荣耀』(glory) 从心态史的氛围词改造为一种可追踪的社会资本 —— 让音乐、外交、宫廷、战争、艺术、沙龙进入同一分析框架。局限：(1) 『五场革命』的框架在书内实际只用三条 —— 农业革命与工业革命被压缩到 Ch.1 与 Ch.2 的一节内；(2) 对东欧 (波兰、俄罗斯、匈牙利) 的处理相对薄弱 —— 除彼得大帝的西化外，本书基本是西欧-中欧史；(3) 1789 之前的法国部分与 1789 之后的革命部分之间有一道方法论裂缝 —— Blanning 在旧制度部分用文化-社会史方法，在革命部分退回政治-军事史。
**Overall verdict after a full read**: Blanning's book is the summing of his own 1986-2002 research: every Part has a monograph source (Joseph II 1986 → Part II; French Revolutionary Wars 1989 → Part IV; Culture of Power 2002 → Part III). His most original contribution is transforming 'glory' from a mentalités mood-word into a traceable form of social capital — music, diplomacy, court, war, art, salon in one frame. Limitations: (1) the five-revolutions frame really uses only three in-text — agricultural and industrial revolutions compressed to sub-sections of Chs.1-2; (2) Eastern Europe (Poland, Russia, Hungary) under-covered — apart from Peter the Great's Westernisation, the book is West-Central European; (3) a methodological break between pre-1789 France and post-1789 Revolution — cultural-social history in the ancien régime sections, political-military in the revolutionary sections.
