背景Background

Appadurai 的全球化理论代表作，提出「景观」与「想象的共同体」在全球化时代的新形态。 《Modernity at Large》1996 年由 University of Minnesota Press 出版（248 页，ISBN 9780816627936），是全球化文化研究的经典。Appadurai 提出五种「景观」，并强调「想象」是一种社会实践。 它也可以被读成一次「想象的社会学」：想象不是私人幻想，而是被媒体、迁徙与消费组织起来的社会实践；正因如此，地方性才能在全球流动中被生产。
Appadurai's key work on globalization, proposing scapes and rethinking the imagined community under global flows. Modernity at Large (University of Minnesota Press, 1996; 248 pp., ISBN 9780816627936) is a classic of cultural globalization studies. Appadurai proposes five scapes and treats imagination as a social practice. It can also be read as a sociology of imagination: imagination is not private fantasy but a social practice organised by media, migration and consumption; that is how locality is produced within global flows.

Appadurai 提出五种「景观」（族群、媒体、技术、金融、意识形态），它们的流动速度与方向彼此不同，因此全球化产生的是差异而非同质。

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

流动中的地方：读《大现代性》The local within flows

全球化不是同质化：五种「景观」在生产地方差异。Globalization is not homogenisation: five scapes produce local difference.

《Modernity at Large》提出五种「景观」：族群、媒体、技术、金融、意识形态。它们跨边界流动，又在地方被重新组合；因此全球化不是同质化，而是差异的生产。

它的力量在于给「全球/地方」一个可分析的中介层；它的风险也在这里：景观可能过于抽象，经验 grounding 不足。

读它时最好把每一种景观落到一个具体案例：食物、媒体或城市空间，看它如何被本地化。五种景观不是五个盒子，而是五条同时流动的线；真正的分析，是看它们如何在同一个地方打结。

最强反读：五种景观可能只是分类框架，未必能解释具体的地方差异；若如此，本书是「全球化的语法」，而不是「全球化的解释」。

若把五种景观当作五个盒子，会把流动切成静态分类；Appadurai 的重点是五条线同时流动、在同一个地方打结。分析的任务，是看它们如何互相缠绕。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Modernity at Large proposes five scapes: ethnic, media, techno, finance and ideo. They flow across borders and are recombined locally, so globalization is not homogenisation but the production of difference.

Its power is to give the global/local an analysable middle layer; its risk is that scapes may be too abstract and empirically under-grounded.

Land each scape in a concrete case — food, media, urban space — and watch how it is localised. The five scapes are not five boxes but five lines flowing at once; analysis watches how they knot in one place.

Strongest counter-reading: the five scapes may be a classification rather than an explanation of concrete local difference; on that reading it is a grammar of globalization, not an explanation.

Treat the five scapes as five boxes and flow becomes static classification; Appadurai's point is five lines flowing at once, knotting in one place. Analysis watches their entanglement.
