背景Background

Cobb 在学校与大学教过大量初学者，并在日本棋院接受过八周教学训练；本书据此设计，从「先吃子版」自然过渡到正式围棋。书附 9×9 与 13×13 棋盘及塑料棋子。目录自 Introduction 至 Study Problems，共 12 章加练习与索引。
Cobb taught hundreds of beginners in schools and colleges and trained in Tokyo for eight weeks with the Nihon Kiin; the book grows from First-Capture Go into regular Go. It includes 9×9 and 13×13 sets with plastic stones. Its 12 chapters run from Introduction to Go and the Internet, plus Study Problems and an index.

结构Structure

教学递进式：先降低规则复杂度（只吃子），再逐步引入连接、死活、战略与战术；每章以「概念 + 示例」推进，末附练习。它不讨论佛教，也不假装是哲学书。
A graded teaching sequence: reduce the rules first (capture-only), then add connections, life and death, strategy and tactics; each chapter proceeds by concept plus example, with exercises at the end. It does not discuss Buddhism and does not pretend to be philosophy.

推断 · The claimThe claim

Cobb 要证明：初学者的真正瓶颈是「规则复杂度」，不是「计算」；因此他把围棋拆成「先吃子版」再到完整围棋的阶梯，让入门者在几十分钟内就能下第一盘。

Cobb wants to show that the beginner's real bottleneck is rule complexity, not calculation; he therefore builds a ladder from First-Capture Go to full Go so a newcomer can play a first game within minutes.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

目录以「First-Capture Go」为第二章，而不是直接讲完整规则；随后才进入「Playing Regular Go」「Capturing」「Connections」「Eyes and Life」；书附 9×9 与 13×13 棋具，说明教学设计指向「立刻能下」而不是「先学完再下」。Cobb 在学校与大学教过大量初学者，这一设计有教学经验基础。

The contents put First-Capture Go in chapter two instead of starting with full rules, then move to regular Go, capturing, connections and life; the included 9x9 and 13x13 sets point to 'play at once' rather than 'learn everything first'. Cobb taught many beginners in schools and colleges, grounding the design in teaching experience.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

先学会吃子：Cobb 的入门教学法Learn to capture first

Cobb 的入门书把「规则复杂度」当作初学者的真正瓶颈，因此先教「先吃子版」，再过渡到完整围棋。Cobb's primer treats rule complexity as the beginner's real bottleneck, teaching First-Capture Go before full Go.

《The Book of Go》是 Cobb 的入门教材，也是理解他「技术教师」一面的关键。它的设计很激进：第二章不是完整规则，而是「先吃子版围棋」——先只教吃子，把规则复杂度降到最低，再在后续章节引入连接、眼与活、战略、战术与受子。

这套设计的假设是：初学者的瓶颈不是计算，而是规则太多。书附 9×9 与 13×13 棋具，目录以棋谚与练习收束，说明它追求的是「立刻能下、可记忆、可练习」。Cobb 在学校与大学教过大量初学者，这一设计有教学经验基础。

它的局限也在这里：作为入门书，它不处理高级战略与全局判断；而「规则复杂度是瓶颈」本身是一个可争议的教学假设。读它时不妨把它当一次教学实验：先吃子版是否真的让初学者更快进入完整围棋？如果是，它的价值就超出「又一本入门书」。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Book of Go is Cobb's primer and the key to his 'teacher' side. Its design is radical: chapter two is not the full rules but First-Capture Go, which teaches capture only and lowers rule complexity, before later chapters introduce connections, eyes and life, strategy, tactics and handicap.

The assumption is that the beginner's bottleneck is not calculation but too many rules. The included 9x9 and 13x13 sets and the closing chapters on proverbs and problems show a pedagogy of 'play at once, remember, practise'. Cobb taught many beginners in schools and colleges, so the design has an experiential basis.

Its limit is the same: as a primer it does not treat advanced strategy or whole-board judgment, and 'rule complexity is the bottleneck' is itself contestable. Read it as a teaching experiment: does First-Capture Go really move beginners faster into full Go? If so, its value exceeds that of another primer.
