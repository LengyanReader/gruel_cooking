为何此刻Why now

全篇美学自述的收束点，「温柔哀感」在二十世纪的最后回响。川端不讲理论，只把几首和歌、几处庭园、几只茶碗摆在一起，让美自己说话。
The closing self-portrait, and the twentieth century's last echo of tender grief. Kawabata offers no theory: a few waka, a few gardens, a few tea bowls, and beauty speaks for itself.

背景Background

1968 年 12 月 12 日，川端康成在斯德哥尔摩以日语发表诺贝尔文学奖纪念讲演《美しい日本の私――その序説》，英译本题为 Japan, the Beautiful and Myself。讲演由道元禅师的和歌「春は花 夏ほととぎす 秋は月 冬雪さえて すずしかりけり」开篇，继而说明惠、良宽、一休、亲鸾，论枯山水、茶道、花道与俳谐，把日本之美归为对空无与无常的亲近。中译有河北教育出版社 2002 年叶渭渠译《我在美丽的日本》（「东瀛美文之旅」丛书），另有版本题作《美丽的日本与我》。
On 12 December 1968 Kawabata delivered his Nobel lecture in Japanese at Stockholm under the title Utsukushii Nihon no Watashi: sono josetsu, translated as Japan, the Beautiful and Myself. It opens with Dogen's waka, 'In spring, flowers; in summer, the cuckoo; in autumn, the moon; in winter, snow, clear and cool', then moves through Myoe, Ryokan, Ikkyu and Shinran, treating dry gardens, tea, flower arrangement and haiku, and tracing Japanese beauty to an intimacy with emptiness and impermanence. Ye Weiqu's Chinese translation appeared from Hebei Education in 2002 as Japan the Beautiful and Myself; some editions use the title The Beautiful Japan and I.

结构Structure

讲演没有论证链条，而以「引诗—解诗—转入下一物」的方式推进。每一段都从一句古典和歌起，落到一个具体的器物或场所，再滑向无常之感。最后回到「我」：一个站在空无前面的日本人。
The lecture has no chain of argument; it proceeds by quoting a poem, glossing it, and moving to the next object. Each passage starts from a classical waka, lands on a vessel or a place, and slides toward impermanence. It ends on the 'I': a Japanese standing before emptiness.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

诺奖讲演的经典文本，以极简的物象说尽日本美学的核心；以日语讲演本身即是文化立场的声明，中译本长期是进入川端美学的第一扇门。 把日本美写成连续的、无历史的传统，略去近代断裂；「空无」的概括也被后来的研究者视为过度统一。 **合成 Synthesis：** 与《茶之书》对读，看外宣与自述的差别；与《日本侘寂》对读，看「空无」如何被概念化。
A canonical Nobel text, saying the core of Japanese aesthetics through minimal objects; delivering it in Japanese was itself a cultural stance, and the Chinese translation has long been the first door into Kawabata's aesthetics. Japanese beauty is written as a continuous, unhistorical tradition with modern ruptures omitted; the summary of emptiness has been seen by later scholars as over-unified. **Synthesis:** Read with The Book of Tea for export against self-portrait; with Japanese Wabi-Sabi for how emptiness becomes a concept.

手法与亮点 · CraftCraft & highlights

以诗证美：不作定义，以和歌与物象代替论证。

Poetry as proof: no definitions, waka and objects in place of argument.

连缀式结构：一段一物，物与物之间靠气息相连。

Linked structure: one object per passage, joined by atmosphere rather than logic.

日语讲演：以本国语言的节奏面对世界，本身就是一次立场声明。

Delivered in Japanese: addressing the world in the language's own rhythm is itself a stance.

先读开篇与结尾，抓住「四季」与「空无」两端，再读中间的禅僧与庭园。
Read the opening and close for seasons and emptiness, then the monks and gardens between.
