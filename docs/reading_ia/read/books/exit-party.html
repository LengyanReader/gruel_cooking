在美国自身极化的当下，读一部把「两个美国」写成一确二真的小说——现实已经在分叉，小说只是把它显影。
In a moment of American fracturing, a novel that writes "two Americas" as one-true-two — reality is already forking; fiction only develops the film.

背景 · Background背景 · Background

曼德尔 2026 年的第七部长篇，也是她最政治化的作品。2031 年一场政变后美国分崩离析为互战的共和国、城邦与民兵地盘（A 面，混乱）；同书并置一个从未分裂、却收缩为铁腕监控国家的「美利坚合众国」（B 面，极权）。两个美国由一场洛杉矶派对事故与一幅 1968 年的画作《天鹅潜水》（Swan Dive）相连。A 面的混乱与 B 面的秩序互为「国家的反生活」，也是作者对「半数选民在基本事实上都不再一致」的当下焦虑的直接投影。曼德尔自称「最政治、最酷儿、最好的小说」。
Mandel's seventh novel and her most openly political. After a 2031 coup the U.S. splinters into warring republics/city-states and militia zones (A-Side, chaos); a parallel America never fragments but constricts into a surveillance state run by the "Service" (B-Side). The two are bridged by a party accident and a 1968 painting, "Swan Dive." Chaos and totalitarianism function as a "dark twinship" — the counterlife of the state — mirroring Mandel's anxiety that "half the electorate disagrees on basic reality." She calls it "my most political work, my most queer work, I think it's my best novel."

第 1 幕 · 派对之夜Act 1 · The Party

2031 年春——美国政变崩解后的「第一个春天」。国际维和部队撤离洛杉矶，宵禁随《萨克拉门托协定》解除，加州共和国成立。48 岁的阿里·韦克刚出狱（18 年刑期，雇凶杀害表兄安东）。她与室友格洛丽亚穿过棚户区、红十字帐篷与炸弹嗅探犬去银湖参加卡里姆·法耶德的派对。派对上「出了毛病」：塞拉亚的第二个版本（穿便装的困惑女人）现身又逃入夜色；自称尼科的「第二个格洛丽亚」闯入厨房；主人卡里姆在一间没有外门的厨房里凭空消失。格洛丽亚取下画作《天鹅潜水》带画赴欧洲（她怀着卡里姆的孩子）。事后有人对阿里说：「我想，那场派对也是一扇门。」
In the first spring after the collapse of the United States, peacekeepers withdraw from LA, the curfew is lifted (Sacramento Accord), the Republic of California is declared. Ari Waker, 48, is newly released after 18 years for hiring a hit on her cousin. She walks with roommate Gloria to Kareem Fayed's Silverlake party past tent cities and bomb-sniffing dogs. At the flower-filled party "there's something wrong": a second, casually dressed Saraya appears and flees; a woman entering the kitchen; the host Kareem vanishes from a doorless kitchen. Gloria takes the painting and flees to Europe, pregnant. "I think the party was also a door."

第 2 幕 · A 面漂流Act 2 · A-Side Picaresque

阿里在崩解的 A 面世界漂泊求生：洛杉矶街头当洗碗工/零售店员；东迁俄亥俄共和国，为饯行组织做杂役、替民兵头目爱德华多跟踪尼科；与曾是芭蕾舞者的军火商费利克斯结伴南下得克萨斯，再逃往欧洲（巴黎圆顶孤城、希腊军火商据点）。她后来甩掉费利克斯，与雇佣兵比尔·索里亚诺结成交易式联盟，最终化名巴黎艺术藏家「阿里·索里亚诺」。她天生的预感（clairvoyance）时灵时不灵。核心台词：「若没有人见证，你的过去就开始像虚构。」
Ari drifts through the broken A-Side world: dishwashing/retail in LA, militia work in the Ohio Republic (surveilling Nico for Eduardo), a partnership with Felix (a former ballet dancer turned gunrunner), flight through Texas to Europe (domed Paris, a Greek militia hangout), a transactional alliance with mercenary Bill Soriano, and finally a life as Paris art collector "Ari Soriano." Her premonitions are unreliable. Motto-line: "If you don't have a witness, your past starts to feel fictional."

第 3 幕 · B 面之墙Act 3 · The Service & the Experiment

B 面：合众国没有崩解，却成了极权国家——「Service」（内部安全局）当街掳人，劳动营供「批评政府者」改造；投票不再必需，总统之位预定由他儿子接任。UCLA 四名物理学家——尼科·李、丈夫卡里姆·法耶德、塞拉亚·德·桑托斯与同事莱恩——深夜秘密做传送实验：先用《天鹅潜水》试机（完美传送），随后卡里姆踏上平台，被复制成两个——B 面出现两个卡里姆，均被 Service 羁押；同时 A 面派对上的卡里姆在厨房消失。阿里的 B 面对应体是资深探员伊巴里·韦克：公开职业是城市规划讲师；鼻梁上一副银胶带修补的眼镜可骗过人脸识别；她自称「我一生都带着一点点直觉」。她用假名「大卫·格里森」（《歌者之枪》旧角色）救出 A 面卡里姆。
B-Side America stayed whole — and constricted: the "Service" yanks people into unmarked cars, labor camps reform critics, voting is no longer required. Four UCLA physicists (Nico Li, her husband Kareem Fayed, Saraya de Santos, colleague Len) secretly run a teleportation experiment; "Swan Dive" transmits perfectly, then Kareem steps on the platform and is duplicated — two Kareems, both detained. Ari's parallel is Ibari Waker, senior Service detective whose cover is urban-planning professor; her silver-taped glasses defeat facial recognition. She frees the A-side Kareem under the alias "David Grissom" (a Singer's Gun character).

第 4 幕 · 汇流Act 4 · Convergence (Paris, 2060s)

故事跨越数十年。巴黎在对世界其余部分关闭的圆顶内成为相对安稳的孤岛（约 2065 年前后）。格洛丽亚是不尽责的母亲；尼科成为塞巴斯蒂安（格洛丽亚与 A 面卡里姆之子）的代理母亲；《天鹅潜水》经手多人。塞巴斯蒂安 20 岁决定把画从年迈藏家阿里·索里亚诺处「偷回」，作为给尼科的生日礼物；阿里在巴黎文森森林追逐画作时被劫匪重创。B 面：获释的卡里姆患癌临终，成年的萨米尔（月面殖民地建筑师）与马洛（传送物理学家、患有「时间线损坏」式的复杂 PTSD）守于床边；另一个卡里姆翻窗而入与他道别。动身退休的伊巴里（79 岁）行使「火化权」销毁追踪编码、下载国家机密档案交给抵抗组织，随后独自走进太平洋。
The novel arcs across decades into a domed-Paris refuge (circa 2065) sealed off from the broken world. Gloria is a neglectful mother; Nico is a surrogate mother to Sebastian (Gloria's son by the party Kareem). Sebastian, at 20, decides to steal "Swan Dive" back from elderly collector Ari Soriano as a birthday present for Nico; Ari is badly hurt chasing him through the Bois de Vincennes. B-Side: the freed Kareem dies of cancer with grown Samir (architect on the lunar colony) and Marlowe (a transport physicist wracked by complex PTSD — "timeline corruption") at his bedside; the other Kareem climbs in a window to say goodbye. Retiring Ibari (79) uses her "right of incineration," destroys her trackers, copies the State's files to the Resistance, then walks into the Pacific surf.

第 5 幕 · 余烬与希望Act 5 · Embers & Hope

尾声不以乌托邦收束，而以「一丝希望的微光」收束：艺术的长寿与「短暂即甜蜜」互为支撑；「如果世上没有见证者，你的过去就开始像虚构」一语回收。曼德尔强调这是「希望之书」，是疫情后纽约那个奇迹般盛夏的「desperate joy」所驱动。
The ending declines utopia for "a little glimmer of hope": art outliving makers and countries is the governing theme; "temporary brings its own kind of sweetness" recurs. Mandel insists this is a hopeful book born of the "desperate joy" of New York's magical pandemic-era summer.

叙事结构 · Narrative structureNarrative structure

结构 · Structure结构 · Structure

全书三部分——A-Side（A 面/混乱宇宙）、B-Side（B 面/极权宇宙）、Swan Dive（《天鹅潜水》：近卷末的短章，交代画作 1968 年在纽约公寓的缘起）。整体第三人称叙述，部分段落转为现在时；章节内嵌标题小节（如「The Birthday Present」「True Diligence」）；含审讯转录稿——其中一段是「只听一方」的独角转录，书评人指为炫技。双镜结构：A/B 两面的对应人物互为「反生活」，以跨世界重复的句子与意象缝合全书（「我想跳进海里，一路游过那片蓝」「真正的勤奋=追随不可能之路」「PTSD 是时间线损坏」）。机制几乎不给解释——可穿点疑似为「同时存在于两个宇宙的物体」（画作），而书内尼科自嘲「我觉得那幅画是个障眼法」。
Three parts — A-Side, B-Side, and a late, short "Swan Dive" section on the painting's 1968 New York origins. Third person throughout (present tense in stretches); embedded titled micro-chapters; an interrogation transcript, plus a famously one-sided "transcript" chapter that critics call showy. The doubled worlds braid via mirror-selves and repeated lines/images. The physics is deliberately unexplained — "a notion that objects might be where parallel universes converge" — undercut in-text by Nico: "I think the painting's a red herring."

An idea that I've been fascinated with for a long time is this notion of the counterlife, the life you didn't live.
「反生活」（counterlife）这个念头我着迷已久——那些你未曾活过的生活。

It's interesting to think about the counterlife of the individual, but it's also interesting to think about the counterlife of the state.

评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis

Kirkus——「自《大站十一》以来最好的作品……把平行时间线的概念天才地接上政治主题」；Booklist、Library Journal 星评；Publishers Weekly ★「tour de force」。NPR 的 Corrigan 说「派对值得等待」；New Yorker 的 Waldman 自称「一口读完」；Boston Globe「曼德尔最好的状态」；Saturday Paper「精彩、完全笃定」。 反面/保留：Irish Examiner ——情节「unwieldy（笨重）」、结尾「陷入混乱」；Goodreads 1.5★ 博客则骂前提拉仲；BookAndFilmGlobe「圆满收束但非解决」；New Yorker 亦点出主角群「each seem to represent a different ethnic group」的危险——近乎零风险的讨好式演员名单。文学评论（Literary Review of Canada, 2026-10）正文未取到（○）。 **合成 Synthesis：** 作者明确把书写成「关于余波而非灾难」的希望之书，同时它是警世书：监控调性之外，「当半国选民不信基本事实」才是她笔下两个世界共同的原初创伤。主流书评盛赞居多；质疑集中在结构炫技与中段巴黎的拖沓。
Kirkus — "best since Station Eleven… brilliantly ties parallel timelines to a political theme"; Booklist & Library Journal starred; PW ★ "tour de force." NPR's Corrigan: the party "is worth the wait"; The New Yorker's Waldman "gulped it down"; Boston Globe: "Mandel at her best." Dissent: Irish Examiner calls the plot "unwieldy" and the climax "descends into confusion"; a 1.5★ Goodreads blog resents stretched premises; BookAndFilmGlobe: "satisfying closure but not resolution." The New Yorker flags the "attractive prodigies" who "each seem to represent a different ethnic group." Literary Review of Canada, 2026-10 unretrieved (○). **Synthesis:** Mandel frames the book as about aftermaths, not disasters, while warning that fractured consensus reality is the root trauma of both worlds. Mainstream reception is largely rapturous (Kirkus: "best since Station Eleven"), with dissent on pacing and the showy transcripts.

手法与亮点 · CraftCraft & highlights

画作作纽带——一幅《天鹅潜水》串起 A/B 两面与十余年幕：艺术是「最古老的时间旅行器」，延续《大站十一》的漫画即契约母题。

The painting as thread — "Swan Dive" ties A/B sides across decades; art is "the oldest time machine," continuing Station Eleven's comics-as-covenant motif.

A/B=唱片 AB 面——双面结构取黑胶语义，「无须对称」；B 面机械的和谐反写 A 面随机的枪火。

A/B updates an LP's sides — "you don't demand symmetry"; B-side's mechanical harmony inverts A-side's random fire.

重复句缝合世界——跨世界重复的句子制造「既视感的惊悚」（"I want to dive into the ocean and keep swimming through the blue"; "True diligence means following the improbable path"）。

Repeated sentences across worlds create déjà-vu dread, reading the two worlds as one coin.

零科学软科幻——机制从不解释：「如果书里有时间机器，我不会告诉你怎么运作」，重点在人。

"If there's a time machine in my book, I'm not going to tell you how it works" — the physics stays unexplainable.

PTSD=时间线损坏——用科幻概念映射日常心理损伤，是全书的微缩模型。

"PTSD is timeline corruption" compresses the novel's theme into an aphorism.

酷儿与照护——与早期作品相比，「做母亲」上升为核心情感（女儿 2015 年出生后她自认才懂某些细微差别）；阿里的双性恋被无戏剧化地正面写出。

Parenthood became a core emotional register after her daughter's 2015 birth; Ari's bisexuality is stated without drama.

派对即门 · 双关「party」party as door / doorway and faction
I think the party was also a door.
全书钥匙母题：派对既是字面的门（无外门的厨房），也是宇宙论的门（物体是平行世界相交之处）；「party」兼指社交聚会与政治党派——一个词承载两重灾难（国家碎裂 vs 极权收束）。开场的洛杉矶夜景里，「一身银裙、褶如锡箔」的女子在派对上出现为一重分裂的预兆。
The book's hinge-motif: the party is a literal door (the kitchen with no door) and a cosmological one (objects as the convergences of parallel universes); "party" doubles as social gathering and political faction — one word, two collapses.
npr.org · emilymandel.com/exit-party · ✓
天鹅潜水 · 画作纽带Swan Dive — the painting as thread
The painting, "Swan Dive," first appeared in The Glass Hotel (2019), and is central to the plot here.
跨作品之物：《天鹅潜水》早在《玻璃旅馆》（2019）出场，在此成为情节枢纽——既是私人物品史，也印证「艺术比承载它的人与国更长寿」；作者称艺术是「最古老的时间旅行器」，延续《大站十一》的漫画即契约母题（结构无损层的 cross-text object）。
A cross-book object: "Swan Dive" first appeared in The Glass Hotel (2019) and is central here — a micro-history of art outliving people and nations, the "oldest time machine," continuing Station Eleven's art-as-covenant motif.

蓝花楹 · 灾后春天jacaranda — beauty on its own calendar
In the first spring after the collapse of the United States, the blossoming of the jacaranda trees felt to Ari like grace.
开篇第一句的景象母题：被战争、宵禁与维和部队撤军标记的「第一个春天」，由一棵南美外来树的气味打开——灾难下的美不来自国家，而来自仍按时开花的东西。
The opening-image motif: the "first spring" after the collapse is opened by a smell, not by the state — a South American import that keeps to its own calendar.

国家的反生活 · 两版美国counterlife of the state — two versions of the country
There was a world where my homeland had fractured into disarray, and another where my homeland had closed like a fist.
比喻由人滑向国家：不只个人有分身的「反生活」，国家也有——一个世界「碎裂成一片狼藉」，另一个「收缩成一只拳头」；无序的散开与压抑的收束，两极同源。
The simile slides from persons to the country: one world "fractured into disarray," the other "closed like a fist" — a civilization's dystopia and a police state's, shaped by the same fear.
kirkusreviews.com · npr.org · ✓
和谐不惜代价 · 监控即服务harmony at any cost — surveillance as Service
The opposite of disorder is harmony. Peace is achieved through surveillance. Service is the surveillance department of the State. Harmony at any cost.
B 面极权的排比宣言：「混乱的对立面是和谐；和平靠监控达成；服务局即国家的监控部门；不惜代价的和谐」。把监控委婉命名为「Service」，秩序被定义为混乱的对立面——而非混沌与自由。
The B-side manifesto in tetracolon: order as the opposite of disorder, not of chaos and freedom — surveillance rebranded as "Service," harmony at any cost.

见证公信 · 无证即虚构witness — without a witness, the past turns fictional
If you don't have a witness, your past starts to feel fictional.
全书母题最凝缩的一句（也是本仓「witness-aesthetics」术语的源头）：没有见证者，记忆就失去公信力——个人如此，国家亦然，史学即见证。
The tersest statement of the book's core concern: without a witness the past loses all standing — true of persons, true of countries. History is testimony, or it is fiction.

精读建议 · Deep readingDeep reading

把 A/B 两面当作「同一焦虑的两种结局」来读，而非平行世界解谜：逐句捕捉跨面的镜像句与镜像人物（Ari/Ibari、格洛丽亚/尼科、双卡里姆、萨米尔/马洛、费利克斯/爱德华多）。
Read A/B as twin outcomes of one anxiety, tracking mirror-sentences and mirror-selves (Ari/Ibari, Gloria/Nico, the two Kareems, Samir/Marlowe) rather than solving the portal.
逐一到路追踪《天鹅潜水》的每一次转手（派对墙→格洛丽亚→塞巴斯蒂安→尼科→伊巴里）：这是一部私人物品史，也印证「艺术比承载它的人与国更长寿」的主题。
Map every hand-off of "Swan Dive" — a micro-history of art outliving people and nations, Mandel's central thesis.
注意文体异色：审讯转录、独角转录、1968 年画作前史。作者坦承在「神秘但不令人抓狂地困惑」上走钢丝；The Leftovers（剧集）提供氛围参照。
Watch the formal oddities (interrogation/one-sided transcripts, the 1968 origin chapter) against her stated tightrope — "mysterious, but not maddeningly confusing" — with The Leftovers as tonal precedent.
预兆的「失灵」是自嘲的传记性投射（「我绝对无法预测大流行病」）——别把超自然当答案，答案在「见证」与照护里。
Treat Ari's premonitions as wry self-portrait ("I absolutely cannot predict pandemics") — the answer is witness and care, not psi.
