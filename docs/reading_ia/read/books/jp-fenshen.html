为何此刻Why now

整套特辑里唯一由中国人写的「论日本」，也是把其余七册收拢成一个问题的抓手：我们读日本，究竟在读什么。李永晶的答案不是「日本是什么」，而是「我们为何总借日本谈论自己」——分身。
The only China-authored Japan-ology in the set, and the hinge that gathers the other seven volumes into one question: what are we reading when we read Japan? Li's answer is not what Japan is, but why we keep speaking of ourselves through Japan. He calls that figure the avatar.

背景Background

李永晶（华东师范大学政治学系副教授）把近代中国的日本认识重读为一条「以他者界定自身」的镜像链：王韬、黄遵宪的使日记载，戴季陶《日本论》，战后的日本热与厌日交替。每一次对日本的描述，同时是一次自我定位。书分三层推进：先立「分身」的方法，再沿近代精神史追溯日本如何由「精神帝国」走向「政治帝国」，最后回到中国自身的认识论。
Li Yongjing (associate professor of political science, East China Normal University) rereads modern China's knowledge of Japan as a mirror-chain of defining the self through the other: Wang Tao and Huang Zunxian's mission accounts, Dai Jitao's Japan-ology, the post-war alternation of Japan-fever and aversion. The book moves in three stages: the method of the avatar, the spiritual history by which Japan went from a 'spiritual empire' to a 'political empire', and a return to China's own epistemology.

论述框架 · Analytical frameworkAnalytical framework

结构Structure

写法上是「论」而非「史」：以问题带材料，用日本近代思想史做中国的镜子。全书的关键动作是把「知日」从对象认识改写成自我认识，因此黄遵宪、戴季陶、本尼迪克特都不是被评价的对象，而是镜链上的节点。
It is an argument rather than a history: the question carries the material, and Japan's modern intellectual history serves as China's mirror. Its decisive move is to rewrite 'knowing Japan' from knowledge of an object into knowledge of the self; Huang, Dai and Benedict thus become nodes in a mirror-chain rather than objects of appraisal.

方法与材料 · Method & sourcesMethod & sources

全书由引子、绪论、七章、结语、尾声构成。七章不作编年，而以七个精神主题为纲：尊王攘夷、竞逐亚洲、超克西方、世界革命、民主主义、日本宪法、明治维新。每一章处理一次「日本如何定位自己」的危机，再把这次危机读成中国的一面镜子。

材料以中文日本论述为主：使日记载、政论、战后评论与研究著作；日文一手档案使用有限。作者自述做的是「精神史」，但就证据结构而言，它更接近「接受史」：研究对象与其说是日本，不如说是中国关于日本的想象。这个选择本身可以成立，问题在于书名与结论常以日本史的口吻说话。

方法上的另一个特点是「国家的精神分析」：把国家当作有欲望、有焦虑的行动者。这个类比有启发性，也有风险：国家不是 psyche，制度、阶级与国际结构不能都化约为精神动因。

The book runs: preface, introduction, seven chapters, conclusion, coda. The chapters are thematic rather than chronological: revere the emperor and expel the barbarians; competing in Asia; overcoming the West; world revolution; democracy; the constitution; the Meiji Restoration. Each handles a crisis of Japan's self-positioning and then reads that crisis as a mirror for China.

The material is chiefly Chinese writing on Japan: mission accounts, political tracts, post-war commentary and scholarship; Japanese archives are used sparingly. The author calls it a spiritual history, but in evidentiary terms it is closer to a reception history: the object is less Japan than China's imagination of Japan. That choice can be defended; the problem is that the title and conclusions often speak in the voice of Japanese history.

A second feature is the 'psychoanalysis of the state', which treats the state as an agent with desires and anxieties. The analogy is suggestive and risky: a state is not a psyche, and institutions, classes and international structures cannot all be reduced to spiritual motives.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

把「日本论」从国情介绍提升为认识论问题，是近年中文日本研究里少见有理论野心的一本；对黄遵宪—戴季陶—本尼迪克特这条线的重述，给学生一条可走的路径。 批评集中在两点：一是「分身」作为总钥匙，解释力被拉得过宽，几乎任何中日互视都可套入；二是 530 页的篇幅里，日本思想史的具体论证有时让位于框架的自我展开。 **合成 Synthesis：** 读它不要当日本史，要当「中国如何看日本」的方法论自省。与《日本国志》《日本论》《菊与刀》《杂种文化》并置，才看得出它回应的是谁。
It lifts 'Japan-ology' from country briefing to epistemology, a rare theoretical ambition in recent Chinese writing on Japan; its retelling of the Huang–Dai–Benedict line gives students a walkable path. Criticism centres on two points: the avatar as a master key is stretched so wide that almost any mutual looking fits; and across 530 pages the concrete argument sometimes yields to the frame's self-unfolding. **Synthesis:** Read it not as Japanese history but as a methodological self-examination of how China sees Japan. Place it against Huang, Dai, Benedict and Katō to see whom it answers.

推断 · The claimThe claim

李永晶真正想说的不是「日本是什么」，而是「近代中国为什么始终不能用自己的语言说自己」。日本在他笔下是一面借来的镜子：因为直接自述已经失效，只能通过描述一个最像自己、又最不像自己的邻人来完成自我定位。全书的落点因此不在日本史，而在中国的自我理解能力。

再往下一层，他想终结一种摆荡。近代中国的日本论在「学它」与「防它」之间来回，两端都把日本当作工具。李永晶要把这种摆荡本身变成研究对象：一旦看清摆荡的机制，中国就有可能不再需要在两极之间选边。书末的「新世界主义」是这个愿望的规范表达——不是对日本的态度，而是对世界位置的态度。

Li Yongjing's real subject is not what Japan is but why modern China has been unable to speak of itself in its own terms. Japan serves as a borrowed mirror: direct self-description having failed, self-location proceeds by describing a neighbour that is at once most like and most unlike the self. The book therefore lands on China's capacity for self-understanding rather than on Japanese history.

Beneath that lies a wish to end an oscillation. Chinese writing on Japan swings between learning from it and guarding against it, and both poles treat Japan as an instrument. Li turns the swing itself into the object of study: once its mechanism is visible, choosing a pole may cease to be necessary. The closing 'new cosmopolitanism' is the normative form of that wish, an attitude to the world rather than to Japan.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

第一，材料的分布暴露了对象。全书倚重中文的日本论述——使日记载、政论、战后评论——而极少使用日文一手档案。如果研究对象真是日本，这个材料结构无法成立；如果研究对象是中国关于日本的想象，它就恰好合适。作者自述做的是「精神史」，但就证据结构而言更接近接受史。这个错位不是疏漏，而是意图的痕迹：他要的本来就是中国人的精神脉络。

第二，章节的排法暴露了关切。七章不是编年，明治维新被放在最后。若以日本近代化为对象，起点应当在前；把起点当作终点来追问，说明他追的不是事件顺序，而是反复出现的自我定位方式。同样，战后没有单列一章，被分散进民主主义与宪法。分期不是他的问题，结构才是。

第三，书名的承继暴露了文体。《新日本论》直接接戴季陶的《日本论》。戴书一九二八年由上海民智书局初版，胡汉民作序，收二十四篇短文，本质是政党要人对党务的训诫：分析日本是为了规训中国。李永晶续的是这个文体，不是日本学的学术题目。续一个文体，就是续它的问题意识。

第四，结论的性质暴露了落点。「新世界主义」是规范主张，不是历史描述。一部以历史叙述为主的书，最后给出一个关于应当如何的主张，说明前面的叙述是为这个主张服务的。它想超越民族国家叙事，而全书的分析单位仍是民族国家——这个张力恰恰证明，作者真正焦虑的是中国在世界里的位置，不是日本在历史里的位置。

第五，接受史反过来印证了意图。推荐语强调「审视中国的另一个自我」，读者记住的却是「日本」。一本书被误读的方式，往往正对着它最想纠正的东西：他想让读者从日本身上收回目光，读者却继续盯着日本。

First, the distribution of sources betrays the object. The book leans on Chinese writing about Japan, mission accounts, tracts and post-war commentary, and rarely on Japanese primary archives. That structure cannot support a study of Japan; it fits a study of Chinese imagining of Japan exactly. Li calls it spiritual history, but the evidential shape is reception history. The mismatch is a trace of intent rather than an oversight.

Second, the ordering betrays the concern. The seven chapters are not chronological, and the Meiji Restoration comes last. A study of Japanese modernisation would begin there; placing the origin at the end shows that what is being traced is a recurring mode of self-location, not a sequence of events. The post-war period likewise has no chapter of its own and is distributed across democracy and the constitution. Periodisation is not the problem; structure is.

Third, the title betrays the genre. A New Discussion of Japan succeeds Dai Jitao's Discussion of Japan, published in 1928 with a preface by Hu Hanmin, twenty-four short pieces of party admonition: analysing Japan in order to discipline China. Li continues that genre, not an academic problem in Japanese studies, and continuing a genre means continuing its question.

Fourth, the conclusion betrays the destination. The new cosmopolitanism is a normative claim, not a historical description. A historical narrative that ends in an ought has been serving that ought. It wants to move beyond the nation-state while its unit of analysis remains the nation-state, and that tension shows the anxiety is China's place in the world.

Fifth, reception confirms it. The blurb stresses examining China's other self; readers remember Japan. The way a book is misread often faces exactly what it wanted to correct.

反读 · The strongest alternativeThe strongest alternative

最强的反读有三条。其一，取材偏中文可能只是条件所限：在中国写作、面向中国读者、以中文出版，日文档案的缺席未必反映对象的选择，只反映写作的位置。若如此，「精神史」就是诚实的自述，不是掩饰。

其二，章节的非编年排列可能只是论文结集的自然结果。七章各自成文，先写哪一篇、后写哪一篇，未必有整体设计；把明治维新置末读成「以起点为终点」，可能是读者替作者补上的对称。

其三，「新世界主义」也许只是尾章的例行升华。学术著作常在结尾给出一个规范展望，它未必承担全书的动机。用一个尾章反推整本书的意图，证据上是薄的。

这三条都不能被现有材料排除。它们合起来构成一个更节制的读法：这是一部以中国材料写的日本论述史，作者关心自我理解，但没有证据表明他有一个贯穿全书的规范计划。

Three strong objections. The Chinese-heavy sourcing may simply reflect conditions: writing in China, for Chinese readers, in Chinese. The absence of Japanese archives would then record a position of writing, not a choice of object, and 'spiritual history' would be an honest description.

The non-chronological ordering may be an artefact of collected essays. Seven free-standing chapters need not embody a design, and reading the Restoration's final place as 'the origin as end' may supply a symmetry the author never intended.

The new cosmopolitanism may be a routine closing gesture. Academic books often end with a normative outlook that carries no motivating weight, and inferring a whole intention from a final chapter is evidentially thin.

None of these can be excluded on present material. Together they support a more restrained reading: a history of Chinese writing on Japan by an author concerned with self-understanding, without evidence of a normative plan governing the whole.

推断的边界 · Limits of the inferenceLimits of the inference

可以说的是：这部书的对象、材料与文体都指向中国的自我理解，而不是日本的社会史；这一点由目录、征引结构与书名承继三重支持，把握较高。不能说的是：作者有一个自觉的、贯穿七章的规范计划，或「新世界主义」是全书的动机。后者只有尾章一处证据，且与全书分析单位冲突，更像是愿望而非设计。

还要划清一条界：推断作者意图与评价其成就无关。即使他只是写了一部中国日本论述史，这部书仍然成立；把意图读得比成就更大，反而是对书的抬举式误读。

What can be said: object, sources and genre all point to Chinese self-understanding rather than Japanese social history, supported by the contents, the citation structure and the title's lineage. What cannot: that the author held a conscious normative plan across all seven chapters, or that the new cosmopolitanism motivates the whole. That rests on one closing passage and conflicts with the book's unit of analysis; it looks like a wish rather than a design.

One further line: inferring intent is separate from judging achievement. Even if this is only a history of Chinese writing on Japan, the book stands. Reading the intention as larger than the achievement would be a flattering misreading.

争议与反证 · CritiqueCritique

局限与争议 · Limits & disputesLimits & disputes

第一重局限在对象。书名是《分身：新日本论》，读者自然期待一部关于日本的著作；实际的主体却是中国关于日本的论述。作为「中国日本认识史」，它成立；作为「新日本论」，它没有兑现。这个错位不是宣传问题，而是论证结构问题：当证据全部来自中国一侧，关于日本内部动因的判断就只能靠推论。

第二重局限在「分身」的适用性。镜像关系在解释中国时很有力：为什么中国的日本论总在学习与警惕之间摆荡，因为日本被当成可替换的自我形象。但它解释不了日本为何在某些时刻作出与中国无关的选择。把一切都读成「给中国看的」，会删掉日本的社会史、制度史与国际关系。

第三重局限在规范与描述的混淆。「新世界主义」是一个值得讨论的主张，但书中常把它写成从历史里读出的结论。读者需要自己分辨：哪一句是史料，哪一句是愿景。

The first limit is the object. The title promises a new theory of Japan; the actual subject is Chinese writing about Japan. As a history of Chinese knowledge of Japan it works; as a new theory of Japan it does not deliver. The mismatch is structural: when all evidence comes from the Chinese side, claims about Japan's internal motives can only be inferred.

The second limit is the avatar's reach. The mirror explains China powerfully: why Chinese Japan-writing oscillates between emulation and vigilance, because Japan serves as a replaceable self-image. It cannot explain why Japan sometimes chose courses unrelated to China. Reading everything as 'for China to see' deletes Japanese social, institutional and international history.

The third limit is the confusion of description and prescription. 'New worldism' is a proposal worth arguing, but the book often writes it as a conclusion read off history. The reader must separate the document from the vision.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

镜中的国族：读《分身》与「以日本认识中国」的限度The Nation in the Mirror: Fenshen and the Limits of Knowing China through Japan

一部以「分身」为总钥匙的近代日本精神史，野心在于把日本研究改写为中国自我认识；它的力量与风险，都来自这同一把钥匙。A spiritual history of modern Japan organised around the avatar: its ambition is to turn Japan studies into Chinese self-knowledge, and both its power and its risk come from that single key.

二〇二〇年一月，北京联合出版公司推出一部五百三十余页的著作，题作《分身：新日本论》。作者李永晶，华东师范大学政治学系副教授。书很厚，问题却可以一句话说完：我们读日本，究竟在读谁？

书的做法不是回答「日本是什么」，而是把中文世界的日本论述串成一条镜链。王韬、黄遵宪的使日记载，戴季陶的《日本论》，战后的日本热与厌日交替，都被放进同一条线索：每一次描述日本，同时是一次自我定位。这个转向很重要。它把日本研究从对象认识变成认识论自省，也把「知日」从情报工作变成自我理解的一部分。

「分身」与「镜像」并不完全相同。镜像强调反映，分身强调可替换：镜子里的形象不能替你行动，分身却可能替你行动，也可能脱离你。作者用「分身」而不用「镜像」，大约是想保留这层能动性：日本不只是被看，它也在近代东亚的历史里替中国演出了另一种可能。可惜全书对「替身」的这一面展开不够，多数时候仍停留在反映论。

「分身」因此不是比喻，而是方法。它要求读者把关于日本的事实读成关于中国的句子。黄遵宪写《日本国志》，既是地理志，也是改革方案；戴季陶写《日本论》，既分析日本，也训诫国民党。这些判断并不新鲜，新鲜的是把它们串成一条链，并宣称这条链就是近代中国精神史的一部分。

但书名是《新日本论》，不是《中国日本论述史》。读者会期待日本，得到的多半是中国人看日本。这个错位不是小事，它决定了全书的证据与结论。当材料全部来自中国一侧，关于日本内部动因的判断就只能靠推论；推论可以很精彩，却不能替代日本自己的社会史、制度史与国际关系。

七章的安排值得注意：尊王攘夷、竞逐亚洲、超克西方、世界革命、民主主义、日本宪法、明治维新。这不是编年。明治维新被放在最后，等于把近代日本的起点当作终点来追问：精神史的秘密不在事件顺序里，而在反复出现的自我定位方式里。尊王攘夷对应水户学与中华世界的投影，竞逐亚洲对应脱亚论与亚洲主义，超克西方对应近代的超克，世界革命对应日本马克思主义，民主主义对应大正民主，宪法对应明治国家与战后重编。每一章都是一次危机，每一次危机都被读成一面镜子。

七个主题串起来，其实是一条问题链：先有外部秩序的投影（尊王攘夷），再有区域竞争的野心（竞逐亚洲），然后是普遍主义的自我授权（超克西方），接着是左翼的世界革命想象，再退回国内的政治参与（民主主义），最后落在制度文本（宪法）与历史起源（明治维新）。这条链从外向内收，从想象落到制度，再从制度返回起源。值得注意的是，全书没有为战后单列一章；战后被分散在民主主义与宪法两章里。这个安排暗示作者的关切不在分期，而在结构：同一套自我定位方式，换了制度外壳仍在运行。

这个结构自有代价。战败被处理成一次制度重编，而不是一次断裂。就日本自身而言，一九四五年之后的问题不是「再定位一次」，而是「在占领条件下重造定位所依托的全部条件」：主权、宪法、天皇制、教育、经济。镜链能解释连续，解释不了断裂；能解释中国如何在日本身上找自己，解释不了日本如何在一九四五年之后被迫重新成为自己。这一章缺席，使全书的「精神史」在时间上完整，在经验上却留下一个空洞。

「分身」的力量在于解释中国。为什么中国的日本论总在两极之间摆荡，一时要学，一时要防？因为日本被当成可替换的自我形象：它可以是中国成功改革的样板，也可以是中国失败沦落的警告。同一个日本，承担两种投射。这个分析对理解近现代中国的焦虑很有说服力。

它的弱点在于解释日本。日本的政治选择并不都需要中国的镜子才能发生。明治宪法的设计、军部的抬头、战后民主化，各有其国内与国际条件。把一切都读成「给中国看的」，会删掉日本自身的历史。作者并非不知道这一点，但为了让「分身」贯穿全书，他倾向于把日本史写成中国认识史的注脚。

这不是说镜像分析无效。明治宪法的设计，确实有借普鲁士模式确认「非西方而能现代」的用意；战后民主化，也确实被中国反复当作参照与警戒。问题在于比例：镜像能解释选择中的一部分，不能解释全部。作者有时把「一部分」写成「全部」，论证就从分析滑成了断言。

「国家的精神分析」这个说法，也容易让人想起把国家拟人化的老毛病。国家没有童年，也没有潜意识；它有制度、利益集团与国际位置。作者用「精神」一词，实际指的是集体的自我理解。这层意思如果讲清楚，类比就站得住；讲不清，就会被读者当成把国际关系心理化。

书末提出的「新世界主义」是规范主张，不是描述。它希望超越民族国家叙事，建立一种新的世界意识。这个主张值得讨论，问题在于它与前面的历史叙述没有真正接上。书批评以民族为单位看历史，却又反复使用「日本精神」这样的整体词；它想走出民族国家，分析单位却仍是民族国家。这是全书最大的理论张力。

材料上的选择也值得说明。作者倚重中文日本论述，少用日文一手档案。他自述做的是「精神史」，但就证据结构而言，更接近「接受史」。这不是致命伤：如果研究对象本来就是中国关于日本的想象，接受史是合适的方法。问题在于书名与结论常以日本史的口吻说话，读者容易把接受史误当成日本史。

接受情况印证了这种张力。《新京报》二〇二〇年一月把它列为书单主打，十二月又入选年度社科入围榜，推荐语正是「关注日本这个『分身』近代以来的国家演化历程，也是在审视中国的另一个『自我』」。豆瓣有长评顺着「反射之反射」发挥，给出高分；得到电子书上则有读者给两星，批评它「属于学者在书斋里剪辑摘抄后的臆想，不能解释精神形成的必然性」。专业期刊的专文书评，截至检索时未见。这个接受结构颇有意思：它成功进入了公共讨论，却尚未经过学术共同体的严格检验。

公共讨论对这本书的用法，也值得注意。它常被当作「理解日本」的入口，而不是「理解中国日本论」的入口。这个误读一半来自书名，一半来自书评：推荐语强调「审视中国的另一个自我」，读者记住的却是「日本」。一本书的接受史，有时正好复制它要分析的那条镜链。

把李永晶与戴季陶、本尼迪克特并置，可以看出三种写作位置。戴季陶写《日本论》，是政党要人对党务的训诫；本尼迪克特写《菊与刀》，是战时国家对敌国的情报式把握；李永晶写《分身》，是学院知识分子在两国关系回暖与竞争并存时期的自我反省。三种位置各有盲点：戴季陶太近，本尼迪克特太远，李永晶则太想从镜子里看出一个完整的中国。

加藤周一是第四个位置。一九五六年他写《杂种文化》，是日本人自己回答「日本是什么」，答案恰恰否认了纯粹：日本文化从来是混种。把这个答案放回镜链，会得到一个尴尬的推论：中国论述里那个可供投射的「日本」，在日本自述里并不存在。李永晶把加藤列为对手，是对的；但全书没有让加藤真正打断镜链，加藤只被引来作证，没有被引来反驳。一个框架若只收编对手而不消化对手的否定，它的解释力就要打折。

书里有不少可独立成篇的段落，例如对戴季陶《日本论》与国民党政治动员关系的分析，对战后中国日本观反复摆荡的梳理。这些段落的说服力，往往高于全书的总框架：具体材料比「分身」这个大词更能留住读者。

链条的起点也值得再看一眼。黄遵宪的《日本国志》是志书，体例要求分类、征引、存目，它把日本写成可以查检的对象。李永晶的《分身》是精神史，体例要求解释、串联、判断，它把日本写成可以投射的对象。从志书到精神史，中间丢掉的正是可查检性：读者能顺着作者的思路走，却很难顺着他的注释回到材料。一部以方法自省为号召的书，最好在这一点上先自省。

如果把它当日本史入门，会失望；如果把它当「中国如何看日本」的方法论自省，则大有可读。与《日本国志》《日本论》《菊与刀》《杂种文化》并置，能看出它回应谁、漏掉谁。它回应的是中国日本论的传统，漏掉的是日本自身的社会史。它的价值在提问，不在答案。

如果要把《分身》从「中国日本论述史」推进成真正的「新日本论」，至少还缺三样东西：日文一手材料，日本内部的社会史与制度史，以及一个不再把国家当作单一人格的分析框架。三者都不容易，但缺了它们，「新日本论」就只能是一面更精致的镜子，而不是一扇窗。窗与镜的差别，不在材料多少，而在是否允许对方不照我们的问题回答。

读这本书最容易被说服的地方，是它对「日本热」的解释；最容易被激怒的地方，是它对日本自身历史的省略。两者其实是一件事：看得越清楚的是中国，越模糊的是日本。这也是所有以「他者」为方法的著作共同的代价：方法越锋利，对象越容易被削成方法的形状。

还有一层不对称值得追问。如果「分身」成立，日本人看中国是否也是分身？书中对此着墨不多。镜像理论若只在一个方向生效，它就不是普遍结构，而是中国现代性的特殊经验。这个未完成的问题，或许比书里的结论更有生产性。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Published in January 2020, Li Yongjing's Fenshen: A New Theory of Japan runs past five hundred pages and asks a question that fits in one line: when we read Japan, whom are we reading?

The book does not ask what Japan is. It strings Chinese writing on Japan into a mirror-chain: Wang Tao and Huang Zunxian's mission accounts, Dai Jitao's Japan-ology, the post-war alternation of Japan-fever and aversion. Every description of Japan, it argues, is also a self-positioning, and the avatar is therefore a method: read facts about Japan as sentences about China.

The seven chapters are thematic, not chronological: expelling the barbarians, competing in Asia, overcoming the West, world revolution, democracy, the constitution, the Meiji Restoration. Placing the Restoration last turns the starting point into the final question. No chapter covers the post-war; it is split between democracy and the constitution, so defeat becomes an institutional revision rather than a rupture.

The title promises a new theory of Japan; the book delivers a history of Chinese writing about Japan. When all evidence comes from the Chinese side, claims about Japanese motives can only be inferred. The avatar explains China well: Chinese Japan-writing oscillates between emulation and vigilance because Japan is a replaceable self-image. It explains Japan less well, and reading everything as 'for China to see' deletes Japanese social and institutional history.

'Psychoanalysis of the state' compounds this. A state has no childhood and no unconscious, only institutions, interests and positions. If 'spirit' means collective self-understanding the analogy stands; otherwise it psychologises international relations.

'New worldism' is normative, not descriptive. It criticises national units while repeatedly using terms like 'the Japanese spirit': it wants to leave the nation-state while keeping it as the unit of analysis.

The evidence base repeats the problem. Li calls the result spiritual history, but in evidentiary terms it is reception history: legitimate if the object is China's imagination of Japan, misleading when the title speaks in the voice of Japanese history.

Reception reflects the tension. The Beijing News made it a lead title in January 2020 and longlisted it in December; Douban carries an enthusiastic long review, Dedao a two-star review calling it scissors-and-paste speculation. No specialist journal review was found.

Read it as a self-examination of how China sees Japan, not as an introduction to Japanese history. Beside Huang Zunxian, Dai Jitao, Ruth Benedict and Kato Shuichi, it shows whom it answers and whom it omits.

One asymmetry remains: if the avatar holds, is Japan's view of China also an avatar? The book says little. A mirror theory that works in one direction only is not a universal structure but a particular experience of Chinese modernity. To become a real new theory of Japan it would need Japanese archives, Japan's internal history, and a framework that does not treat the state as a person.

先读导论与结论，标出「分身」的定义句，再回中段找它被使用的每一次；若某处用法与定义不符，就是全书的裂缝。
Read the introduction and conclusion first, mark the definition of 'avatar', then hunt every use of it in the middle; where a use departs from the definition, you have found the book's seam.

Set Li's paraphrases of Huang and Dai against the originals (both in Ring 2) and ask whether the mirror-chain is a finding or a narrative device.
