背景Background

《多元宇宙政治》是 Escobar 的英译文集，汇集他在《多元宇宙的设计》之后关于「真实与可能」的文章与讲演。书名针对的是政治想象力的贫乏：当代政治总是说「现实一点」，而 Escobar 要问，谁定义了现实？他借用政治本体论与「认识论南方」的资源，主张「真实」（lo real）从来不是给定的，而是在权力关系中被做出来的；因此改变世界不只是改变分配，而是改变什么算作真实。书中讨论的案例包括拉丁美洲的领地运动、过渡实践（transiciones）、以及「另一种可能是可能的」这一口号的政治含义。
Pluriversal Politics is a collection of Escobar's essays and talks in English translation, following Designs for the Pluriverse and centring on the real and the possible. The title targets the poverty of political imagination: contemporary politics keeps saying 'be realistic', and Escobar asks who defines the real. Drawing on political ontology and the epistemology of the South, he argues that the real (lo real) is never given but made within relations of power; changing the world is therefore not only redistributing but changing what counts as real. Cases include Latin American territorial movements, transition practices and the politics of the slogan 'another possible is possible'.

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

文集以短章推进，反复在三个词之间移动：真实（lo real）、可能（lo posible）、多元宇宙（pluriverso）。第一部分诊断「现实主义」如何成为一种去政治化的技术；第二部分展示「过渡」如何在地方实践中被想象与试验；第三部分回到本体论，主张「可能」不是未来的选项，而是当下被压抑的实在。篇章之间有重叠，但整体构成一个从批判到实践的弧线。
Short chapters move repeatedly among three words: the real, the possible and the pluriverse. A first movement diagnoses how 'realism' becomes a depoliticising technique; a second shows how transitions are imagined and tested in local practice; a third returns to ontology, arguing that the possible is not a future option but a presently suppressed reality. Chapters overlap, but together they form an arc from critique to practice.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

出版后成为「多元宇宙」讨论的常用入口，因篇幅短、命题清晰，常被用于课程与运动读本；它也让 Escobar 的政治哲学面向更明确。 作为文集，重复较多，理论的系统性不如《多元宇宙的设计》；「另一种可能是可能的」也被批评为循环论证——可能之所以可能，正因为被定义为可能。 **合成 Synthesis：** 本书的价值在于把「现实感」本身问题化：当有人说「这不现实」时，他其实在保护一个特定的世界。
It became a common entry point to pluriverse debates: short, clearly argued, and often used in courses and movement readers; it also makes Escobar's political philosophy more explicit. As a collection it is repetitive and less systematic than Designs for the Pluriverse; 'another possible is possible' has been criticised as circular — the possible is possible because it is defined as such. **Synthesis:** Its value is to problematise the sense of reality itself: when someone says 'that's not realistic', they are in fact protecting a particular world.

推断 · The claimThe claim

Escobar 要证明：「真实」是被权力做出来的，因此政治的想象力不应被「现实一点」所限制；「多元宇宙政治」的任务是扩大可存在的世界，而不是在既定现实中重新分配。

Escobar wants to show that the real is made by power, so political imagination should not be limited by 'be realistic'; the task of pluriversal politics is to expand the worlds that can exist, not to redistribute within a given reality.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

书名把「政治」放在「多元宇宙」之后，说明政治是落实多元宇宙的场域；全书反复处理「真实/可能」这对词，说明作者要动摇的是现实感本身；以「过渡」而非「革命」为框架，说明其政治是渐进而非决裂的。

The title places politics after the pluriverse, making politics the field where plurality is realised; the book keeps working the pair real/possible, showing that what is shaken is the sense of reality itself; the frame of transition rather than revolution shows a gradual rather than ruptural politics.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

谁定义了现实：读《多元宇宙政治》Who defines the real

「现实一点」往往不是忠告，而是保护一个特定世界的政治技术。'Be realistic' is often not advice but a political technique for protecting a particular world.

《多元宇宙政治》篇幅不大，但论点锋利：当代政治最有效的手段，不是禁止你想什么，而是告诉你「这不现实」。一旦「现实」被设定，讨论的范围就被悄悄缩小了。Escobar 要问的正是：谁有权力定义现实？这个定义服务于谁？

他的回答借自政治本体论：真实不是给定的，而是在实践、制度与叙事中被做出来的。因此政治的争夺不只是资源分配，而是「什么算作真实」的争夺。当拉丁美洲的领地运动说「另一个世界是可能的」，他们不是在许愿，而是在陈述一个已经部分存在的实在。

这本书的优点是清楚、可引用、适合带入讨论；缺点是重复，且「另一种可能是可能的」这句口号有循环论证之嫌——如果可能之所以可能，只是因为它被定义为可能，那么这个命题就无法被反驳，也就难以指导实践。

读它最好的方式，是把它当作一个提问工具：下次有人说「这不现实」时，先别争辩可行性，先问——你说的现实，是谁做的？

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Pluriversal Politics is short but sharp: the most effective move in contemporary politics is not to forbid what you think but to tell you that it is 'not realistic'. Once the real is set, the range of discussion quietly shrinks. Escobar asks: who has the power to define the real, and whom does that definition serve?

His answer comes from political ontology: the real is not given but made in practice, institutions and narrative. Political struggle is therefore not only about distributing resources but about what counts as real. When Latin American territorial movements say 'another world is possible', they are not wishing; they are stating a reality that already partly exists.

The book's virtue is clarity and quotability; its weakness is repetition, and the slogan risks circularity — if the possible is possible only because it is defined as such, the claim cannot be refuted and can hardly guide practice.

The best way to use it is as a question-tool: next time someone says 'that's not realistic', do not argue feasibility first. Ask instead — whose reality is that, and who made it?
