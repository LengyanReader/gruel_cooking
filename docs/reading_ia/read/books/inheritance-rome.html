背景 · Background背景 · Background

Wickham（牛津大学 Emeritus 社会史教授，《中世纪欧洲》作者）撰写企鹅欧洲史第二卷。全书核心论题：西罗马帝国476年「灭亡」之后，地中海世界并非陷入黑暗，而是在拜占庭、法兰克、伊斯兰、斯拉夫等多条路径上各自演化出新秩序。方法论上强调经济考古（陶器分布、贸易网络）与制度史（税收、地权、教会组织）并重。时间跨度600年，地理涵盖从爱尔兰到美索不达米亚、从北欧到北非的整个后罗马世界。获2010年Wolffson History Prize。
By Wickham (Emeritus Professor of Social History, Oxford; author of Medieval Europe). Volume 2's core thesis: after the 'fall' of 476, the Mediterranean world did not enter darkness but evolved new orders along Byzantine, Frankish, Islamic, and Slavic paths. Methodologically combines economic archaeology (pottery distribution, trade networks) with institutional history (taxation, land, church organization). Spans 600 years and the entire post-Roman world from Ireland to Mesopotamia. Winner of the 2010 Wolfson History Prize.

第 1 节 · 罗马的遗产（400–600）Section 1 · The Roman Inheritance (400–600)

西罗马解体不是突然断裂——税收体系、城市生活、教会组织的延续与渐变；东哥特与西哥特王国作为「罗马继承人」。
Western dissolution not abrupt rupture — taxation, urban life, church organization continuing; Ostrogothic/Visigothic kingdoms as 'heirs of Rome'.

✓

第 2 节 · 拜占庭、伊斯兰与地中海分裂Section 2 · Byzantium, Islam, Mediterranean Rupture

东罗马/拜占庭的持续；七世纪伊斯兰扩张切断地中海统一经济圈；拜占庭-伊斯兰边界成为欧洲东部新结构。
Eastern Roman survival; 7th-century Islamic expansion severing the unified Mediterranean economy; Byzantine-Islamic frontier as new eastern structure.

✓

第 3 节 · 法兰克与加洛林Section 3 · Franks & Carolingians

墨洛温到加洛林：法兰克王权、修道院经济、查理曼加冕（800）作为罗马-基督教-日耳曼三传统合成、加洛林文艺复兴。
Merovingian to Carolingian: Frankish kingship, monastic economy, Charlemagne's coronation (800) as synthesis of Roman-Christian-Germanic traditions, Carolingian Renaissance.

✓

第 4 节 · 维京、斯拉夫与十世纪的新欧洲Section 4 · Vikings, Slavs, 10th-Century New Europe

9–10世纪第二次崩溃（维京、马扎尔）后的地方化——封建制的形成、德意志东进、基辅罗斯的基督教化、到1000年前后的新秩序。
Localization after 9th–10th century second collapse (Viking, Magyar) — feudal formation, German Ostsiedlung, Kievan Rus' conversion, new order circa 1000.

结构 · Structure结构 · Structure

大空间比较结构：按政治体分区（拜占庭、西欧拉丁、伊斯兰、北方斯拉夫/维京）而非单纯编年；每区内再按时间展开。经济考古证据先行——从陶器、钱币、沉船重建贸易网络地图，然后叠加制度分析。
Large-space comparative structure: organized by polity (Byzantine, Latin West, Islamic, Northern Slavic/Viking) rather than pure chronology; within each, temporal progression. Economic-archaeological evidence leads — trade networks rebuilt from pottery, coins, shipwrecks, then institutional analysis layered on.

评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis评价与合成 · Reception & synthesis

「自Pirenne《中世纪的城市》以来最有力的地中海经济史综合」；数据密度惊人但论证清晰。 部分学者认为对不列颠/爱尔兰着墨偏少；「黑暗时代」标题本身是否合适有争议。 **合成 Synthesis：** 「黑暗时代」是一个神话——Wickham 以此卷为欧洲最常被忽视的六百年正名。
'The most powerful Mediterranean economic synthesis since Pirenne'; staggering data density with clear argumentation. Some note insufficient coverage of Britain/Ireland; the 'Dark Ages' framing itself is debated. **Synthesis:** 'Dark Ages' is a myth — Wickham's volume rehabilitates Europe's most neglected six centuries.
