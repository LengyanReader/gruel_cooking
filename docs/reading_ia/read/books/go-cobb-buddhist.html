长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

原理先行的佛教—围棋导论：读 Cobb 的 2021 年小书A principles-first introduction to Go and Buddhism

一本 106 页的自出版小书：它要把佛教哲学的基本原理与围棋经验对齐，而不是重复专栏文集。A 106-page self-published book that aligns basic Buddhist philosophy with Go experience rather than repeating the columns.

Cobb 的佛教—围棋写作有一条清晰的时间线：1997 年的《The Eastern Buddhist》论文、1999 年 Tricycle 的改写本、2005 年的专栏文集，再到 2021 年这本 106 页的小书。与前面的文本相比，本书的野心更「教学化」：先把佛教哲学的基本原理讲清，再用系列文章把它们与围棋经验对齐。

这使它的价值与风险都很明确。价值在于，它为不熟悉佛教的棋手提供了一条入口：先懂原理，再看棋局。风险在于，106 页的篇幅与自出版的形式，可能让「原理」停留在导论层面，而「系列文章」仍是旧文的改写；它与《Reflections》的重合度尚未核实，不能互相补全。

最稳妥的读法，是把它当作一本「导论」而非「体系」：先读原理部分，标出他实际使用的佛教概念（空、互连、无常、无我、慈悲），再逐篇问：这些概念是在解释棋局，还是只是被棋局举例？答案会决定这本书是 Cobb 的体系，还是 Cobb 的入门课。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Cobb's Go–Buddhism writing has a clear timeline: the 1997 Eastern Buddhist paper, the 1999 Tricycle adaptation, the 2005 collection of columns, and this 106-page book in 2021. Compared with the earlier texts, the ambition here is more pedagogical: set out the basic principles of Buddhist philosophy, then align them with Go experience in a series of essays.

That makes its value and risk clear. The value is an entry point for players unfamiliar with Buddhism: principles first, then the board. The risk is that 106 pages and self-publication keep the principles at the level of introduction while the essays remain reworked old pieces; overlap with Reflections is unverified and the two must not be used to fill each other.

The safest reading is as an introduction, not a system: read the principles first, mark which Buddhist concepts he actually uses (emptiness, interconnectedness, impermanence, no-self, compassion), then ask essay by essay whether the concepts explain the board or the board merely illustrates them. The answer decides whether this is Cobb's system or Cobb's primer.
