推断 · The claimThe claim

《棋经十三篇》的意图不是写一本佛教修行书，而是把围棋放进宇宙论与伦理秩序：以棋局之数法天地阴阳，以「得算」「权舆」「合战」「虚实」「自知」构成从象数到实战、再到自我认识的论说链。

The Thirteen Chapters does not aim at a Buddhist manual; it places Go within cosmology and ethical order: the board's number figures heaven/earth and yin-yang, and calculation, opening, battle, emptiness/solidity and self-knowledge form a chain from number to practice to self-knowledge.

为什么这样读 · The groundsThe grounds

开篇「夫万物之数，从一而起……白黑相半，以法阴阳」把棋局直接接到宇宙论；篇目从「棋局」到「得算」「权舆」「合战」「虚实」，是技术性的论说链；末以「自知」收束，说明作者关心的不只是胜负，而是棋手对自己的认识。三处结构共同指向「立象比德」而非「佛教修行」。

The opening ties the board to cosmology through number and yin-yang; the chapters run from board to calculation, opening, battle and emptiness/solidity as a technical chain; the close on self-knowledge shows concern beyond winning. The structure points to imaging virtue, not Buddhist practice.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

从象数到自知：《棋经十三篇》的论说链From number to self-knowledge

宋代以来最常被引用的围棋理论文本：它把棋局接到宇宙论，又以「自知」收束。The most cited Go theory text since the Song: it ties the board to cosmology and closes on self-knowledge.

《棋经十三篇》常被当作「围棋哲学」来引，但它首先是一部棋论。它的论说链很清楚：开篇以「万物之数，从一而起」「白黑相半，以法阴阳」把棋局接到宇宙论；随后以「得算」「权舆」「合战」「虚实」展开技术判断；末以「自知」收束，把棋艺与自我认识相连。

这条链条说明，它的意图不是「佛教修行」，而是「立象比德」：让棋局成为天地阴阳与伦理秩序的图像，同时不放弃技术性。它的风险也在这里：现代读者容易只引开篇的宇宙论，而忽略后面的技术篇目，把一个棋论读成哲学寓言。

由于十三篇全目与宋本原貌未核，本文只能判断其论说方向。最稳妥的读法是：先读开篇与「自知」，再读「得算」「权舆」「合战」「虚实」，看「象数」如何落地为「判断」。如果落地失败，它就只是宇宙论修辞；如果成功，它才是「技进乎道」的古典原型。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Thirteen Chapters is often quoted as Go philosophy, but it is first a Go treatise. Its chain is clear: the opening ties the board to cosmology through number and yin-yang; calculation, opening, battle and emptiness/solidity develop technical judgment; self-knowledge closes by linking Go to self-understanding.

That chain shows its intent is not Buddhist practice but imaging virtue: the board becomes an image of cosmic and ethical order without abandoning technique. The risk is the reverse: modern readers quote the cosmology and ignore the technical chapters, turning a treatise into allegory.

With the full chapter list and Song original unverified, this essay can judge only the direction. The safest reading is to take the opening and self-knowledge first, then calculation, opening, battle and emptiness/solidity, and see whether number actually lands in judgment. If it does not, the cosmology is rhetoric; if it does, this is the classical prototype of skill-into-Way.
