为何此刻Why now

近代中国「知日」的原点，也是《分身》所追溯那条镜链的第一环。它把日本从一个模糊的邻邦，第一次写成了可查、可引、可作变法之资的对象。
The origin of modern China's knowing of Japan and the first link in the mirror-chain Avatar traces. It turned Japan from a vague neighbour into an object that could be consulted, cited and used for reform.

背景Background

黄遵宪（1848—1905）1877 年随首任驻日公使何如璋赴日，任使馆参赞，遍访日本朝野；1882 年调任驻旧金山总领事，1885 年回国后闭门续写，1887 年成书，共四十卷、十二志、五十余万言，1895 年始刊行。十二志为国统、邻交、天文、地理、职官、食货、兵、刑法、学术、礼俗、物产、工艺，以中国正史的「志」体写一个外国的当代史，每志之后以「外史氏曰」发议论。它与作者早年的《日本杂事诗》互为表里：诗记见闻，志立体例。
Huang Zunxian (1848-1905) went to Japan in 1877 with the first minister He Ruzhang as legation counsellor and travelled widely; posted to San Francisco in 1882, he returned in 1885 and finished the book in 1887: forty juan, twelve treatises, over half a million characters, first printed in 1895. The treatises cover dynastic line, foreign relations, astronomy, geography, offices, revenue, arms, law, learning, custom, produce and craft, using the dynastic-gazetteer form to write a foreign country's contemporary history, each closing with commentary voiced by the 'Outer Historian'. It pairs with his earlier Japan Miscellany in Verse: the poems record what he saw, the treatises fix the form.

论述框架 · Analytical frameworkAnalytical framework

结构Structure

全书是「横切」而非「纵述」：不以王朝更替为序，而以制度门类分卷，让读者一次看见明治日本的行政、财政、军制、法制、学术与风俗。这种写法本身即是一次判断，日本值得被当作一个完整国家来解剖，而不只是「蕞尔岛夷」。
The book cuts across rather than narrates: not dynastic succession but institutional compartments, so that Meiji Japan's administration, finance, army, law, learning and manners come into view at once. The form is itself a judgement: Japan deserves dissection as a whole country, not dismissal as a small island neighbour.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

中国第一部系统研究日本的著作，开创「知日—用日」的传统。志书体例使日本成为可比较、可引用的对象，直接影响戊戌前后的变法论述，也为此后所有中国人写日本提供了最初的格式。 以中国正史的框架套日本，制度分类未必贴合明治实况；「外史氏曰」的经世判断有时压倒材料本身。晚清士人读它，往往读出自己想读的变法。 **合成 Synthesis：** 当「方法样本」读：看一个中国官员如何把陌生国家拆成可写、可比的模块。再与《日本论》对读，看记录如何变成判断。
The first systematic Chinese study of Japan, opening the know-and-use tradition. Its gazetteer form made Japan comparable and citable, shaped reform writing around 1898, and gave every later Chinese account of Japan its first format. A Chinese dynastic frame imposed on Japan, its institutional categories not always fitting Meiji reality; the Outer Historian's statecraft sometimes overwhelms the record. Late-Qing readers tended to find the reform they wanted. **Synthesis:** Read it as a methodological specimen: how a Chinese official breaks an unfamiliar country into writable, comparable modules. Then read Dai to see record turn into judgement.

精读建议 · Deep readingDeep reading

先读地理、职官、食货三志，看黄遵宪如何把「日本」拆成可比较的模块；再读各志末的「外史氏曰」，看判断如何落下。
Start with geography, offices and revenue to see Japan broken into comparable modules; then read the Outer Historian's closing notes to see judgement land.
与《日本论》对读：黄遵宪写「是什么」，戴季陶写「会怎样」，前者是志，后者是策。
Read with Dai Jitao: Huang writes what Japan is, Dai what it will do; one is gazetteer, the other policy.
注意书中保留的汉文概念如何被用来翻译明治新制，这是「概念旅行」最早的一批样本。
Watch how classical Chinese terms are stretched to translate Meiji institutions: some of the earliest samples of travelling concepts.
