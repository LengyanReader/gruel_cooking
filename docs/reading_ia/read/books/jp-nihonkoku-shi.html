背景Background

黄遵宪以外交官与诗人的双眼睛，用通志体例记日本国事，附以「外论」，实为戊戌前后中国借日本变法之资的经世之作。它是近代中国人「知日—用日」传统的开端。
With a diplomat's and a poet's double eye, Huang recorded Japan in gazetteer form with commentary — a statecraft resource for China's reform around 1898; the opening of the modern Chinese know-Japan / use-Japan tradition.
