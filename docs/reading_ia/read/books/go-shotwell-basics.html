背景Background

Publisher description: 600+ diagrams, starting from 9×9 to teach tactics and winning strategy, explaining Go's handicap system, with a CD-ROM by the American Go Association. Listed strategies include invading, sacrificing, ko and territorial thinking. Compared with Go! More Than a Game, it gives less space to history and philosophy.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

图解入门：读 Shotwell《Go Basics》An illustrated entry

一本纯入门的图解手册：600+ 图解、9×9 先行、受子解释与 AGA CD-ROM。A purely introductory illustrated handbook: 600+ diagrams, a 9x9 start, handicap explanation and an AGA CD-ROM.

《Go Basics》是 Shotwell 的第二本围棋手册，与《Go! More Than a Game》形成明确分工：后者把教学与文化史放在一起，前者则专注「让零基础读者尽快能下棋」。

它的教学设计很清楚：600+ 图解、从 9×9 入手、讲基本战术与取胜策略、解释受子制，并附美国围棋协会制作的 CD-ROM。所列策略是侵入、弃子、用劫、地域思维等实战条目，而不是历史或哲学章节。

它的局限也在这里：2005 年版未覆盖 AI 时代的围棋；随书 CD-ROM 在今天可能难以使用；文化维度较轻。读它时不要期待「围棋为什么重要」，而应把它当「图解词典」：先做 9×9 练习，再查受子与劫的条目。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Go Basics is Shotwell's second Go handbook, clearly divided from Go! More Than a Game: the latter joins teaching and cultural history, while this one concentrates on getting a complete beginner to play.

The pedagogy is explicit: 600+ diagrams, a 9x9 start, basic tactics and winning strategy, handicap explanation and an AGA CD-ROM. The listed strategies are practical — invasion, sacrifice, ko, territory — not historical or philosophical chapters.

Its limits follow: the 2005 edition predates AI-era Go; the bundled CD-ROM may be hard to use today; culture is light. Do not expect 'why Go matters'; use it as an illustrated dictionary: do 9x9 drills first, then look up handicap and ko.
