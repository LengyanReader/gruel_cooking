背景Background

Juul 的游戏本体论代表作，讨论规则、虚构、玩家体验与「游戏是什么」。 《Half-Real》2005 年由 MIT Press 出版，是游戏本体论的核心文本。Juul 提出：玩家同时在「真实规则」与「虚构世界」两层行动，游戏的意义正在两层之间。 它也可以被读成一次「概念清理」：在游戏研究还充满混杂用法时，Juul 先把规则、虚构、玩家、游戏四个词分开，再讨论它们如何叠合。这使得本书的方法比结论更持久。 它也可以被读成一次「定义工作」：在「游戏是什么」还没有共识时，Juul 用「半真实」给出一个可操作的定义，让后续研究有共同的起点。
Juul's ontological study of games, discussing rules, fiction, player experience and what a game is. Half-Real (MIT Press, 2005) is a core text of game ontology. Juul argues that players act in real rules and fictional worlds at once, and that meaning lives between the two. It can also be read as conceptual housekeeping: when game studies was still full of mixed usage, Juul separated rules, fiction, player and game before discussing how they overlay. That makes the method outlast the conclusion. It can also be read as definition work: when there was no consensus on what a game is, Juul's half-real offered an operational definition, giving later research a shared starting point.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

两层之间：读《半真实》Between two layers

游戏是「半真实」：玩家同时在真实规则与虚构世界两层行动。Games are half-real: players act in real rules and fictional worlds at once.

《Half-Real》提出一个简洁的本体论：游戏是「半真实」——玩家同时在真实规则与虚构世界两层行动。规则是真的（输赢、移动、限制），世界是虚构的（角色、故事、场景），意义正在两层之间。

它的力量在于给「规则 vs 叙事」之争一个折中；它的风险也在这里：两层未必能干净分开，定义也可能过度抽象。

读它时最好把「半真实」当作一个描述而非定义：它帮助我们看见两层，但不必把所有游戏都塞进同一个模型。真正的检验，是问：去掉虚构，规则还成立吗？去掉规则，虚构还能玩吗？

最强的反读是：玩家从不「同时」在两层，而是在两层之间来回切换；「半真实」因此是分析者的静态模型，而不是玩家的经验。若如此，本书应被读成一套分类工具，而非一种现象学。

最强反读：规则与虚构并非两层，而是同一经验的两面；「半真实」可能只是隐喻。若如此，本书应被读成「一个有用的隐喻」，而不是「一个本体论」。

若把「半真实」当作定义，会忽略它的描述性：它帮助我们看见两层，却不要求所有游戏都按同一比例分配。真正的检验是「去掉一层，游戏还成立吗」。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Half-Real proposes a concise ontology: games are half-real — players act in real rules and fictional worlds at once. Rules are real (winning, moving, constraints), worlds are fictional (characters, stories, settings), and meaning lives between them.

Its power is to mediate the rules-versus-narrative debate; its risk is that the two layers may not be cleanly separable and the definition may be too abstract.

Read half-real as a description rather than a definition: it helps us see two layers without forcing every game into one model. The real test: remove fiction, do the rules still hold? Remove rules, can the fiction still be played?

The strongest counter-reading: players never inhabit both layers 'at once' but switch between them; half-real is thus the analyst's static model, not the player's experience. On that reading the book is a taxonomy, not a phenomenology.

Strongest counter-reading: rules and fiction are not two layers but two sides of one experience; half-real may be a metaphor. On that reading it is a useful metaphor, not an ontology.

Read half-real as a definition and its descriptive force is lost: it helps us see two layers without requiring the same proportion in every game. The test is whether the game survives removing one layer.
