背景Background

世阿弥元清（1363?—1443?）是室町前期的能乐艺人、理论家。《风姿花传》约成书于 1400 年前后，原为传予子婿的秘传，今存诸本，通行结构为序与七篇（年来稽古、物学、问答、神仪、奥义、花修、奥义）。核心概念是「花」：观众意料之外的那一下惊艳；以及「时」：美的标准随时代变化。「秘すれば花」一类名句常被引用，但确切文本归属学界有异，本页不取逐字。
Zeami Motokiyo (1363?–1443?) was a Muromachi Noh actor and theorist. Fūshikaden was completed around 1400 as a secret transmission to his son-in-law; the received structure is a preface and seven fascicles. Its core concepts are hana — the shock of the unexpected in the audience — and toki, the way standards of beauty shift with the age. The famous line 'what is hidden is the flower' is widely quoted, though its exact textual home is debated; no verbatim quotation is given here.

结构Structure

它不是美学论文，而是给艺人的训练手册：如何从七岁到老年安排修习，如何在不同场合选曲，如何让「花」在观众心里开放。理论藏在训练法里——世阿弥谈的每一件事最后都回到「观众此刻的感受」。
It is not an aesthetic treatise but a training manual: how to schedule practice from age seven to old age, how to choose pieces for an occasion, how to make the flower open in the audience. Theory hides inside method — everything returns to the audience's present feeling.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

日本艺道论的源头文本，影响能乐、茶道、俳谐乃至现代表演理论；「花」与「时」两个概念至今被反复引用。 秘传体例使文本难以通读，且不同传本差异大；现代读者容易把「花」浪漫化，忽略它原本是训练与观众管理的技术概念。 **合成 Synthesis：** 把它当「训练手册」而非「美学宣言」：先看世阿弥如何安排一个人的一生，再理解「花」为何必须与「时」一起谈。
The source text of Japanese craft theory, shaping Noh, tea, haikai and modern performance theory; 'flower' and 'time' are still cited. The secret-transmission form makes it hard to read, and the recensions differ widely; modern readers romanticise 'flower' and forget it was a technical concept of training and audience management. **Synthesis:** Read it as a training manual, not an aesthetic manifesto: first see how Zeami schedules a life, then why flower must be discussed with time.

Audience-centred: every judgement lands on whether the audience is moved now.

精读建议 · Deep readingDeep reading

先读「年来稽古」，把它当成一份人生课程表；再读后面诸篇，理论才有落点。
Read the age-based training first as a life curriculum; the later theory then has a place to land.
把「花」与《茶之书》的「不完美」对读：一个讲瞬间惊艳，一个讲残缺之美，都是反完成的美学。
Read 'flower' against The Book of Tea's imperfection: one is momentary shock, one incomplete beauty — both anti-completion aesthetics.
查一下「秘すれば花」的出处争议，练习把「传诵句」与「原文」分开。
Look up the disputed source of 'what is hidden is the flower' and practise separating received sayings from text.
