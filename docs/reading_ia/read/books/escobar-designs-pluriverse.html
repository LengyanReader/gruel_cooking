背景Background

《多元宇宙的设计》写于 Escobar 在哥伦比亚、北卡罗来纳与欧洲多所院校任教与合作的时期，是他在设计研究、人类学与政治本体论之间的综合之作。他关心的不是产品设计，而是「设计」作为一种本体论实践：现代性把世界设计成单一版本（发展、增长、技术），并以此抹平其他世界的存在。Escobar 因此提出「为多元宇宙而设计」：不是为所有人设计同一个未来，而是为不同世界的共存设计条件。书的对话对象包括设计研究（Papanek、Manzini、Fry）、本体论人类学（Viveiros de Castro、Descola）与自主政治（Castoriadis、自治主义）。
Designs for the Pluriverse was written while Escobar taught and collaborated in Colombia, North Carolina and Europe, and it synthesises design studies, anthropology and political ontology. Its concern is not product design but design as an ontological practice: modernity designed a single version of the world — development, growth, technology — and flattened other worlds in the process. Escobar therefore proposes 'design for the pluriverse': not one future designed for everyone but the design of conditions for different worlds to coexist. Its interlocutors include design studies (Papanek, Manzini, Fry), ontological anthropology (Viveiros de Castro, Descola) and the politics of autonomy (Castoriadis, autonomism).

论证结构 · ArgumentArgument

结构Structure

全书分三部分：先诊断——现代性如何通过设计把世界单一化，设计与发展如何共谋；再理论——引入政治本体论、关系性与「多元宇宙」，说明世界是如何被做出来的；最后实践——从拉丁美洲的领地运动、社区设计与自主实践中提炼「为多元宇宙而设计」的原则。Escobar 不提供工具箱，而是提供一组姿态与判准：设计应服务于自主、公共性与生命的维持，而不是抽象的效率。
Three parts: diagnosis — how modernity singularised the world through design and how design colluded with development; theory — political ontology, relationality and the pluriverse, explaining how worlds are made; practice — principles of 'design for the pluriverse' distilled from Latin American territorial movements, community design and autonomous practice. Escobar offers no toolbox but a set of stances and criteria: design should serve autonomy, the communal and the maintenance of life rather than abstract efficiency.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

2018 年出版后成为设计研究、人类学与后发展研究的交叉核心文本；「为多元宇宙而设计」被设计学院、社会创新与去殖民设计广泛采用。 批评主要有三：一，设计被赋予了过重的本体论使命，具体项目难以承载；二，「多元宇宙」有时被用成口号，缺少对权力冲突的分析；三，对专业设计实践的操作指导偏少。 **合成 Synthesis：** 本书的贡献是把设计从「解决问题」提升为「决定哪些世界可以存在」的问题，同时要求设计者放弃单一现代性的普遍主义。
Published in 2018, it became a crossroads text for design studies, anthropology and post-development; 'design for the pluriverse' was widely taken up in design schools, social innovation and decolonial design. Three criticisms recur: design is given an ontological burden that concrete projects can hardly carry; the pluriverse sometimes functions as a slogan without analysis of power conflict; and operational guidance for professional practice is thin. **Synthesis:** The contribution is to raise design from problem-solving to the question of which worlds may exist, while asking designers to give up the universalism of a single modernity.

推断 · The claimThe claim

Escobar 要证明：设计不是中立的技术活动，而是决定哪些世界可以存在的本体论实践；因此设计必须从服务单一现代性，转向为多个世界的共存与自主设计条件。

Escobar wants to show that design is not a neutral technical activity but an ontological practice deciding which worlds may exist; design must therefore turn from serving a single modernity to designing conditions for the coexistence and autonomy of many worlds.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

设计作为造世界：读《多元宇宙的设计》Design as world-making

如果世界是多元的，设计就不能只为一种未来做方案，而要为多个世界的共存做条件。If worlds are plural, design cannot plan for one future; it must make conditions for many worlds to coexist.

《多元宇宙的设计》是 Escobar 最难被归类的书：它既不是设计手册，也不是人类学专著，而是一次把设计研究、政治本体论与后发展理论缝合起来的尝试。它的核心命题很简单：设计不是造物，而是造世界。每一次设计都在决定哪些生活方式被允许、哪些被边缘化、哪些根本不被看见。

这个命题的批判面指向现代性：发展、增长、技术效率，都是被设计出来的单一世界版本。Escobar 把这种单一化称为「一元宇宙」（universe）对「多元宇宙」（pluriverse）的压制。他的替代不是「更好的设计」，而是「为多元宇宙而设计」——为不同世界的共存设计条件，为共同体自我规定生活条件的能力（自主）设计支持。

这本书的力量在于视野，局限也在于视野。当设计被赋予「造世界」的使命时，具体的设计项目很难承载这样的重量；「多元宇宙」也容易被用成口号，而回避一个尖锐的问题：如果某个世界以伤害他人为基础，它还应被允许存在吗？Escobar 对这一点的回答并不充分。

尽管如此，这本书仍然是设计研究过去十年最重要的理论干预之一。它让设计者无法再假装自己只是解决问题的人。读它最好的方式，是把它当作一面镜子：你做的每一个设计决定，正在让哪些世界变得可能，又让哪些世界变得不可能？

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Designs for the Pluriverse is Escobar's hardest book to classify: neither a design manual nor an anthropology monograph, but an attempt to suture design studies, political ontology and post-development. Its core proposition is simple: design is not object-making but world-making. Every design decision decides which ways of life are permitted, which are marginalised, which are not seen at all.

The critical edge points at modernity: development, growth and technical efficiency are designed versions of a single world. Escobar calls this the suppression of the pluriverse by the universe. His alternative is not 'better design' but 'design for the pluriverse' — designing conditions for worlds to coexist and for communities to define the conditions of their own life (autonomy).

The strength is the vision, and so is the limit. When design is given the mission of world-making, concrete projects can hardly carry the weight; the pluriverse can also become a slogan that avoids a sharp question: if a world is founded on harming others, should it be allowed to exist? Escobar's answer is not fully developed.

Still, it is one of the most important theoretical interventions in design studies of the past decade. It makes it impossible for designers to pretend they only solve problems. The best way to read it is as a mirror: which worlds does each of your design decisions make possible, and which does it make impossible?
