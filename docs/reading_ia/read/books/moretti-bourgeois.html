背景Background

与《The Way of the World》《Modern Epic》构成三部曲；本书从关键词与「散文」形式入手，而非从意识形态口号入手。 《The Bourgeois》2013 年由 Verso 出版，是「资产阶级三部曲」的收官。它不从一个阶级的意识形态口号开始，而从几个关键词开始：有用、舒适、效率、严肃；最后回到「散文」——资产阶级最典型的形式。 它的方法也可以被称作「概念史 + 形式史」：关键词提供概念线索，散文提供形式落点。两者合起来，才使「阶级」不只是一个社会学标签。
With The Way of the World and Modern Epic it forms a trilogy; it starts from keywords and the form of prose rather than ideological slogans. The Bourgeois (Verso, 2013) closes the bourgeois trilogy. It starts not from a class ideology but from keywords — useful, comfort, efficiency, seriousness — and returns to prose, the bourgeoisie's most characteristic form. Its method can be called conceptual history plus formal history: keywords supply conceptual threads, prose supplies the formal landing. Together they keep 'class' from being only a sociological label.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

用关键词写阶级：读《资产阶级》Writing class through keywords

三部曲的收官：用「有用、舒适、效率、严肃」与「散文」，追踪资产阶级如何把形式变成自己的生活。The trilogy's close: useful, comfort, efficiency, seriousness and prose track how the bourgeoisie made form its life.

《The Bourgeois》是 Moretti「资产阶级三部曲」的收官。它不从一个阶级的意识形态口号开始，而从几个关键词开始：有用、舒适、效率、严肃；最后回到「散文」——资产阶级最典型的形式。

它的力量在于把「阶级」从口号变成可追踪的文学形式；它的风险也在这里：为什么是这几个词？它们能否代表整个阶级？「散文」是否只是一个文学理论的老问题？

读它时最好把关键词当作「线索」而非「结论」：每一个词都应该回到具体文本，看它如何出现、变形、消失。若关键词法成立，它应该能解释为什么某些词在某个时代突然变得可写。

最强反读：关键词可能只是作者挑的几个词，散文也可能是文学理论的老问题；若如此，本书是「一次关键词实验」，而不是「资产阶级的完整史」。

若只读关键词章，本书像一部概念词典；读到「散文」章才看见 Moretti 的真正目标：资产阶级不只占有一种意识形态，也占有一种写法。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Bourgeois closes Moretti's bourgeois trilogy. It does not start from a class ideology but from a few keywords — useful, comfort, efficiency, seriousness — and returns to prose, the bourgeoisie's most characteristic form.

Its power is to turn class from slogan into trackable literary form; its risk is the same: why these words, and can they stand for a whole class? Is prose merely an old problem of literary theory?

Read the keywords as clues rather than conclusions: each should return to concrete texts and be watched as it appears, mutates and disappears. If the keyword method works, it should explain why certain words suddenly become writable in a period.

Strongest counter-reading: the keywords may be the author's selection, and prose may be an old problem of literary theory; on that reading the book is a keyword experiment, not a complete history of the bourgeoisie.

Read only the keyword chapters and the book looks like a conceptual dictionary; the prose chapter reveals the aim: the bourgeoisie owned not only an ideology but a way of writing.
