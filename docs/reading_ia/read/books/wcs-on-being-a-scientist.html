为何此刻Why now

科研大厦立于「信任」之上。这本书把研究者日常面对的伦理与规范（数据、署名、评审、利益冲突、不当行为、与公众沟通）写成一份可教可读的负责任研究 conduct 入门。
Science rests on trust. This guide turns the everyday ethics of research — data, authorship, peer review, conflicts, misconduct, communication with the public — into a teachable primer on the responsible conduct of research.

背景Background

由美国国家科学院、国家工程院与医学院联合委员会（第三版主席为 Carolyn R. Bertozzi）撰写， NSF 资助，National Academies Press 出版并提供免费 PDF。定位为「补充导师日常伦理示范」的自助读本，适用于学术、工业、政府一切研究场景与所有学科。全书按官方结构分三大板块：其一，研究的人文维度——把科学视为一种社会与历史事业，讲研究者的价值与信任；其二，做研究的有效管理者——处理数据、合作研究、署名与发表、同行评审、利益冲突与承诺、监管与伦理标准、以及科研不当（伪造/篡改/剽窃 FFP）；其三，对更广阔世界的责任——与学生和公众、政策制定者的沟通，及面向可持续未来的负责任科学。它正是「论文写作与发表」链条上游的伦理底座：先有可信赖的研究，才有可发表的论文。
Written by a joint committee of the National Academy of Sciences, National Academy of Engineering, and Institute of Medicine (3rd-ed. chair Carolyn R. Bertozzi), sponsored by the NSF and published by the National Academies Press, which offers a free PDF. It is meant to 'supplement the informal lessons in ethics provided by research supervisors,' applying to academic, industrial, and governmental research across all disciplines. Per the official layout it moves in three parts: the human dimension of research (science as a social and historical enterprise; the researcher's values and trust); being an effective steward of research (the treatment of data, collaborative research, authorship and publication, peer review, conflicts of interest and commitment, regulatory and ethical standards, and research misconduct — fabrication, falsification, plagiarism); and responsibilities to the broader world (communication with students, the public, and policymakers, and responsible science for a sustainable future). It is the ethical base beneath the whole 'write and publish a paper' chain: trustworthy research first, publishable papers after.

结构Structure

全书非论述体，而是一份「负责任研究 conduct」的分板块指南（第三版）。三板块：其一，研究的人文维度，把科学当作一种社会与历史事业，讲共同体的价值（普遍主义、有组织的怀疑、共享与诚信）与信任何以为根基；其二，做研究的有效管理者，逐条处理数据（记录、保存、共享）、合作与跨学科研究、署名与发表次序、同行评审、利益冲突与承诺、受试者保护与动物福利等监管伦理标准，以及科研不当（伪造、篡改、剽窃，FFP）的界定与应对；其三，对更广阔世界的责任，带教学生、面向公众与政策制定者的沟通、以及面向可持续未来的科学。每章末附「供讨论的问题」案例，供课堂使用。
Not a discursive argument but a sectioned guide to the responsible conduct of research (3rd ed.). Three parts. First, the human dimension of research: science as a social and historical enterprise, the community's values (universalism, organized skepticism, communalism and integrity) and trust as its foundation. Second, being an effective steward of research: the treatment of data (recording, retention, sharing), collaborative and interdisciplinary work, authorship and the order of names, peer review, conflicts of interest and commitment, regulatory and ethical standards such as the protection of human subjects and animal welfare, and the definition and handling of misconduct, that is fabrication, falsification and plagiarism (FFP). Third, responsibilities to the broader world: mentoring students, communication with the public and policymakers, and responsible science toward a sustainable future. Each chapter closes with 'Issues for Discussion' cases for classroom use.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

作为美国国家科学院等三院联署、NSF 资助的自助读本，长期是「负责任研究」(RCR) 课程的标准入门教材；因其简明、跨学科、可免费下载而广为采用，被导师与学生共用作科研伦理的共同参照。 保留意见：以美国学术生态为中心，部分情境（资助体系、机构审查、法律框架）迁移性有限；篇幅所限，若干议题（如数据管理、开放获取、AI 辅助研究）在旧版中着墨偏浅，需配合更新的指南使用。 **合成 Synthesis：** 它把科研大厦赖以立足的「信任」摊开成可教的具体规范，是写作与发表链条的上游底座：先有可被信赖的研究（诚实的数据、正当的署名、公正的评审、披露的冲突），才轮得到「怎么写、怎么发」。这也是它被纳入「学术写作正典」的理由。
As a self-instructional reader issued jointly by the National Academies and sponsored by the NSF, it has long been a standard primer for courses in the responsible conduct of research (RCR); adopted widely for its brevity, its cross-disciplinary reach and its free download, it serves mentors and mentees alike as a shared reference for research ethics. Reservations: it is centred on the American academic ecosystem, so some settings (funding systems, institutional review, legal frames) transfer imperfectly; given its length several topics such as data management, open access and AI-assisted research are treated thinly in the older edition and call for updating guides alongside it. **Synthesis:** It spreads the premise of trust on which science rests out into teachable, concrete norms, and is the ethical base upstream of the write-and-publish chain: only research that can be trusted (honest data, rightful authorship, fair review, disclosed conflicts) earns the later questions of how to write and how to publish. That is why it belongs in a canon of academic writing.
