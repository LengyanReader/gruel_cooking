背景Background

大西克礼的《物哀论》是三部曲的中篇，英文世界长期没有单行译本。可核实的通道是 Michele Marra 编 A History of Modern Japanese Aesthetics（University of Hawai'i Press, 2001）中对大西物哀论的节译与讨论；此外英语研究（如关于 Onishi 的 sabi 与 aware 的论文）多转引其日文原著。旧档案所记「约 2022 年英译单行本」查无书目依据，已改为本条并降为 ○。
Onishi's aware study is the middle panel of the trilogy, and the English world long lacked a standalone translation. The verifiable channel is the excerpt and discussion of Onishi's aware in Michele Marra (ed.), A History of Modern Japanese Aesthetics (University of Hawai'i Press, 2001); English scholarship otherwise cites the Japanese original. The old record's 'c.2022 standalone English translation' has no bibliographic basis and is replaced by this entry at ○.

第 2 节 · 术语的英译处理Section 2 · Handling the term in English

英译保留 mono no aware 的音译，再以 impermanence、sentiment 等词作解释。术语被保留，语义却被重新编排，这正是读节选最该留意的地方。
The translation keeps mono no aware in romanised form and explains it through words such as impermanence and sentiment. The term survives, but its semantics are rearranged, which is exactly what an excerpt asks the reader to notice.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

让英语读者知道「物哀」不是简单的「悲」，而是一套关于感应与无常的审美结构；编本的注释也提供了日文文献线索。 节选必然割裂论证；且英语转述常把 aware 浪漫化为「日本式感伤」，失掉大西的概念史严格性。 **合成 Synthesis：** 把它当「接受史材料」而非「译本」：读英语如何误读，也是一种读法。真正的文本仍须回到《幽玄·物哀·寂》。
It shows English readers that aware is not mere sadness but a structure of attunement and impermanence; the volume's notes also give Japanese sources. An excerpt inevitably fragments the argument, and English restatements often romanticise aware as Japanese sentimentality, losing Onishi's conceptual rigour. **Synthesis:** Treat it as reception history rather than a translation: reading how English misreads is itself a method. The real text remains the trilogy.
