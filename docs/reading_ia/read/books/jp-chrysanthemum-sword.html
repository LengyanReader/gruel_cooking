背景Background

本尼迪克特受美国战时委托、在未踏足日本的情况下以文献与研究对象构建其「文化模式」：耻感与罪感、忠孝的三角张力、「各安其分」的等级观。它是外视式定型的典范，也开「日本文化论」的方法之辩。
Commissioned in wartime, Benedict built a culture-pattern from materials without entering Japan: shame versus guilt, the loyalty-filience triangle, a hierarchy where each stays in his place — a model of outside typing and a founding method-debate of Japan-culture studies.
