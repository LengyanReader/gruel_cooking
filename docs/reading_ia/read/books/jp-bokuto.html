背景Background

荷风以「最后的江户人」之笔，写下町的风俗、游里的人物与消逝的街景。放浪记是巴黎之颓，墨东是江户之挽，两者合成一套反近代的审美。中译版本与选目待核，故 ◐。
As the last Edo man, Kafu sketches low-town custom, the quarter's people and vanishing streets; Parisian decadence in Wandering, Edo mourning here — together one anti-modern aesthetic. Chinese editions and contents to verify, hence ◐.
