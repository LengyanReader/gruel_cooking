背景Background

Fairbairn 与已故 T. Mark Hall 从 GoGoD 资料与各地棋书搜集文章，2009 年由 Slate and Shell 出版。BGJ 书评称全书 300 页，章节涉及开局、贴目、棋人、棋书、历史、现代围棋、业余与职业，并有关于战时日本围棋与原子弹对局的较长论述；另有两位客座作者。 BGJ 书评列出的章节包括：开局、贴目、棋人、棋书、「sexy sekis」、历史、现代围棋、业余与职业；客座作者 Ivan Vigano 写日本盲人棋手，Pieter Mioch 写第 45 期十段与 2006 世界业余赛；并有一篇关于战时日本围棋与原子弹对局的较长论述。
Fairbairn and the late T. Mark Hall assembled essays from GoGoD and Go bookshops; Slate and Shell published it in 2009. The BGJ review describes 300 pages on openings, komi, players, books, history, modern Go, amateur and professional play, with a long piece on wartime Japan and the atom-bomb game, plus two guest authors. 长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

把棋道放回历史：读《The Go Companion》Putting the Way back into history

Fairbairn 与 Hall 的随笔集：从开局、贴目到棋人、棋书与战时日本，把围棋放回历史与文化。Fairbairn and Hall's collection: from opening and komi to players, books and wartime Japan, putting Go back into history and culture.

《The Go Companion》的书名就是方向：Go in History and Culture。它不是 Cobb 式的哲学文集，也不是技术手册，而是一本把围棋放回历史与文化的随笔集。

BGA 书评列出的章节很说明问题：开局、贴目、棋人、棋书、sexy sekis、历史、现代围棋、业余与职业；还有客座作者写日本盲人棋手、第 45 期十段与 2006 世界业余赛；以及一篇关于战时日本与原子弹对局的较长论述。这些题目共同构成一个判断：围棋的价值不在格言，而在具体人物、赛事与制度处境。

它的风险是「随笔集」的松散：长短文章并置，论证依赖多篇互补，而非一条主线。读它时最好按主题选取，而不是从头到尾；并把「棋道」类格言放回具体历史处境检验。这正是它与 Cobb 的分工：Cobb 谈观心，Fairbairn/Hall 谈历史。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The title of The Go Companion is the direction: Go in History and Culture. It is neither Cobb-style philosophy nor a technical manual, but a collection that returns Go to history and culture.

The BGA review's sections are telling: opening, komi, players, books, sexy sekis, history, modern Go, amateur and professional; guest pieces on blind players in Japan, the 45th Judan and the 2006 World Amateur; and a long piece on wartime Japan and the atom-bomb game. Together they argue that Go's value lies in concrete persons, matches and institutions, not aphorism.

Its risk is the looseness of a collection: long and short pieces coexist and the case is cumulative rather than a single line. Read by topic rather than straight through, and test 'Way' aphorisms against concrete historical situations. This is its division of labour with Cobb: Cobb contemplates, Fairbairn and Hall historicise.
