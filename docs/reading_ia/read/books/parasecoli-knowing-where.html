推断 · The claimThe claim

Parasecoli wants to show that place-based labels are cultural politics; tradition is legalised and marketed, protecting and excluding producers at once.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

谁有权定义传统：读《知其来源》Who has the right to define tradition

原产地标签的文化政治：传统如何被法律化、市场化，并同时保护与排除生产者。The cultural politics of place-based labels: how tradition is legalised, marketed, and protects and excludes producers at once.

《Knowing Where It Comes From》研究原产地标签：地理标志、原产地命名、非物质遗产。它问的不是「什么才正宗」，而是「谁有权定义传统」。

它的力量在于把「传统」放进法律与市场文件，而不是停留在文化抒情；它的风险也在这里：多国比较可能牺牲单案深度，消费者视角也可能不足。

读它时最好把「标签」当作三件事的交汇：法律保护、市场区分、文化叙事。三者并不总是一致。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Knowing Where It Comes From studies place-based labels: geographical indication, appellation, intangible heritage. It asks not what is authentic but who has the right to define tradition.

Its power is to place tradition in legal and market documents rather than cultural lyricism; its risk is that multi-country comparison may sacrifice depth, and the consumer view may be thin.

Read the label as the intersection of legal protection, market differentiation and cultural narrative. The three do not always align.
