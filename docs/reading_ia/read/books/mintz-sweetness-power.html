长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

甜是权力：读《甜与权力》Sweetness is power

糖从奢侈品到工人阶级日常：殖民、奴隶制与资本主义的世界体系。Sugar from luxury to working-class staple: colony, slavery and the capitalist world-system.

《Sweetness and Power》以糖为线索，连接加勒比种植园、英国工人阶级与全球贸易。它的核心命题是：食物消费不是口味问题，而是殖民、劳动与阶级关系的沉淀。

它的力量在于把「甜」写成历史产物，而非自然偏好；它的风险也在这里：以英国为中心、生产面较重，消费者的主动性可能被低估。

读它时最好把「糖」当作一个入口，而不是全部：它展示的是资本主义如何组织一种食物，而不是所有食物的历史。若要检验它，可以问：另一种食物（茶、盐、肉）是否也走同一条路径？

最强的反读是：糖的普及也可能由消费者主动选择，而非只由生产结构决定；以英国为中心也可能把「西方」当成「全球」。若如此，本书是「一种食物的一国史」，而不是「所有食物的世界史」。

若把糖当作「一种食物」，会错过它的方法论野心：Mintz 要写的是资本主义如何组织一种日常，而不是糖的口味史。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Sweetness and Power follows sugar to link Caribbean plantations, the British working class and global trade. Its claim: food consumption is not taste but the sediment of colonial, labour and class relations.

Its power is to make sweetness a historical product rather than a natural preference; its risk is a British focus and a production-heavy account that may underplay consumer agency.

Read sugar as an entry, not the whole: it shows how capitalism organises one food, not the history of all food. To test it, ask whether another food — tea, salt, meat — follows the same path.

The strongest counter-reading: sugar's spread may also be an active consumer choice, not only a production structure; a British focus may mistake the West for the globe. On that reading it is one food's national history, not a world history of all food.

Read sugar as one food and its methodological ambition is missed: Mintz writes how capitalism organises an everyday, not a taste history of sugar.
