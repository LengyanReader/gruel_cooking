背景Background

勒梅特「光荣岁月」（Les Années glorieuses）四部曲终章，2026-01-06 出版，512 页，真实 ISBN 9782702191477（旧卡 9782702189825/543pp 已更正）。背景设定在 1963–64 年的巴黎与乡村：长子让·佩尔格（Jean Pelletier）因在火灾中救出一名婴儿而成为国民英雄，被捧为「美丽的承诺」的化身；他的弟弟弗朗索瓦却开始怀疑——这个被全家供奉的英雄，正是连环杀害年轻女性的真凶。经济繁荣的「光辉三十年」遮蔽了道德的溃败：进步的神话与它庇护的罪长自同一根。
Finale of Lemaitre's Glorious Years quartet, published 2026-01-06, 512 pp, ISBN 9782702191477 (corrected from the wrong 9782702189825/543pp in the earlier card). Set in 1963–64 Paris and the countryside: eldest son Jean Pelletier, a national hero for saving a baby from a fire, becomes the incarnation of "beautiful promises"; his brother François begins to suspect the family's idol is the serial killer of young women. The boom of les Trente Glorieuses masks moral decay — the myth of progress and the crime it shelters grow from the same root.

第 1 幕 · 英雄的长子Act 1 · The Hero Son

巴黎 1963–64。让·佩尔格为救火中婴儿成为国家英雄——媒体造神、「美丽的承诺」全民共情。家中老猫约瑟夫（Joseph）是沉默的观察者。弟弟弗朗索瓦试笔的小说《美丽的承诺》（书名即来自这部「手稿」）把兄长写成连环杀人犯，而现实中蹊跷的失踪案接踵而至。
Paris 1963–64. Jean Pelletier becomes a national hero rescuing a baby from a fire — the press canonizes him, "beautiful promises" everywhere. The family cat Joseph is the silent witness. Brother François's manuscript-novel Les Belles Promesses (the real title) imagines the eldest as a serial murderer — then girls really start disappearing.

第 2 幕 · 疑邻与沉默Act 2 · Suspicion and Silence

François's suspicion meets the family's collective refusal to ask: Geneviève keeps the house, siblings Philippe and Colette have agendas, and hero-worship is the rule. Subplot: Manuel Ramos, an Algeria veteran, carries the war's unresolved trauma into the postwar countryside.

结构Structure

勒梅特的典型声部：高速第三人称、冷峻的黑色幽默、家族史诗的大构架与小景深高密度切换。小说借「书内书」（François 的《美丽的承诺》手稿）制造叙述套盒：作者、角色与读者共享同一份猜疑。
Lemaitre's signature register: rapid third-person, bleak humour, the big family-epic frame cut with dense close-ups. The novel-within-the-novel (François's manuscript) creates a nesting of suspicion shared by author, character and reader.

评价与合成Reception & synthesis

法媒普遍称赞终章把四部曲收束到「家—历史—正义」三角，猫约瑟夫的设置被反复引用为全书最锋利的意象。 部分评论认为乡村次线与巴黎主线两线并轨略迟，节奏在第三卷末处偏缓。 **合成 Synthesis：** 作者在 Giscard 时代与「光环三十年」的交汇点写作：真正的主角不是凶手，而是「供奉英雄的家族」这个社会机制。
French media praise the quartet's convergence on family–history–justice; the cat Joseph is repeatedly cited as the sharpest image. Some critics find the rural thread converges late; pacing dips near the end of part three. **Synthesis:** The real protagonist isn't the killer — it's the family-machine that canonizes its hero.
