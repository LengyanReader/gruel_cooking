背景Background

本书为国家社科基金「中国围棋思想史」研究的成果之一，是何云波对历代围棋论说的选编。它与《中国围棋思想史》互为表里：一为论述，一为文献。 湘潭大学官网把《中国历代围棋棋论选》与《中国围棋思想史》并列为国家社科基金研究成果；《光明日报》2022 年文章亦以两书为该项目的成果。
An output of the NSSF 'History of Chinese Go Thought' project, this is He's anthology of historical Go discourse. It pairs with the History as text and commentary: one argues, one presents the documents. Xiangtan University lists the Selected Treatises together with the History as NSSF outputs; the 2022 Guangming Daily article likewise presents both as project results.

推断 · The claimThe claim

何云波编这本选本，是要给《中国围棋思想史》提供「可复核的文本底座」：让读者不只看现代解释，而能回到历代棋论原文，检验「立象比德」「技进乎道」「和谐」是否真的在文本中成立。

He compiles the anthology to give the History a checkable textual base: readers should return to the treatises themselves and test whether imaging virtue, skill-into-Way and harmony actually hold in the texts.

长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

给思想史一个文本底座：读《中国历代围棋棋论选》A textual base for the history of ideas

与《中国围棋思想史》配对的选本：它的价值不在新论，而在让读者回到历代棋论原文。An anthology paired with the History: its value is not a new thesis but a return to the treatises.

《中国历代围棋棋论选》与《中国围棋思想史》同为国家社科基金项目成果。这种「一论一选」的配对，决定了它的性质：它不是普及读物，而是研究工具——给《思想史》的分期与解释提供可复核的文本底座。

它的价值在「序列」：读者可以沿着选目，从先秦到现代，看「立象比德」「技进乎道」「和谐」这些概念在原始棋论中如何出现、变形与收束。它的风险也在「选择」：若选目受「思想史」框架影响，技术类棋论可能被边缘化，选本就会强化它本来要检验的框架。

因此，读它时最好带着两个问题：第一，选目是否覆盖了《思想史》各章的关键文本？第二，未被选入的棋论是否恰好是最难纳入「思想史」的那一类？这两个问题，比「选得全不全」更能检验它的学术价值。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

The Selected Go Treatises and A History of Chinese Go Thought are both outputs of the same NSSF project. That argument-plus-anthology pairing defines its nature: it is not a popular reader but a research instrument, supplying a checkable textual base for the History's periodisation and interpretation.

Its value is sequence: following the selection from pre-Qin to modernity, a reader can watch concepts such as imaging virtue, skill-into-Way and harmony emerge, mutate and close in the primary treatises. Its risk is selection: if the anthology is shaped by the History's frame, technical treatises may be marginalised and the anthology will reinforce the very frame it should test.

Read it with two questions: does the selection cover the key texts of each chapter of the History, and are the excluded treatises precisely those hardest to fit into intellectual history? These questions test its scholarly value better than completeness.
