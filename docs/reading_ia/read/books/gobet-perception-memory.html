长评 · Critical essayCritical essay

两代人的一眼：读《知觉与记忆》Two generations of the glance

de Groot 与 Gobet 的跨代对话：棋手的专长是知觉与记忆，而非更深的搜索。A cross-generational dialogue between de Groot and Gobet: chess expertise is perceptual memory, not deeper search.

《Perception and Memory in Chess》把 de Groot 的经典研究与 Gobet 的组块理论放在同一本书里。它的核心命题是：棋手不是想得更多，而是看得不同；知觉与记忆才是专长的核心。

它最特别的地方，是第九章让两位作者以「两种不同观点」对话，保留分歧而不是制造共识。这让本书不仅是研究，也是一份学术争论的记录。

读它时最好把「一眼识别」当作可检验的假设：如果专长真是知觉，那么训练就应该改变「看」的方式，而不只是增加「想」的时间。

英文摘要 · AbstractAbstract

Perception and Memory in Chess puts de Groot's classic research and Gobet's chunking theory in one book. Its core claim: masters do not think more but see differently; perception and memory are the core of expertise.

Its most distinctive feature is chapter nine, where the two authors stage 'two different views', preserving disagreement rather than manufacturing consensus. The book is thus both research and a record of scholarly debate.

Read 'seeing at a glance' as a testable hypothesis: if expertise is perceptual, training should change how one sees, not only how long one thinks.
