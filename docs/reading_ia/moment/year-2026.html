此刻：秋分之日ORIENTATION — THE DAY ITSELF

今晨，全球各地入暮时分，是秋分。太阳停在天赤道正上空，昼夜精确到分钟地两分；我们这颗倾斜、顽固、永远失衡的星球，决然地向寒冷倾过身去。半年前、一战之前，波斯历的春天带来诺鲁孜节——「新的一天」，比我们称之为现代的大多数事物早三千年。那个春分落在战争的第 21 天；这个秋分，落在战争的第二十七周。

Today, September 23, 2026, at dusk everywhere it is autumn's hinge. The sun stands over the equator, day and night balanced to the minute, and the earth — our tilted, stubborn, perpetually off-balance star — tips decisively toward cold. In the Persian calendar, half a year and one war ago, the spring brought Nowruz: New Day, a festival three thousand years older than most of what we call modernity. That equinox landed on the 21st day of the war. This one lands on its 27th week.

秋分不是法庭。它不裁断这一年是否公正。它只宣报几何学：光正按部就班地离开北半球，对柴油价格、导弹弹道、总统的行程一无所知。而就在这无动于衷里，又藏着某种消息：一年走到了它的平衡点，我们正被载着越过它。

The equinox is not a tribunal. It does not weigh whether the year was just. It announces only the geometry: the light is leaving the northern half of the world, exactly on schedule, indifferent to diesel prices, to missile trajectories, to the itinerary of presidents. And somewhere in that indifference there is, again, a form of news: the year has reached its balance point, and we are being carried past it.

2026 年的天平与其说是倾斜，不如说是裂开——裂成可见的一半与机器生成的一半；裂成本土与战区；裂成一个奉献了近年最美足球赛事的年份，与一次近年最致命的校园空袭并存的年份；裂成一个教会机器思考、却还没决定允许机器替人瞄多少靶的文明。

In 2026 the balance is not so much tilted as split — split into visible and machine-made halves, into homeland and war zone, into a year that produced the most beautiful football tournament in memory and the most lethal school strike in recent memory, into a civilization that taught its machines to reason and is still deciding how much of the aiming those machines may do.

这就是从秋分之衡读到的这一年之形：多数事件，如同多数日子，是相等的两半。裂开，就是故事本身。而这个秋分我在读的五本书，像是专为这一道裂缝而写——清单不入此文，单独存于独立的书单页。

This is the shape of the year, as read from its autumn balance: most events, like most days, are equal halves. The split is the story. And on this equinox the five books I am reading seem to have been written for exactly this split — the list itself is filed separately, on its own page, not inside this essay.

回望：一年的弧线PART I — LOOKING BACK: THE ARC OF A YEAR

一月之风：暴风前的预告The January wind — before the storm

新年以期待的音阶开场。足球世界杯即将在北美举行；中期选举在争议总统的阴影下成形；AI 产业承诺年末会迎来一个「推理之夏」，新一代模型届时登场；而海湾——在它成为非正常运转地带的最后几周——仍是畅通的咽喉。一月像是正在布景的舞台。战争早已排在历史的日程上，只是台下还看不见节目单。

The year opened in the register of anticipation. A football World Cup was coming to North America; the midterms were forming in the shadow of a contested presidency; the AI industry promised a "summer of reason" with new model generations at the end of the year; and the Gulf remained — for the last weeks it ever would be — a functioning chokepoint. January felt like a stage being dressed. The war was already on the calendar of history; the audience just couldn't see the program yet.

二月二十八：断裂February 28 — the rupture

然后断裂降临，它将——若还能被记住的话——被记住为一个日期，而非一个季节：2026 年 2 月 28 日。美国对伊朗的打击开启了被称为「大规模作战行动」的进程 ✓。数日之内，霍尔木兹海峡——承载着全球约五分之一石油的二十一英里动脉——对多数交通关闭 ✓，战火蔓延到也门与沙特本土 ✓。丰裕的物流，那个让一切廉价可及一切的奇迹，被揭露出它是有咽喉的；而有人正把手按在上面。

Then the rupture, which will be remembered — if it is remembered at all — as a date rather than a season: February 28, 2026. United States strikes on Iran began what was declared "major combat operations" ✓. Within days the Strait of Hormuz — the 21-mile-wide artery carrying roughly a fifth of the world's oil — was closed to most traffic ✓, and the war widened to Yemen and to Saudi soil ✓. The logistics of abundance, the miracle that had made everything cheap everywhere, was revealed to have a throat; someone's hands were on it.

同一波开场炮火中，米纳布一所学校，超过一百二十名儿童罹难 ✓——这个数字可能将与这个世纪对「精准战争」的关系，如同美莱村之于越战、德累斯顿之于战略一样具有定义性 ○。9 月 18–19 日，Bloomberg 报道 Pentagon 自我审查将该次打击的一部分归因于对 AI 生成目标的过度依赖——Palantir 的 Maven 系统读着战场模式，把一个机器认不出的地点标为军事目标 ○。第一场死亡机器大体上由模式识别机器瞄准的战争，不在我们的未来。它已经发生。我们就活在这个季节里。

In that same opening salvo, at a school in Minab, more than a hundred and twenty children were killed ✓ — a figure that may define the century's relationship to precision warfare the way My Lai defined a war or Dresden defined a strategy ○. On September 18–19, Bloomberg reported that the Pentagon's own review attributes part of that strike to over-reliance on AI-generated targeting — that Palantir's Maven system, reading the patterns of the battlefield, flagged a site the machine could not see as a school ○. The first war in which the machinery of death was substantially aimed by the machinery of pattern recognition is not somewhere in our future. It happened. It is the season we are in.

三月：战争中的春分March — the equinox inside the war

第一个春分——3 月 21 日诺鲁孜节——落在战争的第 21 天 ✓，世界正面遭遇了这个悖论：即便空袭夜夜落在德黑兰，伊朗人仍在达吉里什巴扎买风信子、在跳火节上跳过火焰；即便有一位总理宣称轰炸是送给人民庆祝新年的礼物，人民依然排练着他们正典里最古老的文化论证——无论此刻多么动荡，未来总会到来。这句话至今仍是全年被引用最多的句子 ✓。七个停火条件经由卡塔尔渠道被提出 ○；没有一个站住。

The first equinox — March 21, Nowruz — arrived on the 21st day of the war ✓, and the world met the paradox head-on: even as nightly airstrikes hit Tehran, Iranians bought hyacinths in the Tajrish Bazaar and jumped over fires for Chaharshanbe Suri; even as a prime minister claimed the bombing was a gift to the people's ability to celebrate their own new year, the people rehearsed the oldest cultural argument in their canon — despite any turmoil now, there will be a future. That clause is still the most quoted sentence of the year ✓. Seven conditions for settlement were floated through Qatari channels ○; none held.

夏：世界杯的间奏Summer — the World Cup interlude

随后七月奉献了这一年最具分量的文化慰藉：世界杯。7 月 19 日，西班牙在纽约大都会人寿球场于加时赛中 1–0 击败阿根廷，费兰·托雷斯第 106 分钟头球破门 ✓。西班牙成为历史上首个同时捧起男足与女足世界杯的国家 ✓。英格兰获季军；佛得角的一记进球被评为赛事最佳 ✓。没有任何体育能治愈战争。但体育保有一种值得一提的古老力量：它给世界一份共享的奇迹日历——两百个国家同意在同一个下午感受同一种情绪。在分裂的一年里，有一个情绪的共和国持续了一个月，而这很重要。

Then July gave the year its most consequential act of cultural consolation: the World Cup. Spain beat Argentina 1–0 in extra time at MetLife Stadium on July 19, the goal a 106th-minute header by Ferran Torres ✓. Spain became the first country ever to hold both the men's and the women's world titles simultaneously ✓. England took bronze; a Cabo Verde goal was named goal of the tournament ✓. Nothing in sport heals a war. But sport retains one ancient power worth naming: it gives the world a shared calendar of wonder — two hundred countries agreeing to feel the same emotion on the same afternoon. In a year of fracture, a republic of feeling held for one month, and it mattered.

九月：崩塌与喧哗并至September — collapse and clamor together

  • 9 月 3 日——双重葬礼与秋季军备竞赛。September 3 — the double funeral and the fall rush.

    格洛丽亚·斯泰纳姆 92 岁辞世 ✓，距她参与创立的运动被同时授予一项胜利和一份验尸报告，不过一周。同日，AI 产业引爆秋季发布：OpenAI 的 GPT-6「Astra」、Anthropic 的 Claude Fable 5.1、Google 的 Gemini 3.8，以及持续扩张的 Meta 产品线——该领域历史上最密集的前沿发布节奏 ✓。

    Gloria Steinem died at 92 ✓, a week after the movement she co-founded was handed both a triumph and an autopsy. On the same day the AI industry detonated its autumn release: OpenAI's GPT-6 "Astra," Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1, Google's Gemini 3.8, and an expanding Meta line — the most compressed frontier-launch cadence in the field's history ✓.

  • 9 月 4 日——礼貌的助手死去。September 4 — the polite assistant died.

    Google 关闭了它的 Assistant，终结一场十二年的对话伴侣实验 ✓——一场更安静、也更奇特的葬礼。整整一代用户曾被一个说着「抱歉，我不明白」的设备训练。它的离世，标志着恭顺式界面的退场。

    Google shut down its Assistant, ending a twelve-year experiment in conversational companionship ✓ — a quieter funeral, and a stranger one. A whole generation of users had been trained by a device that said "I'm sorry, I don't understand." Its death marks the passing of the deferential interface.

  • 9 月 9–10 日——共和党全国代表大会在达拉斯举行，September 9–10 — the Republican convention in Dallas,

  • 9 月 14 日——艾美奖September 14 — the Emmys

    went to The Pitt and Widow's Bay, and Jean Smart won her eighth, tying the all-time record for a performer ✓ — television, in the autumn of the fractured year, once again functioning as the culture's pulse.

    Macron met Trump at Trump Tower amid UNGA 81 ✓; Washington announced two new military bases in Greenland ✓ — the Arctic, the year's final map where the melt gives way to missiles.

    Diesel at $5.85 a gallon, a record ✓; an oil slick spreading in the Gulf ✓; and the quiet catastrophe behind the headlines: Kuwait draws 90% of its water, Oman 86%, Saudi Arabia 70% from desalination plants sitting directly in the war's firing line ✓. The chokepoint was never only about oil. It was about the fact that a city in the Gulf is a machine for turning seawater into life, and the switch is a shipping lane.

AI 之线：未经许可的智能PART II — THE AI THREAD: INTELLIGENCE WITHOUT PERMISSION

如果说战争给了这一年它的伤口，人工智能则给了它速度。而两者已经开始以一种无人预谋的方式彼此照亮。

If the war gave the year its wound, artificial intelligence gave it its speed. And the two have begun to glow together in a way no one planned.

秋季发布是一场协奏，而非独奏。9 月 3 日，前沿在四条战线同时移动——而每家公司都声称自己领先：推理（◐）、长程行动（◐）、价格（◐）。值得读两遍的不是基准，而是方向：每一套大系统如今都是智能体，而非应答者。问题不再是 AI 知道什么；而是 AI 做什么。Anthropic 自称的业绩——Claude 现承担公司约 26% 的研发任务，即 AI 在设计下一代 AI ◐——是这领域最骄傲的里程碑，还是它最重大的未知数，取决于你问谁。

The autumn release was a concert, not a solo. On September 3 the frontier moved on four fronts at once — and each company claims its model leads: reasoning (◐), long-horizon action (◐), price (◐). The detail that deserves a second read is not the benchmarks but the direction: every major system is now an agent, not a respondent. The question is no longer what AI knows; it is what AI does. Anthropic's own reporting — that Claude now performs roughly 26% of the company's R&D tasks, i.e. AI designing the next AI ◐ — is either the field's proudest milestone or its most significant unknown, depending on who is asked.

治理问题穿着丑闻的外衣到场。有人提议为前沿实验室设立一个仿 FINRA 的行业标准机构 ✓，反对者——包括政府内部的声音——斥之为「卡特尔」 ✓。这场碰撞真实而富含思想：一个预防性的机构，是对市场的矫正，还是对市场的俘获？这一年目前的答案是：监管这个词本身已经党派化——对一个影响如此深重的技术而言，这或许是最坏的可能结果，因为它保证让嗓门最大而非头脑最清的人来定规则。特朗普的「AI Force」与一位 AI 沙皇进一步把美国的姿态集中化 ✓：国家要的是能力，不是克制。

The governance question arrived dressed as a scandal. A FINRA-style standards body was proposed for the frontier labs ✓, and its opponents — including voices inside the administration — attacked it as a "cartel" ✓. The collision is genuine and philosophically rich: is a precautionary institution a correction of the market, or a capture of it? The year's answer so far is that regulation itself has become a partisan word, which is perhaps the worst possible outcome for a technology this consequential — it guarantees the loudest, not the wisest, voices set the rules. Trump's "AI Force" and an AI czar further centralize the American posture ✓: the state wants the capability, not the restraint.

而机器去找它的第一张支票。Anthropic 着手推进 11 月上市，据报营收超过一千亿美元 ◐——这一估值事件若成立，将让「人类史上最值钱的公司」从比喻沦为陈述句。

And the machine looked for its first checkbook. Anthropic filed toward a November IPO built on reported revenues exceeding $100 billion ◐ — a valuation event that, if it holds, would make the clause "the most valuable company in the history of the planet" a routine statement rather than a metaphor.

【我注】My Comment.

2026 年 AI 叙事从产品故事变为文明故事，恰好发生在它与战争交汇之处——Maven 审查、米纳布学校、那台认不出学校、因为学校不是一个「模式」的机器 ○。我们整年都在教机器识别规律性。而伦理生活整个都在不规则之中：那栋同时也是学校的建筑，那个同时也是威胁的手势，那个同时也是节日的下午。前沿模型令人惊叹。它们认不出的那个模式，正是例外。而例外——明年的战争、下一场选举——一直是人性寄居之所。

The AI story of 2026 stops being a product story and becomes a civilizational story at exactly the point where it and the war merge — the Maven review, the Minab school, the machine that couldn't see a school because a school is not a pattern ○. We have spent the year teaching machines to recognize regularities. But the whole of the ethical life is in the irregularities: the building that is also a school, the gesture that is also a threat, the afternoon that is also a holiday. The frontier models are astonishing. The pattern they cannot recognize is the exception. And the exception — next year, in the next war, in the next election — is where our humanity has always lived.

前瞻：接下来的约一百天PART IV — LOOKING FORWARD: THE NEXT ~100 DAYS

秋分是一年的龙骨；接下来的约一百天决定船身怎样破浪。

The equinox acts as the year's keel; the next hundred days determine how the hull rides.

  • 11 月 3 日——中期选举。November 3 — the midterms.

    Cook Political Report 当前预测：众议院倾向共和党，参议院 53–47 共和党领先 ✓。五千美元小费税减免的承诺为竞选加温 ✓；达拉斯大会定下策略，包括那句将被研究多年的短语 ✓。总统那句「拼命作弊」的鼓励 ✓，此刻必须以文化对待征兆的方式去衡量：不是看它证明了什么，而是看它练习了什么。如果九月时模式已如此清晰，那么差距不需要很小——它需要可核验，而今年最典型的反讽是：恰是核验的机制正被侵蚀。

    Cook Political Report currently projects: House leans Republican, Senate 53–47 Republican ✓. The $5,000-tip-credit pledge animates the campaign ✓; the Dallas convention set the strategy, including the phrase that will be studied for years ✓. A presidential encouragement to "cheat like hell" ✓ must now be weighed the way cultures weigh omens: not for what it proves, but for what it practices. If the pattern is this clear in September, the margin does not need to be small — it needs to be verifiable, and the year's signature irony is that the machinery to verify is precisely what is being eroded.

  • 11 月（暂定）——Anthropic 上市。November (tentative) — the Anthropic IPO.

    一个以千亿美元营收为前提的估值事件 ◐。若成立，「AI 资本开支」就不再是科幻，而成为中产养老金账户里的一个科目。市场的下注不再是某个产品，而是一个智能体——把智能当作一种自行续费的水电煤气。

    A valuation event predicated on $100B+ revenue ◐. If it holds, the phrase "AI capex" stops being science fiction and becomes a line item in the retirement accounts of the middle class. The market's bet is no longer on a product; it is on an agent — on intelligence as a utility that renews its own subscription.

  • 战争的冬天。The winter of the war.

死亡，被叙述。一场让机器向学校瞄准的战争、一部让受害者书写自己遭难的回忆录、一位教国民如何走向棺材的医生——2026 是死亡要求由其参与者、而非旁观者书写的一年。

Death, narrated. A war that lets a machine aim at a school, a memoir that lets a victim author her abuse, a doctor who teaches his countrymen how to walk to a coffin — 2026 is the year death demanded to be written by its participants, not its spectators.

收束CLOSING OBSERVATIONS

从秋分龙骨读到的一年最深的一课，是三千年一句中文写下的：日中则昃，月盈则食。2026 是若干个「盈」按时刻、同时地开始转「昃」的一年：廉价能源的帝国、廉价证据的帝国、耐心助手的帝国、前沿无可置疑的帝国。衰落不是崩塌，充盈不是胜利。周易不为偏转赋道德——它只让偏转可读。在充满裂缝的一年里，「可读」正是宇宙给出最仁慈的东西。

The year's deepest teaching, read from its autumn keel, is a Chinese sentence written about three millennia ago: 日中则昃，月盈则食 — the sun declines at its zenith; the moon wanes at its full. 2026 was a year in which several fullnesses began to decline on schedule and at once: the empire of easy energy, the empire of easy evidence, the empire of the patient assistant, the empire of the unquestioned frontier. Decline is not collapse; waxing is not victory. The I Ching does not moralize the turning — it only makes it legible. That legibility, in a year of fractures, was the kindest thing the cosmos offered.

而这个秋分，本季的阅读用各自的调门讲着同一件事——人类在裂缝中的位置，是留在例外一侧：与那场不属于模式的欢聚同在，与不叙述的美同在，与可能是无辜的英雄同在，与夺回笔杆的受害者同在，与那场日复一日练习、终结于善终的步行同在。光正在离开北半球。那是几何。它留在地上的一切——那一部分，是我们的。

And on this equinox the reading of the season tells, in its own keys, the same thing — that the human position in the split is to remain in the exception: with the party that isn't a pattern, the beauty that doesn't narrate, the hero who might be innocent, the victim who reclaims the pen, and the walk, practiced daily, that ends in a good death. The light is leaving the north. That is geometry. What it leaves behind on the ground — that part is ours.
