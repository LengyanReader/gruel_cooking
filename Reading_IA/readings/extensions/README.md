# extensions/ 延伸地图

Where reading becomes **hunting**. Every finished book should drop at least one new lead; the moment (Part 2) is the signal for which leads to promote.

阅读转为**狩猎**之处。每读完一本书至少要落下一个新线索；「当下的形状」（第二部分）正是你决定升级哪些线索的信号。

## Files 文件

- `leads.md` — the open field of leads, each one a question- or book-shaped idea with a one-line reason.
- `reading_paths.md` — sketched multi-book paths (e.g. "认识论的谦卑" path: A → B → C, with what each step unlocks).
- `README.md` — this file: the method.

## Method 方法

1. **Capture 捕获** — whenever a book/author/word makes you want something else, write one line into `leads.md` immediately.
2. **Score 打分** — keep three tags on every lead: `anticipation`（期待度 1–3）, `relevance-to-moment`（与当下相关 1–3）, `cost`（成本 1–3）.
3. **Prune 修剪** — every month, kill leads that score low on both (anticipation and relevance). A dead lead is a freed mind.
4. **Promote 升级** — a lead becomes a category suggestion or a next reading when it survives two months or is pulled by a `moment/` dossier.
5. **Mind-opening beats completeness 开阔胜过完备** — the map is allowed to be unfinished; its job is to make adjacency visible.

## Lead entry 条目格式

```markdown
- [ ] **lead**: 《书名或问题》 *(author / field)*
  - because 理由: one line
  - tags: anticipation=2 · moment=3 · cost=1
  - opened by 从哪本书来: [[library/slug]] · date
  - next step 下一步: chapter 篇目 / article 文章 / person 人
```
