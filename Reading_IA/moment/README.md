# Part 2 · The Shape of the Moment 当下的形状

> **目的**：把一段时期内的文化运动与思潮演化捻成线索——从你提供的材料出发，先向内核实（深度），再向外生长（广度），最后合成「此刻的形状」。
> Tracking cultural movements and thought trends across time windows: verify inward (depth), grow outward (breadth), then synthesise the shape of now.

---

## Windows 时间窗

| Window 窗口 | File 文件 | Purpose 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 7 天 | `series/week-YYYY-MM-DD.md` | what moved this week — signals, spikes, echo 本周动静 |
| 14 天 | `series/fortnight-YYYY-MM-DD.md` | consolidation of 2 weeks, dropped/rising themes 两周统合 |
| 1 个月 | `series/month-YYYY-MM.md` | structural shifts, new fronts 月度结构变化 |
| 1 年 | `series/year-YYYY.md` | the arc of the year, what a decade learned 年终脉络 |

Series are **cumulative**: each window synthesises under it. `themes/` holds the persistent dossiers that outlive any window.

序列是**累积的**：每个窗口统合其下所有时间。`themes/` 存放跨越窗口的持久档案。

---

## Method 方法（从材料出发 → 广度与深度）

### Step 0 · Capture 收料
Place your provided materials (links, quotes, screenshots, scraps) into a dated brief or this week's window file first. Source matters: record where each item came from.

先把你提供的材料（链接、引文、截图、碎片）放进带日期的简报或本周窗口。来源优先：记下每件材料的出处。

### Step 1 · Verify inward · 向内核实（深度 depth）
- **Trace to origin** 溯源：who said it first, who said it loudest, who misquoted it.
- **Separate signal from spike** 区分信号与噪音：is this a repeatable shift or a one-off spike?
- **Source confidence** 来源可信度：mark every claim `verified / company-claim / unconfirmed` exactly as in `direction_refs/mostik-latent-bridge-brief.md`. (给定来源的确切级别)
- **Genealogy of the idea** 观念谱系：which older idea is this a mutation of? Connect to `readings/`.

### Step 2 · Grow outward · 向外生长（广度 breadth）
- **Contrasts** 对照：who opposes, who staffs the opposite camp, what the counter-narrative is.
- **Adjacencies** 邻接：which neighbouring fields are being dragged in (economics ↔ science ↔ culture)?
- **Geography** 地理：is the trend localised or planetary?
- **Institutions** 机构：which labs / presses / think-tanks / platforms are the carriers?

### Step 3 · Synthesise 合成
Write the **shape**: one-page theses per dossier — what is arriving, what is receding, what is being reversed, what the friction points are. End every synthesis with **2–3 consequential questions** (问题即延伸线索).

### Step 4 · Wire back 回连
Link the dossier into `readings/extensions/leads.md` and `readings/relations/moment_ties.md`, so the trend feeds the reading list and vice-versa.

## Card schemes 格式

### Brief 简报 — `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`
Dated notes on a breaking topic, written as it develops (see the template; it carries a source-confidence legend). One topic per file.

### Window file — `series/`
- **Signals moved** 出现的信号（what surfaced, with provenance 出处）
- **Rising / falling / reversing** 上升 / 退场 / 反转
- **Friction points** 摩擦点（两股思潮相撞处）
- **Questions carried forward** 结转问题

### Dossier — `themes/<theme>.md`
Persistent. Updated whenever the theme moves. See template.

## Reliability legend 可信度图例

| Mark 标记 | Meaning 含义 |
|---|---|
| `✓ verified` | independently confirmed, origin located 已独立核实 |
| `◐ company-claim` | primary source reports its own result 主体自述 |
| `○ unconfirmed` | press / echo only, not yet checked 仅见转述 |
| `✗ disputed` | conflicting accounts 各方说法不一 |
