# briefs/ 研究简报

Dated research briefs on breaking topics. Written **while the topic is hot**, then drained upward into `themes/` dossiers as it matures.

围绕热点话题的带日期研究简报。**趁热撰写**，成熟后上提进入 `themes/` 持久档案。

```markdown

- …

## What is actually known 已知与未知
### Confirmed 已核实 ✓
### Owner-reported 主体自述 ◐
```
