# heaven_climate — Astronomy & Climate Research

Starting from recent and leading topics, tracing the research threads of celestial observation, celestial appearance, and climate.

## Subdirectories

- `celestial_appearance/` — Observable sky phenomena: planetary motion, eclipses, meteors, aurorae, etc.
- `climate_dynamics/` — Climate systems: from short-term weather to long-term climate, paleoclimate evidence
- `history_of_ideas/` — Intellectual history: key papers, key figures, school genealogies
- `current_research/` — Current frontiers: latest research developments and controversies

## Working Method

1. Choose a specific topic (e.g., "2024 Arctic sea ice minimum" or "the discovery history of Kepler's laws of planetary motion")
2. Trace: who proposed it first? What were the key turning points? What is the current debate?
3. Find traceable primary sources (papers, observation records, original texts)
4. Interpret using first principles from `/core`
5. Record the intellectual genealogy: where did this idea come from? Who did it influence?
