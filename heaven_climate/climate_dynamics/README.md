# climate_dynamics — Climate Systems

From weather to climate, from modern era to paleoclimate.

## Topic Categories

### Fundamental Mechanisms
- Radiative balance and energy budget
- Physical mechanism of the greenhouse effect
- Atmospheric circulation and ocean currents

### Climate Cycles
- Milankovitch cycles (orbitally driven)
- El Nino-Southern Oscillation (ENSO)
- Solar activity cycles

### Paleoclimate
- Ice core records (historical relationship between CO2 and temperature)
- Deep-sea sediments
- Dendrochronology (tree-ring dating)

### Contemporary Issues
- Attribution analysis of global warming
- Extreme weather events and climate change
- Predictive capability and limitations of climate models

## Record Format

Each topic includes:
- Phenomenon description
- Physical mechanisms
- Key data sources
- Points of controversy
- Related first principles (link to `/core`)
