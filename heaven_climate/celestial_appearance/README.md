# celestial_appearance — Observable Sky Phenomena

Celestial phenomena observable by naked eye and instruments.

## Topic Categories

### Planets and Moons
- Planetary retrograde motion (observational evidence for geocentric vs heliocentric models)
- Lunar phases and tides
- Jupiter's Galilean moons

### Stars and Galaxies
- Stellar brightness variations (Cepheid variables and distance measurement)
- Observational inference of Milky Way structure
- Observational evidence for dark matter

### Celestial Events
- Solar and lunar eclipses (prediction accuracy and historical records)
- Meteor showers and comets
- Aurorae and solar activity

### Cosmological Observations
- Cosmic microwave background radiation
- Supernovae and the accelerating expansion of the universe
- Gravitational wave astronomy

## Record Format

Each topic includes:
- Phenomenon description
- Observation history (from earliest records to latest data)
- Key figures and papers
- Related first principles (link to `/core`)
