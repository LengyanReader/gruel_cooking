# other_traditions — Other Cultural Economic Thought

Economic thought from non-Western, non-Chinese traditions.

## Traditions to Cover

### Islamic Economics (伊斯兰经济学)
- Zakat (天课) — wealth redistribution mechanism
- Riba (利息) prohibition and its implications for finance
- Islamic banking and finance principles
- Ibn Khaldun — *Muqaddimah*, cyclical theory of civilizations and economics

### Indian Economics (印度经济思想)
- Arthashastra (利论) by Kautilya — statecraft and economic management
- Caste system and economic specialization
- Ancient trade networks (Silk Road, Indian Ocean trade)
- Gandhi's village economics and trusteeship concept

### African Economic Thought
- Ubuntu economics — communal ownership and mutual aid
- Pre-colonial trade networks (Trans-Saharan, East African coast)
- colonialism's lasting economic structures
- Post-colonial economic thought (Nyerere's Ujamaa, Nkrumah's Pan-Africanism)

### Indigenous and Other Traditions
- Gift economies and potlatch
- Commons-based resource management (Elinor Ostrom's work connects here)
- Buddhist economics — sufficiency, middle way, non-attachment

## Record Format

Each tradition includes:
- Historical and cultural context
- Core economic principles and key thinkers
- Primary sources
- Comparison with Western and Chinese traditions
- Connection to first principles in `/core`
