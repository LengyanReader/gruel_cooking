# cases — Specific Historical Cases

Concrete historical cases that illustrate economic principles across cultures.

## Case Categories

### Trade and Exchange
- The Silk Road as a multi-cultural economic system
- The Dutch East India Company (VOC) — early multinational corporation
- Qing dynasty Canton System (广州十三行) — managed trade
- The opium trade — economics of addiction and empire

### Financial Systems
- The South Sea Bubble (1720) — speculative mania
- Song dynasty jiaozi (交子) — first paper money
- The 2008 Global Financial Crisis — systemic risk across traditions
- Historical hyperinflations (Weimar, Zimbabwe, Venezuela)

### Resource Management
- The Tragedy of the Commons vs Ostrom's commons governance
- The Dust Bowl — ecological and economic feedback
- China's Grand Canal — infrastructure and economic integration
- Whaling industry collapse — open-access resource depletion

### Institutional Innovation
- The Hanseatic League — trade network as governance
- Japan's Meiji Restoration — rapid economic modernization
- China's Special Economic Zones (经济特区) — experiment-based reform
- Mondragon Corporation — cooperative economics at scale

## Record Format

Each case includes:
- Time period and geographic context
- Key actors and institutions
- Economic principles illustrated
- Cross-cultural comparisons (what would a Western, Chinese, or other perspective say?)
- Connection to first principles in `/core`
- Sources and further reading
