# chinese — Chinese Economic Thought (中国经济思想)

Economic thought from Chinese civilization, spanning ancient statecraft to modern reforms.

## Topic Categories

### Ancient and Imperial Economics
- Guan Zhong (管子) — statecraft, salt and iron monopolies, "light and heavy" (轻重) theory
- Confucian economic thought — agrarian hierarchy, frugality, ritual economy
- Legalist economics (法家) — state control, agricultural production incentives
- Wang Anshi (王安石) — Song dynasty reforms, government intervention

### Fiscal Systems and Statecraft
- Land tax systems across dynasties (初税亩, 两税法, 一条鞭法, 摊丁入亩)
- Salt and iron monopolies (盐铁论)
- Tribute system and long-distance trade
- Granary systems (常平仓, 义仓, 社仓)

### Chinese Commercial Thought
- Guanxi (关系) economics — relationship-based transactions
- Shan-shang (商商) — merchant ethics and practices
- Shanxi merchants (晋商) and Huizhou merchants (徽商) — business networks
- The "high-level equilibrium trap" (高水平均衡陷阱) debate

### Modern Chinese Economic Thought
- Sun Yat-sen — *Fundamentals of National Reconstruction* (建国方略)
- Mao-era planned economy and Mao Zedong Thought on economics
- Deng Xiaoping's Reform and Opening (改革开放)
- "Socialist market economy" (社会主义市场经济) theory

## Record Format

Each topic includes:
- Historical context and key figures
- Core ideas and their practical applications
- Primary sources (classical texts, policy documents)
- Comparison with Western and other traditions
- Connection to first principles in `/core`
