# metathinking — Metathinking

Questioning the foundations of first principles themselves.

## Core Questions

- **Why are these principles "basic"?** Is it because we haven't found deeper explanations, or are they truly irreducible?
- **Where are the boundaries?** Quantum mechanics fails at macro scales, Newtonian mechanics fails near light speed — every principle has a range of applicability.
- **What is the relationship between principles?** Conservation of energy, least action, entropy increase — are they independent or equivalent?
- **Limits of human cognition**: We tend to seek simple laws, but does the universe actually follow simple laws? Or do we only see what we can understand?
