Dr. Ruben Laukkonen is a neuroscientist, philosopher, and contemplative researcher based at the University of Oxford (Department of Psychiatry, Linacre College), where he directs the Flourishing Intelligence Program (FLIP) and co-directs the Centre for Eudaimonia and Human Flourishing. His research bridges computational neuroscience, contemplative wisdom traditions, and artificial intelligence.

**Primary Research Fields**

* **Predictive Processing & Active Inference:** Modeling brain dynamics, perception, and self-referential awareness through hierarchical predictive processing, precision weighting, and Bayesian active inference.
* **Contemplative Science & Consciousness:** Empirically mapping advanced meditative states, Minimal Phenomenal Experiences (MPEs), non-dual awareness, and extreme states like *nirodha samāpatti* (complete cessation of conscious experience).
* **Phenomenology of Insight & Metacognition:** Investigating the cognitive mechanisms and "Eureka!" heuristics behind sudden breakthroughs, creative problem-solving, and intuitive decision-making.
* **Contemplative AI & Alignment:** Translating biological brain dynamics, predictive processing, and wisdom principles to build safer, self-aware, and human-flourishing artificial intelligence.

**Intellectual & Interdisciplinary Circles**

* **Active Inference & Computational Neurophilosophy:** Collaborating with theoretical neuroscientists and computational modelers (such as Shamil Chandaria) to formulate unified active inference models of consciousness and pure awareness.
* **Contemplative Scholars & Virtuoso Meditators:** Partnering with advanced meditation practitioners (such as Delson Armstrong) and contemplative scholars (such as mathematician/Vipassanā teacher Henk Barendregt) to formalize meditative phenomenology.
* **Oxford Eudaimonia & Human Flourishing Network:** Working within Oxford's psychiatric and eudaimonic research centers alongside interdisciplinary scholars exploring human well-being, neuroplasticity, and mental fitness.
* **AI Safety & Ethical Tech Innovation:** Engaging with AI safety researchers, neurotechnologists, and ethics groups to integrate humanistic and contemplative values into emerging tech architectures.
