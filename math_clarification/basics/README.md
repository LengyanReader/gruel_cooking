# basics — Basic Concepts (基础数学概念)

The ingredients: mathematical ideas the rest of the cookbook leans on, each unpacked in one small file. The first three entries form the spine — read them in order; the rest stand alone.

## Entries

- [Numbers](numbers.md) — what lives on the number line: counting → integers → fractions → irrationals, and why $\pi$ and $e$ keep appearing in every field.
- [Exponents & logarithms](exponents_and_logs.md) — repeated multiplication and its undo-button; why logs turn huge ranges (sound, earthquakes, acidity, star brightness) into readable scales.
- [Growth](growth.md) — linear vs exponential vs decay; doubling time, half-life, and why 42 paper folds reach the Moon.
- [Probability & expectation](probability.md) — fractions of possibility; independence, conditional probability and Bayes in plain words; what "average over many runs" means.
- [Infinity & limits](infinity.md) — why $0.999\ldots = 1$, why Zeno's arrow still arrives, and why some infinities are bigger than others.
- [Proofs](proofs.md) — direct, contradiction, induction, counterexample: how mathematics makes "must be so" airtight, and why examples are never enough.

## On the stove (planned)

## House Style

Every entry follows the anatomy in [../README.md](../README.md): idea in a line → precise statement (精确表述) → intuition (譬喻) → premises & conditions (前提·假设·成立条件) → a little math → directions & history (解题方向·历史的努力) → where it shows up → what people get wrong → **sources (参考文献)**. Entries with machine-checkable claims carry a **Machine-checked** line linking to [../lean](../lean/README.md). Sources follow the source rules in the folder README: first-hand where possible, reliability note, verified link.
